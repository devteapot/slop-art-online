# Public-feed scoping (the player)

docs/FEED_SCOPING.md. `scripts/feed_scope.py --run player-feed-…` on a `--seed cullet` run with the player joined (Cullet's fires burn fuel; the shipped script has none, so `inspect` is not listed there). It takes the world to stage 2, then the player, through `human_act`, feeds a hearth it put up (`place_structure`, `grant_items` peat, `grant_know_how` fire) and looks it over with `inspect`; `my_things` shows the hearth out then lit with no fuel field, `my_fuel_seen` the hours found and `my_experience` the line. A hearth out of sight and an absent id give the same `my_act_outcomes` row (`unseen`). A mind beside the hearth is sent it as `"state": "burning"` with no hours, and after its own `inspect` (`mind_act`) as `"fuel": "about N hours … when you looked it over"`, read from its pending `deliberation` scene. Evidence: `feed-player.json`.
