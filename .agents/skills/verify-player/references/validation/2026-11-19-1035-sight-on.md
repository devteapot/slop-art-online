# Cover sight and gaze switched on in Cullet (sight-on)

Branch `sight-on`, commit `47cedac6` on main `2a5cdfdf`, plus the record and two fixture fixes committed after it. All scenes ran on a private SpacetimeDB 2.14.1 container on the Spark (`slop-sighton-stdb`, `127.0.0.1:3391`, started 2026-11-19 11:14:47 CET), one scene at a time, outside `battle.lock` with the Spark coordinator's approval. No model calls. Each launcher stayed alive for its whole scene in one supervising shell, and every `sight_on.py` assertion first checked that the world was unpaused and its clock advancing.

**Cullet, as shipped.** `cover_law` reads (on, 1.6, brush on, 0.6, 2.0) under the player's identity, with no `world_law` rows. `my_senses_v_2` has the player's row at Middle, and the legacy field is directional. The `cover_fixture` positions gave these results: the trunk resident at (785.5, 457.5) was hidden from (781.5, 457.5); the control 4 m the other way on open ground was seen; the overlook (849.5→842.5, 613.5) was clear; the cliff and brush pairs were hidden. Both hedges hid the resident: `mid_row_plot_toft_3_hedge` from (522.5→528.8, 697.5) and `high_row_plot_garden_3_hedge` from (519.0→525.4, 668.5). Each pair was seen with `cover_brush_on` off and hidden again with it back on. Gaze: Middle, Far and Near applied with ceilings 49.0, 63.4 and 29.8 and fields 48 m ±70°, 62.4 m ±35° and 28.8 m ±110°. At 55.2 m, a resident Middle did not see was seen at Far.

**Island with the play-test overlay.** The overlay is the play-test 8 scene's laws, recovered from `.local/bystanders-1/play8/installed_source.rhai` in git: Cullet's light, sight, hearing and names, with lamps and fires. To it the run added `laws()` with `turn_rate` 30 and `look_walk_deg` 180 (play-test 9) and `cover_sight_on`, `gaze_on` and `fov_on` true, with brush as shipped. The fixture's trunk, (481.5→485.5, 8.5), was hidden. The control 4 m away was seen, the overlook (783.5→790.5, 704.5) clear and the cliff hidden. Gaze changed the field, and Far saw a resident beyond Middle's 48 m. The fresh world had its `cover_ground` bands. The doctor's one failure ("installed script is the shipped one") is expected, because the overlay was installed after creation.

**Fixture fixes found on the way.**
- The hedge lines first ran along y = 667.0, a tile edge whose row holds only 0.6 of the hedge. That result depended on direction, so the lines now run along tile centres.
- `stats.ticks` is written only every few seconds, so the liveness check reads the world clock.
- `cover.py` failed "brush, cover off" because Cullet is now directional and its viewer never turns. It now switches `fov_on` and `gaze_on` off with the cover laws.
