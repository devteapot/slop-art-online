## 2026-11-12 fix-sleep-talk: what wakes a player's sleeping character (Spark)

Branch `fix-sleep-talk` (`c7e31a2e`), the trial service, the infant fixture. `scripts/sleep.py` (new) on `player-talk4`: 10/10; also `player-sleep1` and `player-talk3`.

- The player's character sleeps through its own `human_act` (a run of sleeps).
- A resident's quiet line 3 m away does not wake it and is not in `my_experience`.
- The resident calling its name wakes it ("You woke up: A stranger calls out to you from about 3 m: “Sleeper!”"), and the quiet line follows as `heard_asleep`.
- Someone else's baby crying 3 m away wakes it ("You woke up: A baby is crying and looks warm, …").
- A fight 12–13 m away does not wake it; at 3–4 m it does ("You woke up: the noise of a fight, east.").
- Admin calls set the scene only: `place_at`, `set_behavior`, `mind_say`.
- Not covered: a parent's own baby waking a player, since a joined character has no children; verify-core `sleeper` covers a parent.
