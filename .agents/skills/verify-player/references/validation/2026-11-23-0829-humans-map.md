# The player map from the living (`humans-map`): the player surface

Validated on the dev-box on branch `humans-map` from `origin/main` `c5056f2e5`. Servers `hm-deta` (det_compare) and `hm-ka` (private, `127.0.0.1:3404`); `performance` governor. No paid model calls.

**The change** (authority):
- `combat::human()` rebuilds its map of living characters people control through a new btree index on `character.alive` (`.alive().filter(true)` with the same filter), instead of walking the whole `character` table, the dead included;
- the invalidation is unchanged;
- a detail span, `combat.humans` (profiling at L2 only), times each rebuild;
- the bindings were regenerated (`character_type.rs` gains the index column).

Adding an index is a safe automatic migration (https://spacetimedb.com/docs/databases/automatic-migrations/, "Adding indexes"). The player reducers, views and tables are unchanged, so the map has no drift.

## Proof

- **det_compare** (16 players, 200 fighters, 1,800 ticks, 113 tables): identical to main. The negative control (`witness_fold_ms=0`) differs in all 1,800 ticks.
- **The known answer** (`.local/hm/known.py`, a scratch default world each; the rebuild's `combat.humans` span, 40 rebuilds per point):

  | Dead characters | Main's walk (span added for this measure), median µs | The index, median µs |
  |---:|---:|---:|
  | 0 (308 rows) | 139 | 134 |
  | 2,500 | 864 | 195 |
  | 5,000 | 1,572 | 188 |
  | 10,000 | 2,927 | 180 |

  Main's rebuild grows about 0.28 µs per character row; the index's stays flat.
- **verify-player `combat.py` on a scratch island world (`hm-1`):**
  - `--scene all --fresh`: 59 of 59;
  - `--scene death --fresh` four more times: 4/4 each (every rejoin a new character).

Evidence: `.local/hm/` (`det-off/`, `det-negative/`, `known-main.json`, `known-fix.json`, `known.py`) and `.local/living/verify/hm-1/`, archived to `~/archive/worktree-evidence-20261123/humans-map.tar.zst`.
