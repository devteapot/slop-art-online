# The wolves scene's staging (`wolves-scene`): the player surface

Validated on the dev-box on branch `wolves-scene` from `origin/main`. Server `ei-stdb` (private, `127.0.0.1:3384`); `performance` governor. No paid model calls. Test-only: `scripts/combat.py` `scene_wolves` and [features/combat.md](../../features/combat.md) (Gotchas). The scene list, reducers and views are unchanged, so this skill's map has no drift.

## The failure

The scene gate's `gate-1122060233-359c46-a-player` failed "bold wolves attack a healthy adult player": no blow in 20 s, with the player 5 m east of wolf 307. The scene's failures on a reused world on 2026-11-12 ([2026-11-12-0745-fix-pursuit-speed.md](2026-11-12-0745-fix-pursuit-speed.md)) were very likely the same.

## The cause

A wolf attacks only a person it sees within 10 m. The probe (`.local/wolf/scene_probe.py`) repeats the scene's own staging and logs the wolf's activity, mind state, vitals and distance every 0.4 s. It found two races:

1. **Cover.** The spot 5 m east was hidden from the wolf by brush or rock, by day. The player's own view held no wolf either, since sight is the same both ways. The wolf looked again, at 5–7 m, chose to wander and left.
2. **A wolf on the move.** The scene placed the player beside the wolf's row, which is a segment start. A wolf already wandering away was soon more than 10 m off, and wandered on.

**What the probe showed:**

| World | Main | Original staging, traced | Failures and their cause |
|---|---|---|---|
| `wolf-p1` | `85a7478c6` (pre-fair-ties) | 15 rounds | 2: both cover (a replay of each spot failed 3 of 3, and 1 of 3) |
| `wolf-p3` | `0877fe93a` | 30 rounds | 2: one cover (round 16), one a wolf on the move (round 12) |
| `wolf-p2` | `85a7478c6` | 17 rounds, with the cover fix only | 1: a wolf on the move |

## The fix

- The scene tries eight spots round the wolf's current position (`body_now`, from the segment) until the player's `seen_characters` holds the wolf.
- While no blow has come, it places the player again whenever the wolf is more than 7 m off.
- A new check records the placements: "the player stands where the first bold wolf sees it".
