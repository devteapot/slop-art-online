# pacing-wake: the spacing wake with a token limit, and the completion reservation

Branch `pacing-wake` (Spark), `ee4fc2a4` (docs/POOL_STUDY.md, "After the pacing wake"). `llm.rs` changed: a lane head held only by request spacing wakes at its deadline also when its profile has a token limit (`Queue::spacing_deadline`); pacing reserves a call's prompt estimate plus twice the p99 of its profile's recent completions for the purpose (at most the output cap; the whole cap until 100 replies), and a hard check keeps the worst case (every call in flight at its whole cap) under the provider's advertised token limit. Model config format and reply parsing did not change. The local tier ran on the private server `slop-pacingwake-20261121` (127.0.0.1:3397, SpacetimeDB 2.14.1), fake provider only, no paid calls.
