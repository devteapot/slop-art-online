# diag-acts-give: park the actor before placing it in verify-minds `acts`

Branch `diag-acts-give` from `origin/main` `6d58aa5f5`. dev-box (`x86_64`, CPU governor `performance`), private server `neon-acts-stdb` (SpacetimeDB 2.14.1, `127.0.0.1:3455`), private `CARGO_TARGET_DIR` under `.local/neon-diag/target`, builds under `flock -s ~/.local/state/slop-build/gate.lock` with `CARGO_BUILD_JOBS=8`, fake model only (no paid calls).

**Cause (staging, not rules or mind).** In `.agents/skills/verify-minds/scripts/verify_minds.py`, `basic_case` (`acts`, line 424) called `place_near(actor, other)` while Datha (`#1`) was still running her seeded person instinct graph (`living/seeds/instincts.json:300-314`, `seq: [think, wander, wait(3)]`). `motion::place` (`living/authority/src/motion.rs:1476-1503`) cancelled her prior movement, advancing the sequence to `wait(3)`. When running alone, setup finished within 3 s and `run.request` replaced the graph before `wander` resumed; under gate load, setup between `place_near` and `set_behavior` took 6–24 s, so Datha resumed `wander` at `3.37 m/s` and walked 15–56 m away from `MindsReceiver` (`#268`, at `(40.5, 60.5)`). All three historical sightings (2026-11-26 23:54 on `heed-player-2` + `6d58aa5f5`, 2026-11-26 17:22 on `main` `9719791b3`, and 2026-11-23 13:04/13:18 on `gate-say-path`) occurred in daylight (`hour 7.8–9.1`, `night: false`, `sight: 11.0 m`); `mind_act_checked` synchronously refused `gather`, `give`, and `offer` with `no #<id> in sight` (`living/authority/src/acts.rs:206` → `brain.rs:1214`), so `give` never started an activity and `wait` began 3–15 ms after `installed_ms`.

**Fix (test-only).** Call `run.admin('set_behavior', actor, json.dumps({'wait': 120}))` before `run.admin('place_near', actor, other)` in `verify_minds.py` (matching `dealings`, `answers`, `sight`, `fov`, `doing`, and `doing-renew`). Exact diff also saved in `.local/neon-diag/acts.diff`.
