## Found while building the case

- **A real defect, fixed:** a `silence` that arrives while the speaker's own turn is still in flight (`busy`) left the follow-up waiting for good. The turn loop's due test (`spawn_turns`) only counted a line from the other. It now also counts a pending missed line (`minds-rt-1405`: "next turn weighs it" logged, no turn).
- **Setup fixes, in the case:**
  - SQL `IN (...)` is unsupported (`OR` instead);
  - a body row's `x`/`y` is where its segment began (the case projects the position, as `common::pos` does);
  - F's own 2 s speaking gap is timed from the greeting's authority `at_ms`;
  - `walk_base` is 2.6 m/s, so F's second line is a call;
  - each greeting asks a question (an addressed remark that asks nothing may be let pass by chance).
