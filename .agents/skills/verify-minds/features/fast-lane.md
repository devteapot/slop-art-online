# The fast lane

docs/FAST_LANE.md and docs/FAST_LANE_FRAME.md; `living/mind/src/mind/fast.rs` (serving an issued `fast_ask` row; `fast_now`: own health and breath from `vitals`, the issue's `fast_sight` row), `prompts.rs` `fast_system` (by the world law `fast_frame`: `fast_system_v(1)` the first frame, `fast_balanced_in` the balanced one, shipped as 2), `fast_user` and `FastNow`, `main.rs` (the `fast_ask` and `fast_sight` subscriptions, each on its own), `rethink.json` `fast` (route, timeout, experiences).
