# Heed: a mind writes whom its character keeps track of (`heed_on`)

docs/ATTENTION_BUDGET.md ("Built"). Off by default until the owner's play-test.

**What it is.** Under the value-only law `heed_on`, the think system prompt (cached) gains the
reply's optional `"heed"` field and its grammar (`prompts::HEED_GRAMMAR`), and the user prompt a
"# Whom you keep track of" section rendered from the scene's `_heed` (capacity, by its own policy
or its instinct, each rule's `why`, the parser's notes, the last refusal); `_heed` itself never
reaches a model (`model_scene`). A think decision's `heed` goes to the `mind_heed` reducer; the
authority validates it with the shared parser (`living_rules::heed`) and keeps it in the private
`heed_policy` row, or keeps the policy before and records the refusal, which the next think prompt
shows. `"instinct"` deletes the row. No repair turn. With the law off the prompts are as before and
a reply's `heed` is ignored.

**Case** `heed` (`verify_minds.py --case heed`, about 2 minutes): one minded resident, the fake
model's think replies keyed by the request's reason; checks, in order: off (no field, no section,
no row), on (field and grammar, the section "by your instinct"), a valid policy (the row; the next
prompt "by your own policy" with its `why`), a refused policy (an unknown kind: the row keeps the
policy and says why; the next prompt shows the refusal), back to the instinct (no row; "by your
instinct"); `_heed` in no prompt. Unit tests: `the_heed_grammar_names_no_one`,
`the_heed_section_shows_capacity_rules_and_the_refusal`, `a_scenes_stage_mark_never_reaches_a_model`.

**Not covered here:** the selector's ranking and forced contact (verify-core `heed` scene and
`living_rules::heed` tests), a player in a mind's throng (level-of-detail.md), real models.
