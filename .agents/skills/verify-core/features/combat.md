# Combat and force

## Sub-features

- Threats produce percepts and trigger a threatened guard.
- A blow does its full damage and the one that exceeds health kills (no hurt or kill mode since 2026-11-09, docs/COMBAT.md "One damage rule"); a yield only stops the yielder striking back.
- A wolf refuses a healthy adult among others.
- Seize refuses an unyielded healthy person.
- Seize accepts a yielded healthy person (the rules weigh the target's yield for every skill whose `<skill>_combat()` says `dares`: attack, strike, seize).
- Seize transfers an exact quantity from a badly hurt person and informs witnesses.
- A blow can kill someone who yielded; the first blow after the yield is chronicled "struck X, though X had yielded" and the death is "killed by X, after yielding", with X as the victim knew the killer (`perceived_of`: a name only when known, else "a stranger" or "a wolf"); the chronicle keeps real names.
- Starter skills under graphs (`scenes.py skirmish`, docs/COMBAT.md): two sides of 10 (`spawn_skirmish`) use strike, bow shot, block, dodge and heal; uses are recorded from acceptance with source `graph`; arrows strike bodies on their path; no actor's accepted uses of a skill come closer than its `skill_card` cooldown; fighters show a band; deaths go through the death rule and leave no `fighter` or `readiness` rows.
- **Outcomes reach their maker** (`force_outcomes` law, docs/COMBAT.md "The outcome of one's own blow or shot"): whoever made the move, a mind or a person playing the character (the player's side is verify-player `combat.py --scene strike,bow`), is told how its strike, shot or bolt came out (`living/authority/src/act.rs:991` `report` and `:1034` `tell` for a force skill without a missile; `act.rs:1069` `missile_told`, called from `combat.rs:479-543` (`missiles`, `strike`) when a missile strikes, is dodged, hits a wall or nothing, or flies out of the shooter's sight). `scenes.py outcomes` on an island run: the archer's "struck …: hit for N", the striker's "hit for N", an arrow at an empty point told as coming to nothing, every such line in the world the maker's own (never the target's or a bystander's), and none told with the law off (`set_world_law`).
- **Another's health as perceived** (`scenes.py health_seen`, any seed, about 2 min; docs/PERCEPTION.md): two strangers fight and the scene skips each pending `deliberation` as a mind would, so fight reports (`act::fight_report`, a reason to think; AI people only) keep coming. Without an appraisal a report ends "Your health 61; they look scratched." (the band's words, no number); inside A's 20 s `appraise` reveal A's report ends "they have 70 of 100 health." while B's stays banded; after the reveal ends A's is banded again. A graph's `health_of` reads the same perception (`perception::health_seen`): the band's middle without an appraisal, exact during one (a threshold between the two tells which; XFAIL when they are under 4 points apart). Until 2026-11-11 reports read "theirs looks about 70%" and `health_of` read the target's `vitals`. The rules test `combat::tests::fight_reports_show_the_band_unless_appraised` covers the text.
- **A missile is told as a shot** (fix-missile-told; docs/PERCEPTION.md "A missile is told as a shot"): `scenes.py outcomes` runs `missiles_told` after `names_told`: an archer shoots a target 4.5 m off (within night sight, so at any hour) with a witness beside it, then looses at a point where a far target stands 19 m off (past day sight, within the bow's 22 m). The target is told "A stranger shot you with an arrow: hit for N" (subject the archer), the witness "A stranger shot a stranger with an arrow", the far target "An arrow struck you from somewhere you could not see: hit for N" (subject 0); no line about the arrows says "attacked" or a name. `living/authority/src/act.rs:2167` (`damage`, its `missile` argument), `perceive.rs:105` (`witnessed_shot`), `perception.rs` `sees_creature`; the missile's name is the script's `called` (`Scripts::missile_called`; cargo `a_missile_is_called_what_its_skill_says`).
- **A dodge's outcome and a cooldown's refusals, told truthfully** (fix-dodge-told; docs/COMBAT.md "A dodge over before the blow came"): `scenes.py dodge_told`. A stands two tiles short of a wall, B (a spear) beside it; A's graph dodges toward the wall on `doing` attack "winding up" at it, which has room for half the dash: the dash is over, still in reach, before B's heavy blow releases and lands, and A is told "You failed to dodge: it was over before the blow came" once, its `skill_use` outcome "made way: it was over before the blow came" (`living/authority/src/act.rs` `dodge_too_early`, called from `damage`). Then a routine `{"if": {"chance": 1}, "then": {"do": "dodge"}}` (restarted the tick after each dodge) is refused while not ready: "dodge is not ready yet" is told at most once per cooldown (`act.rs` `refused` and `cooldown_news`), while its `routine_stat` counts every refusal.
- **Told lines name others as perceived** (fix-kill-name; docs/PERCEPTION.md "Told lines name others as the teller perceives them"): `scenes.py outcomes` ends with `names_told`: four strangers give, offer and trade, demand, teach, shoot and strike until one dies, a witness by; the killer is told "killed a stranger", the witness "A stranger was killed by a stranger", and no told line or own `skill_use` outcome names someone the teller was not introduced to (`acquaintance`).
- **Committed moves** (`scenes.py committed`, default seed, about 30 s; docs/COMBAT.md "Committed moves"; `living/authority/src/combat.rs` `committed`, `held_by`, `gate`, `rooted_now`; `brain.rs` `leaf` and `after`; the `committed` trait in `<skill>_combat()`): a started committed move ends early only by something physical. On the map's wall geometry (`wall_geometry`) a routine's dodge toward a wall is cut at it (0.78 m in 166 ms against 2.6 m in 516 ms the open way); W killed (admin `kill`, over HTTP so it lands inside the 650 ms windup) while its attack winds up: the attack never releases (`released_ms` 0) and its target takes no blow. A routine that dodges while it stands (`{"moving": false}`, ended by the dash itself) runs its course; with an overlay installed (`install_script.py`'s `compose_laws`, `fn dodge_combat() { let s = base_dodge_combat(); s.committed = false; s }`) the same dodge is broken off and a jump still runs on; the shipped script is reinstalled in a `finally`. With the field on, `doing_seen`'s front check ([doing](doing.md)). Staging (fix-committed-staging, 2026-11-12): every check spawns its own characters; the scene first moves every other body off its spots (an earlier run's actors and wandering residents, 60 m away), places each actor and reads it back standing there with full breath, installs the shipped script when the world runs another, and moves its actors off the spots at the end; the kill is timed off W's `fighter` row (polled over HTTP, `Scene.http_rows`); the standing checks run in a fight (a fresh Z threatens P, `mind_state.fast_until`), since outside one a graph is looked at about once a second and a 0.5 s dodge is usually over before its condition is read again (the flake: 4 of 5 reruns of one world failed before, `core-stage-084914` runs 1-5; 5 of 5 and 2 fresh worlds pass after). Cargo: `living-rules` `script::tests` (the committed set; `a_world_switches_committed_per_skill_in_its_own_laws`), `living-authority` `combat::committed_tests` (both). No rule staggers on a hit, so there is no stagger check.
- **Stable codes:** `scripts/codes.py RUN` on a fresh `verify-core-*` world installs scripts that add and remove a combat and a shown skill and checks that `skill_card`/`act_card` codes never change or get reused (the private `skill_code` table, `living/authority/src/codes.rs`). Passed 10/10 on 2026-11-09 (`verify-codes-1`).
- The authority's own rules tests cover the geometry (`living_rules::combat`: sweeps, guard arcs, bands, codes), the starter skills' Rhai requirements and numbers (`script::tests::starter_combat_skills_follow_the_rules`), that the script's shapes classify the existing skills exactly as the name lists they replaced (`combat_shapes_classify_the_existing_skills_as_before`), and that a new skill works from the script alone (`a_new_skill_needs_only_the_script`).

## How to get to it (user POV)

Characters choose force through graphs or acts; players use the starter skills through `use_skill` (verify-player `combat.md`). Rules live in `living/scripts/skills.rhai:1001,1025,1038` (attack, threaten, seize checks) and the combat shapes at `living/scripts/skills.rhai:1342-1358` (`<skill>_combat()`, `skill_bar()`; docs/COMBAT.md "Where combat rules live"). The authority selects its primitives by those traits: `living/authority/src/act.rs:366-369` (facts by `force`/`dares`), `:579` (`begin`: aim, melee, hostile victim; drops a force word a stored graph still carries), `:2126` (`damage`: a dash that evades dodges, as the dodge does and the jump doesn't (the shape's `evades`, on for a dash unless `evades: false`), a facing guard takes its `takes`, the rest lands in full).

## Driving it with mechanics and scenes.py

Run mechanics, `seize` and `skirmish` from [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md) (`skirmish` on an island run: `$V launch --run core-<x> --seed island`, then `scenes.py skirmish --run core-<x>`). Mechanics covers a threat, a blow that kills, blows landing on the yielded (with the news) and wolf reluctance. The scene gives four stones to a victim. Initial seizure must fail without changing either inventory. The victim yields; the yielded seizure then succeeds. Continue with a badly hurt victim, transfer two stones, check victim and witness percepts, then attack after a fresh yield until death.

## Gotchas

- Seize requires below 35% health without a yield. The fallback path attacks until the victim is below 30%, then replaces the attacker's graph to stop; blows no longer stop by themselves. Record that setup.
- Yield lasts 20 seconds. Re-yield before the lethal check.
- Chronicle coalesces repeated events. Compare `at_ms`, not row count, for repeated yields.
- Dense-battle performance is measured in docs/COMBAT.md, not by this skill. Throw and the older `shoot` remain unverified here.
- The skirmish scene is stochastic (who targets whom); it asserts kinds of outcomes, not counts.
