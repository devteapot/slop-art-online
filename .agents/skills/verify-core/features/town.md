# Town life

Residents of a land world's settlements live a daily rhythm on their own graphs. Every resident has a mind (since 2026-11-11); this skill starts no mind service, so they live on their starting ways with a deliberation pending. Design and measurements: `docs/TOWN_LIFE.md`; personal rhythms: `docs/SEEDED_LIVES.md`.

## Sub-features

- **Places a resident knows.** World creation seeds `place` rows per settled resident (`work`, `square`, `inn`, `evening`) from the seed's `town_life` (`living/authority/src/town.rs` `seat`): lot kinds of the resident's own settlement, a resource or structure kind near it, or `plaza`; only spots a path reaches from home. Graphs reach them as `{"place": "work"}`; `home` stays `character.home_x/home_y` (the household's door).
- **Starting ways.** The seed's `ways` (`living/seeds/island.json`; Cullet's from `living/seeds/worlds/cullet/ways.json`, merged by `cullet_plan.py` with its `town_life`) override the repertoire's `common` routines by name, give each occupation its work routine, and replace the desires `top` (`seed.rs` `give_repertoire`). They are keyed by the occupation string, as data; the engine has no notion of a trade. Rules tests `graph::seed_repertoire_tests::island_ways_are_valid` and `cullet_check::ways_tests::cullet_ways_are_valid_graphs` (every routine and the top parse under the world's own rules, and each trade has a `town_life.work` place) check them.
- **Daily rhythm, each their own.** Each person has a `rhythm` row (private): when they rise and go to bed, how long their evening is, their working hours (`work_from_h`, `work_to_h`) and a second sleep (`nap_from_h`, `nap_to_h`; 0 and 0 is none, the default of both column pairs), from the seed's `residents[].rhythm` (`rises` at any hour, `sleeps`, `evening_hours`, `works: [from, to]`, `second_sleep: [from, to]`, across midnight) or drawn per person at birth (`seed::drawn_rhythm`, from the person's id alone, with no working hours). Graphs read it as the `bedtime`, `evening` and `work_hours` weights and conditions (`common::day_phase` over `living_rules::rhythm::Hours::phase`): bedtime also covers a second sleep; with no working hours set, `work_hours` is every waking hour outside the evening; without a row, the world's night and 18–22. Authority test `seed::tests::drawn_rhythms_keep_their_phase` checks that 20,000 drawn rhythms keep the old rule's bedtime and evening at every minute; `seeded_rhythms_rise_at_any_hour` and the rules' `rhythm::tests` check seeded ones. Starting desires are varied per person (`genes::vary_ways` at seeding). Work by day at the workplace (`keep`, `gather`, `cook`, `read`, `tend`, `store`); children `play` near home or the square; one's own evening at the household's evening place (inn, square, home or work: `town_life.evening`) resting and nodding; one's own bedtime at home asleep or resting; mornings away to work. A person's new day begins at its own rising (a feeling and a deliberation request, once a game day: `rhythm.last_day`, set to the day it is born into), not at a dawn broadcast; `morning` checks that nobody feels it more than once a day.
- **Weather.** One public `weather` row, drawn per spell from the world's seed and its climate (the seed's `weather`: `spell_s`, `rain_share`; none, never rain) by housekeeping, or held by the admin scene reducer `set_weather(raining, secs)` (`secs = 0` hands it back). `{"raining": true}` and the `rain` weight read it; rain out from under a roof is tiring (`rates` in `skills.rhai`, `wet(a)`).
- **Gestures and their meaning.** `wave`, `nod`, `face`, `step_aside`, `keep`, `play` are skills in `skills.rhai` with `_check`/`_duration`/`_done` and a `<skill>_shows()` trait map (`look`, `faces`, `turn`, `aside`, `record`; `living_rules::social`). `hail` (an effect) greets the target: `mind_state.hailed_ms`/`hailer`, `{"hailed": N}`, target `"hailer"`, weight `hailed`. A gesture back within 10 s answers instead of greeting again.
- **Commotion.** Target `"commotion"` and weight `commotion`: the nearest `attack`/`threat`/`killing`/`death` chronicle row of the last 8 s within sight.
- **What clients see.** `act_card` (one row per shown skill: code, look, faces, aside) and `doing` (per character: code, target, point, since/until), public; `skill_use` keeps each `record` gesture from intention to outcome.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Launch an island run (`$V launch --run core-<x> --seed island`) and drive `scripts/town.py --run core-<x>`. It holds the sky dry with `set_weather(false, 3600)` and waits for the world's own hours (12-minute day, no compression), so a full run takes about 14 minutes. Scenes: `work` (10–15 h), `greet`, `approach`, `speak`, `aside` (8–16 h; the player identity through `human_act`, `human_move` and `human_say`), `rain` (11–15 h; `set_weather(true, 120)`), `wolf` (13–17 h; admin `place_at` a bold wolf beside workers, `kill` afterwards), `evening` (18.6–19.8 h), `night` (0.5–4 h; also checks that each keeps their own hours: the spread of `rhythm` rises and bedtimes), `morning` (7.2–9.5 h). `--scene NAME` runs one. `--scene census` records, once per world hour for a day, where adults are and what everyone does, on an undisturbed world with its own weather (evidence, no checks).

**Rhythms at any hour and Cullet's trades** (`scripts/rhythm.py`, since `lives-3`): `rhythm.py seed --run core-<x>` writes `core-<x>/cullet-rhythm.json` (Cullet's plan, laws, ways and town life with thirteen cast residents compiled by `author_world.compile_residents`, a 12-minute day, a few goods in their packs); launch with `--seed "$PWD/.local/living/verify/core-<x>/cullet-rhythm"` and drive `rhythm.py drive --run core-<x>` (about 15 minutes). It checks the rows (a night stoker rising at 14:00 and working 22:00–06:00, the lamp keeper rising at 13:00, the baker's second sleep, Cullet's life stages: Tobiah 16 and Col 15 adults, Halvard 70 an elder), that the stokers and the blower start with their trade's routine and a workplace, then places a furnace beside the day stoker at the Cone (the plan places none yet) and samples: the day watch at the Cone feeding and making glass while the night stoker sleeps at home (09:00–13:30); the night stoker up and off work while the baker sleeps again (15:00–18:30); the night stoker at the Cone keeping the furnace lit while the day stoker sleeps, the lamp keeper at the Watchglass (23:00–04:00); nobody dead of old age. Since `layout-2` it also checks that everyone knows the six named places of the plan's open lots (`town_life.places`: the Bleach, the Readers' yard, the clay pit, the ropewalk, the wrack stance, the boatyard) and that five residents whose trade spends named hours at one are there in those hours, having walked in through the gate of a wall that blocks: Otto Brandt at the clay pit, Benedikt Orme and Edda Hollin in the Readers' yard, Greta Dunmore on the Bleach, Bram Calloway in the boatyard (`HABITS` in the script: on a 12-minute day a game hour is 30 s, so each check takes the later part of the habit's hours). Evidence: `rhythm.json`, `rhythm-rows.json`, `rhythm-samples.jsonl`.

## Evidence

`town.json` (every check) and `town-<scene>.json` (positions now, activity, `doing` code and places per resident; skill uses; chronicle for the wolf) in the run directory; `actions.log` holds every call.

## Gotchas

- Positions are evaluated from each `body` segment at the driver's clock (`pose`), which is the host clock the container shares.
- The natural weather is held dry for the run; without that a natural spell can rain on any scene.
- `aside` needs a resident standing where at most 17 of the 25 surrounding tiles are walkable with a clear 6-tile approach; it retries for 30 s and reports when none is found.
- Scenes assert shares and kinds, not exact counts: who stands where depends on the RNG and the hour the run started.

## Hospitality

`host` (docs/TOWN_LIFE.md, "What residents can do"): `host_check`, `host_duration` and `host_done` in `living/scripts/skills.rhai`; `host_done` names the morning hour the stay ends (`until_hour`). The engine (`town::host`) finds the house at the actor's home door, refuses without one, writes a private `guest` row and the guest's `lodging` place, and chronicles it. `town::guests_leave` (housekeeping, 1 Hz) ends stays through the `guest.until_ms` index; `town::guest_of` makes taking from the host household's store no theft during a stay. Driven by verify-player's `host.py` (a resident's graph runs the skill).
