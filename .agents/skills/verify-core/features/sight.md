# Sight for minds and behaviour graphs

Since 2026-11-09 (docs/PERCEPTION.md, "Minds see what their character sees" and "What an observer perceives of a target"). Every perception path for minds and graphs uses `perception::in_sight`: within the observer's own sight (day, night, a torch) and a clear sight line. Others are described through the `perceived_of` projection (a name only when known).

Since `laws-2` (2026-11-11) how far each creature sees and hears is the script's `senses(a)` law (docs/WORLD_RULES.md, "Laws"): 11 m by day, 6 at night, 9 with a torch in the pack, hearing 9 m (one tile is one metre). The authority asks it per creature through `common::senses_of`, which gathers only the fields the law reads (`act::law_ctx`), keeps the answer per state (`Scripts::senses`) and per creature for one moment. Onlookers and listeners are gathered within the law's `reach` (`senses_law()`), then each one's own senses are tested.

## Sub-features

- A behaviour graph's scene (`nearest`, `sees`, a creature by id) holds only creatures with a clear sight line: a resident whose graph waves at someone it `sees` waves from the open and not from behind rock or a wall (`skill_use`).
- Witnessed events (`perceive::witnessed`): a fight is an experience (`kind` `fight`) for a minded resident in the open within sight, and for none behind rock or a wall.
- Noticing structures (`brain.rs` `noticed`, since `feedback-1`): new tiles of one kind and owner that touch (within 1.5 tiles) are one structure, noticed once with its extent ("You notice a gate about 7 by 8 tiles, 6 tiles southwest"), and not at all when part of it was already familiar. Unit test `a_row_of_gate_tiles_is_one_gate` (the island's first gate, 24 tiles); in a real island arrival a newcomer's percepts held 24 gate lines before and 1 after (trials `fbbase`, `fbbr`, docs/MIND_FEEDBACK.md).
- First sights in a crowd (`scenes.py crowd_sight`, since strangers-1, docs/STRANGERS.md decision 3): everyone in sight is perceived. Before, a person with no relation row was not announced while more than `crowd` (6) people were in sight. Now each person (or one of one's kind) seen for the first time is told; the first sights of one look share one line, unnamed people first, then the nearest ("For the first time you see a stranger, 2 tiles east; and a stranger, 3 tiles east."), at most 8 to a line (more wait for the next look, untouched); the experience's `subject` and `object` are the first two named; each gets its `familiar` row. Other news (a wolf, someone again after a long time, danger close) stays at two lines a look. Unit test `brain::tests::first_sights_of_one_look_share_one_line`. The scene places an observer among 8 spawned strangers within 4.5 m on an open line (a crowd of 9), then brings in 2 more, and records the first-sight lines (count, characters, people named) in `crowd_sight-measure.json`; `--baseline` records without asserting (a build from before the fix gives none).
- Someone too far away to make out (far-label, 2026-11-12, docs/STRANGERS.md §1): a `perceived_of` law may mark a person `far` (Cullet's past `known_within`, 24 m). The projection's label is then "someone too far away to make out", known or not, for minds' scenes, percepts and players' `seen_*` labels; the scene entry keeps its `id` and has no `name` and no `community`; `noticed` tells no first sight (nor "again after a long time") of them and writes no `familiar` row until they can be made out, looking again every 5 s. Animals keep their kind. Rules tests `cullet_check::names_only_within_knowing_distance`, `script::what_one_perceives_of_another_is_the_law_s`; `scenes.py stranger_filter` (with an overlay, [graphs.md](graphs.md)).
- Percepts name others by the projection's label: "A stranger attacked you", "A stranger said to you" until the observer knows the name (family, community, someone its mind has feelings about, someone who said their name).
- What someone is seen doing (`perception::doing_of`, since `fix-perceived-moving`): an AI body's activity label (`goto → (x,y)`, `wait`), with a creature target named as the observer perceives it since first-sight-crowd (`goto → you`, `follow → a stranger`, `follow → Neve Calloway`, the far label; the doer's own act feedback keeps "→ person"). Only a body under motion intent with no activity of its own is seen by its motion (`walk → you`; the player's case is in verify-player [perception](../../verify-player/features/perception.md)). No AI body steers by direction (`steer.goal` 3). Unit tests `perception::doing_tests` (the label's geometry); `scenes.py doing` on a default-seed run.
- Names at a death (`scenes.py death-names`, since `fix-death-labels`, 2026-11-11): who knew the dead by name when they died keeps it. `act::die` calls `perception::remember_member` before the dead's `membership` row goes, so each other member of the dead's community gets an `acquaintance` row (observer = member, known = the dead; gone when that member dies, `forget_dead`), and `knows_name` (and the mind's `known_as`, which reads the same tables) still names the dead in the death percept, at the remains ("the remains of Edda") and in memory. A witnessed event's object reads it as done to them (`perceive::rendered`): the killer reads "A stranger was killed by you after yielding", a storage's owner "… from your storage" (unit test `the_one_an_event_was_done_to_reads_it_as_done_to_them`). The scene spawns four minded people; one founds a community and welcomes a second (`found`, `join`, `welcome` through `mind_act`), an outsider kills the founder after the founder yields, a fourth person who knows no one watches. It checks the member's death percept and remains percept name the dead, the killer's says "you" and not the killer's name, the bystander reads strangers, and only the member gained an acquaintance with the dead. Evidence: `death-names-texts.json` (every witness's death and remains texts), `death-names-membership-{before,after}.json`, `death-names-acquaintance-after.json`.
- Unchanged: hearing (speech, signals) and smell (scent) are not sight.
- Light on people (since `rules-1`): the sight rule everywhere is the wider of the onlooker's sight and the target's `glow` (a law the shipped script lacks, so nothing changes on it); `perceived_of` may read `p.dist`. Driven on a seed with such laws by `scenes.py laws` ([laws.md](laws.md)).
- Drops (`scenes.py drop`, since 2026-11-11, docs/PERCEPTION.md "Drops"): a drop between ground levels (tile 8) hides nothing. A graph across a drop from a fight sees the attacker (waves at it) and witnesses the fight; a person sent (`goto`) across it never stands on a drop tile, and its route (`body.path`) never crosses one (steering and routing treat it as a wall); the geometry is chosen away from the drop's ends. Rules test `map::tests::a_drop_blocks_walking_but_not_sight_or_missiles` and, on the plan-test land, `settle::plan::tests::people_see_across_the_plans_drop_but_cannot_walk_across`.
- Low things (`scenes.py low`, since 2026-11-11, docs/ISLAND_AUTHORITY.md "Sight over low things" and "Sight through what is open at eye height"): the same scene across low tiles (9) on an island run. Since eye-2 those are not only carts: a thing above the eye is `Low` wherever its model is open at eye height (its measured `eye_cells` against `LandParams.sight.solid`, 0.8), so an open stall, the fantasy well, a fountain's basin and a building's overhang or steps are low too; `low-spots.json` records which tiles the scene crossed. Rules tests: `host::tests::sight_is_blocked_only_above_eye_height` (past a cart one sees; the seed's `hides_m` decides a kind outright, stalls at 3 m hiding throughout and wells at 0.95 m nowhere; an eye outside the measured band, 3 m, falls back to the height rule) and `host::tests::a_well_and_an_open_stall_are_seen_through_but_a_closed_building_is_not` (on the arrival square every line past a stall and across the well is seen, every line past a townhouse hidden; the tiles differ from the height rule only where `Wall` became `Low` or `Drop`).
- Senses as a law (`scenes.py senses`): watchers 5, 8, 10 and 13 m from a subject on an open line face it only when they see it. The shipped law by day: 5, 8, 10 (at night: 5). Speech heard at 5 and 8, not 10. An installed overlay with day sight 14 and hearing 12: 13 m seen, 10 m heard. An overlay where it is always dark for the eyes: only 5 m seen, and a torch granted to the 8 and 10 m watchers makes 8 m seen and 10 m not. The shipped script is installed again at the end.

## Driving it

```bash
python3 .agents/skills/verify-core/scripts/scenes.py sight --run "$R" > ".local/living/verify/$R/sight.txt" 2>&1
python3 .agents/skills/verify-core/scripts/scenes.py senses --run "$R" > ".local/living/verify/$R/senses.txt" 2>&1
python3 .agents/skills/verify-core/scripts/scenes.py doing --run "$R" > ".local/living/verify/$R/doing.txt" 2>&1
python3 .agents/skills/verify-core/scripts/scenes.py crowd_sight --run "$R" > ".local/living/verify/$R/crowd_sight.txt" 2>&1
python3 .agents/skills/verify-core/scripts/scenes.py death-names --run "$R" > ".local/living/verify/$R/death-names.txt" 2>&1
```

`drop` needs a world with drops (`$V launch --run $R --seed plan-test`, or `island`): it finds a run of 1 to 3 drop tiles on one axis with walkable ground behind both ends on `terrain_chunk`, spawns four minded people and installs their graphs with `set_behavior`. Evidence: `drop-spots.json`, `drop-walk.json` (the walker's trail and its `goto` uses), `drop-actions.jsonl`.

`doing` finds an open line (`terrain_chunk`), spawns five minded people, places an observer at its west end and four AI characters on it (a walk to the observer, a walk in from 10 m to a spot near it, a follow, one standing by), waits until the observer has been there 45 s (a `think` leaf asks only then), and reads the observer's `deliberation` scene after a `think` leaf. A walk to the observer that ends before the observer looks is reported, not fatal. Evidence: `doing-scene.json`, `doing-activities.json`, `doing-steer.json`, `doing-actions.jsonl`.

`senses` finds an open line of 16 tiles on the map (`terrain_chunk`), spawns five minded people, and installs overlays of the shipped script with `install_script` (admin), as a floor would. It reads the hour from `world` and `clock` and expects the night's values after 20:00; it waits 30 s when within 25 s of dusk or dawn. Evidence: `senses-uses-*.json`, `senses-line.json`, `senses-actions.jsonl`.

On a default-seed run (`$V launch --run $R`). It finds the geometry on the world's map (`terrain_chunk`), spawns four minded people (`spawn_crowd 4 true`), places them with `place_at`, installs their graphs with `set_behavior` (two watchers that wave at the attacker when they see it, an attacker), and reads `skill_use`, `experience` and `chronicle` as the owner. Evidence: `sight-*.json`, `sight-actions.jsonl`.

## Gotchas

- Behaviour change, expected: residents no longer react to fights, people or deaths behind walls, rock or shut gates within their sight radius; they still hear speech through them.
- A map without a thin rock or wall run with open ground beside it fails with "no wall geometry", a fixture failure; `drop` fails with "no drop geometry" on a map without drops (the default seed has none), and `low` with "no low geometry" on one without low things; `senses` fails with "no open line" on a map without 16 open tiles in a row.
- `drop` and `low` place the watcher the gap plus 4 tiles from the eye (5 to 7 m) and do not wait for daylight: at night (6 m sight) a 3-tile gap leaves the watcher out of sight, a fixture condition, not a rule failure. Their sight checks are not fatal; read the scene's output.
- `senses` leaves the shipped script installed even when a check fails (a `finally`); a run interrupted mid-scene keeps an overlay until cleanup deletes the database.

## Owners

Since 2026-11-12 (`owner-known`; docs/PERCEPTION.md "Whose a thing is, and what places are called"). A structure's owner is named to an observer only when the world's `owner_shown(o)` law says so (shipped: it knows the owner's name, or the structure's kind `shows_owner`): `common::owner_label` for a mind's scene (`by`, `home_of`), what it notices ("You notice a house built by …") and the scene's short list ("Bryn Wren's house" / "your house" / "house"). The public `land` row's `params` is `living_rules::land::public_params` of the seed's (no lot, spot, street or household names, keys renamed in step); the private `land_plan` keeps them, and `install_script` scrubs a world made before, once (`island::scrub_land`).

`scenes.py owners` on a `plan-test` run (a planned settlement with households): the public land row carries none of the seed's lot, spot or street names and `land_plan` does, and the public row is no bigger than `land_plan`'s (no defaults written out); a spawned newcomer at an owned structure gets it without `by`/`home_of` and never the owner's name in its scene or lines; the owner is told it is its own; someone of the owner's community is told the owner's name. Cargo: `land::tests::public_params_build_the_same_land` (equal digests for Cullet and plan-test; no bigger than given, floor-megacity too).
