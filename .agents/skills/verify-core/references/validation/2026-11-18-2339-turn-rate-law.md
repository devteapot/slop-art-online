The official [SpacetimeDB reducer documentation](https://spacetimedb.com/docs/functions/reducers/) describes the transactional reducer path. The workspace pins the Rust module and SDK to 2.14.1. Authority facing already consumes the scripted rate through `common::moves_of`; its 6.0 rad/s fallback for a failed `motion` evaluation and the rules library's `Tuning::default()` both remain.

## 30 rad/s overlay

The first overlay attempt (`core-turn-rate-overlay-233923`) hit a fixture error before installing the overlay. The open-ground search started near the map edge and queried a negative terrain chunk ID. The retry (`core-turn-rate-overlay-234022`) searched near the tested interior reversal location from the default run.

The retry composed `fn laws() { let values = base_laws(); values.turn_rate = 30.0; values }` over the shipped script, installed it through the admin reducer, and verified script readback. The same look-4 replay captured 31 body rows and reached 30.0 rad/s. The maximum reconstructed turn over one nominal 60 Hz tick was 0.500002 rad, within floating-point and timestamp precision of **0.5 rad (28.65°) per tick**. The largest apparent facing step between successive rows was 0.0°, with no flips observed. The 30 rad/s scene checks per-row rate and continuity because a continuous turn at that rate can legitimately exceed 90° in 0.25 s. Evidence is in `.local/living/verify/core-turn-rate-overlay-234022/overlay.json`, with the driver transcript in `overlay.txt`.
