# yield-news: striking the yielded is news even when a blow ties the yield in one tick

Branch `yield-news` on main `5655887af`. Spark (aarch64), a private server `slop-layoutseed-20261123` (SpacetimeDB 2.14.1, `127.0.0.1:3591`, `LIVING_STDB_CONTAINER`/`LIVING_STDB_URL`, the container pinned to CPUs 0-4,10-14), a private `CARGO_TARGET_DIR`, builds through `build-slot`. "Under load": 10 CPU-bound processes pinned to 0-4,10-14 for the whole series. No mind service, no model calls.

**The failure.** verify-core mechanics' "force: blows land on the yielded, and striking them is news" failed first-run in the scene gate of `sides-mixed-defence` (gate-1123010413-18a5b3, 2026-11-23 ~01:04) and passed alone; 2 of 30 under pinned load in the planting-flake series; never on dev-box (49/49). Each time J yielded and was told "struck you though you had yielded", but the chronicle's "struck J, though J had yielded" was never written.

**The cause (a rules race, `act.rs` `damage`).** Whether a blow at a yielded victim was news was decided by `struck_since_yield = yielded && v.hurt_ms >= yield_at && v.hurt_by == attacker`: the victim's last hurt at or after the yield's time meant this attacker had already struck since the yield. J yields 1 s (`yield_duration`) after the blow that hurt it, and I's attack cycle is about 983 ms, so I's next blow often completes in the same tick as the yield. Since fair-ties (`0877fe93a`) such a tie is ordered by a per-tick coin. When the blow goes first, it is an ordinary blow at the yield's millisecond (`hurt_ms == yield_at`), and every later blow by I counted as struck since the yield: the news was never told for the whole yield.

**The fix.** A mind-state mark, `YIELD_STRUCK_MARK` (like `YIELD_MARK`; no column), is set on every blow that lands on a yielded victim. `struck_since_yield` (a pure function) reads it: yielded, the same attacker as the last hurt, and a blow on the yielded at or after the yield's mark. A blow processed before the yield in the same millisecond is not on the yielded, sets no mark, and the next blow is the news. Otherwise unchanged (another attacker's blow is news of its own; a new yield starts over).
