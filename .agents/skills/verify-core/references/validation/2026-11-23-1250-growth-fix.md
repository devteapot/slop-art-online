No paid model calls. The diagnosis and the 30-minute measurements are in [docs/GATE216_GROWTH.md](../../../../../docs/GATE216_GROWTH.md).

**The change (authority):**
- `common::lit_at` walks a per-chunk list of the kinds that give light (`chunk_few`, `Few::Lights`, a `BTreeMap` cache drawn from the chunk list).
- `known::remember_gates` walks the gates in sight only (`few_near`, `Few::Gates`), nearest first as before.
- Housekeeping walks the living through the `alive` index in id order, and `stats.deaths` reads a new private `census` row counted in `act::die_unseen`.
- `perception::set_player` drops the former character's `line_seen` rows (`frame::forget_lines`), as `outcomes::forget` drops its outcome rows.
- Bindings regenerated; verify-client's private inventory lists `census`.
