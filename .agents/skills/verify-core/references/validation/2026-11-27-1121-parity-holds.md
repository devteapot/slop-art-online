# parity-holds: a store's contents known only as seen inside it (`perceived_holds_on`, off)

Branch `parity-holds` from origin/main `dd434378e`, driven on `14148992a` (the change) and `2a85a3d5e` (the scene, verify-client's inventories, bindings). Spark (aarch64), private servers `slop-ph-stdb-1127` (scenes, `127.0.0.1:3471`), `slop-pdet-a` (det_compare, copies on 3476–3477), `slop-ph-trials` (trials, `:3481`, with its own Neo4j `slop-ph-neo4j`), SpacetimeDB 2.14.1. Builds through `build-slot` with private target dirs, pinned to CPUs 0-4,10-14; launches under `trial-activity.lock`. No paid model calls.

**Change.** Under the new ordinary law `perceived_holds_on` (off as shipped), a graph's `{"nearest": {"kind": …, "holds": item}}` reads what the character saw inside a store (`stores.rs`, the new private table `seen_store`: one row per store it looked into, at most 32, written by its own looks from within 2.5 m at its noticing, its player's sweep, or a `holds` test beside the store); never looked into, it may hold anything (worth a look); remembered without the item, no, for 10 min. A mind's scene shows contents beyond 2.5 m only as remembered (`"not looked into"` otherwise). A player reads its rows through the new view `my_seen_stores`. Off, nothing is written or read; `det::trace` hashes `seen_store` only once it has rows. Why remembered stores rather than a contents perception: features/perceived-holds.md.
