# exact-items: the frames take the sweep's health readings; `show_fighter` takes the band (verify-core)

Branch `exact-items` (from `origin/main` `4b4b042a5`), dev-box, `sg-stdb` (v2.14.1). These are the Spark's two exact items from sweep-exact (docs/COMBAT_PHASE.md, "What next"), in the call shape it agreed with three conditions.

**Change.**
- `perception::sweep` returns `Swept { ids, health }`: the tick's health readings, taken out of `Shared.health` at the end of the sweeps.
- `frame::deliver` seeds its per-call `Sources.health` with them.
- `show_fighter` takes `known_band: Option<u8>`. The sweep passes each newcomer's band from the projection it has just written, and the frame's own callers pass `None` and read as before.
- `Shared` stays private to `perception.rs`.

**The Spark's conditions.**
1. **No write between the read and its reuse.** In `tick::tick` the perception sweep runs right before `deliver`, after every phase that damages or heals (activity completions, missiles, the combat cadence's begins). Between the two, only the `memory_test` diagnostic runs, and it writes no `vitals`. The sweeps write `projected`, `perceived`, `heard` and copies, never `vitals`. `admin_set_vitals` and players' moves are their own transactions. The reasoning is in a comment at the seeding site. The passed band comes from the projection written in the same sweep, from the same reading, and is used at once.
2. **The cache is local to the call.** `Swept` is a value returned to the tick and moved into `deliver`. Its readings live in `Sources`, which is set at the start of `deliver` and dropped at its end, as before; nothing new is static.
3. **Proof on a battle where health changes, with counts.**
   - Under `regard_cache_check`, `deliver` compares every seeded reading with a fresh `vitals` read (`frames:health_seed_differed`). `show_fighter` compares every passed band with `perceived_band` (`frames:band_passed_differed`), with an error line for each difference.
   - Runs: `battle_viewers.py`, Cullet, 200 fighters, players on the 10 m ring (fought over), keep-alive, `profile_detail`, full module log.

**`det_compare` (the Spark's tool), and why it is not the proof here.**
- My two items run only when players are present (sweeps and frames are for players), so a battle without players proves nothing about them. I added `prepare --players N --ring LO,HI` (players joined over HTTP with no connection, placed round the battle) and `run --cpus ""`. The dev-box's rootless podman has no `cpuset` controller delegated, so pinning fails there.
- With 16 players, a base-against-base run (`ei-base` both sides, 1,200 ticks) differs in 1,199 ticks: `hear_salt` from the second tick, then `heard` (keyed by that salt), and auto-increment ids (`seen_character_copy`, `act_outcome`, `line_seen`). That is the run-to-run nondeterminism the Spark already noted for `projected.id`, plus the hearing salt.
- So `det_compare` cannot yet compare builds with players. The candidate run differs in the same way.
- **For the Spark:** a content comparison without id columns, and a salt that follows `det_clock`, would make it usable with players.
