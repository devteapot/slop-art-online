# parity-can: `can` decided on what the actor perceives (`perceived_can_on`, off)

Branch `parity-can` from origin/main `e220bea8e`, driven on `566e718e0` (the change) and `50161b5c9` (the scene). Spark (aarch64), private servers `slop-ppt-stdb` (scenes, `127.0.0.1:3391`) and `slop-ppt-deta` (det_compare, copies on 3393–3396), SpacetimeDB 2.14.1. Builds through `build-slot` with a private `CARGO_TARGET_DIR`; launches under `trial-activity.lock`; worlds deleted by `verify.py cleanup`. No mind service in the core runs, no model calls. Host load 35–50 (other workers' builds).

**Change.** Under the new ordinary law `perceived_can_on` (off as shipped), `act::can` (a graph's `can`) checks the skill on the context as the actor perceives it (`act::perceive`): another's health as shown (`perceived_hp`), holdings assumed to be what is asked (`TargetFacts::perceived`), knows and sex unknown, a yield only when seen (`yielded_seen` over its own lines), `hurt_by_near` only while the striker is seen, a store's food as "food". `act::begin` still checks the truth. A predator's `dares` counts only the people it sees or smells near its prey, and its prey's visible doing. The game client's choices call the same rules functions (its yield only under the law). Off, every path is as before. features/perceived-can.md.
