## The failure

`scenes.py say` failed 8 of its 37 checks on main's own build, all `mind_install` forms (seen by the `heed` worker on 2026-11-23). Run `core-saymain1-024802` (load 15–16) and run `core-saymain2-025927` (load 17 falling to 4) both failed the same 8:
- canonical to bare-id, canonical to nearest;
- flat to id, flat to nearest;
- do to id, do to bare-id;
- do-object to id, do-object to nearest.

Each read "FAIL mind_install <form> to <target> speaks", with no chronicle line for it.

## The cause: the scene, not the rules

- **The rule.** Since say-once (merge `7f21e425e`, 2026-11-13), a mind plan's say node (`brain.source = "mind"`, which `mind_install` sets) does not say a line that nearly matches one its speaker said to the same person within 45 s. "Nearly" means shared words (3+ letters) make up 0.6 or more of their combined words (`speech::said_to_recently`, `brain.rs` "already said").
- **The scene.** Its lines differed only in number and labels ("Morning, line 14 (canonical, bare-id, mind_install)."), and `mind_install` splits into the words "mind" and "install".
- **The match.** In `core-saymain1-024802`'s chronicle, mind_install lines 13, 17, 21 and 23 were spoken, at 34.0, 69.5, 105.9 and 119.9 s. Each line held back matches one of them spoken less than 45 s before. Lines 17 and 23 fall just under the cut (0.57) against the earlier spoken lines in the window, and 21 and 23 come after the window of the matching earlier line ran out. Load only moves which lines straddle the 45 s window, hence the 7–8 failures the heed worker saw.
- **Bisect.** Both commits were built from `git archive` exports, each in its own target dir: `6790f2ca7` (say-once's parent on main) 37/37 (`core-saypre-025335`); `7f21e425e` (the say-once merge) 29/37, the same 8 (`core-saypost-025335`).
- **Why it went unseen.** Say-once's merge ran verify-core `senses`, not `say`.

## The fix and its proof

Each of the 24 lines now starts with a phrase of its own; any two lines share under 0.3. On main's build: 37/37 twice, `core-sayfix1-025425` (load 20) and `core-sayfix2-025929`.
