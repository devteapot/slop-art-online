## What changed

- **New cases.** `living/tools/scene_gate.py` gains two path-selected groups, each on a fresh default-seed world of its own:
  - **speech:** `say`, `heard_first`, `audience`;
  - **steering:** `flee_wall`, `pursue_last_seen`.
- **Selection.** The speech group is selected by `SPEECH_PATHS`, or by a changed hunk of `brain.rs`, `perceive.rs` or `skills.rhai` that names speech (`SPEECH_WORDS`, identifiers read by their parts). The steering group is selected by `STEERING_PATHS`.
- **Why.** Say-once (`7f21e425e`) changed `speech.rs` and `brain.rs`. It broke `say`, and no gate case ran `say` for ten days.
- **A test-only fix to `audience`.** Its law-off check now reads its own speaker's rows. Run on the shared speech world after `say` and `heard_first`, it had failed "law off: a line writes no audience row". The rows of their lines stay until a line comes 60 s on.
