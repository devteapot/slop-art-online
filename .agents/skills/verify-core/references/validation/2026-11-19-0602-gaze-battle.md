# Gaze cost in the wooded battle

Eight five-minute runs alternated gaze off and mixed gaze under
`~/slop-mq/battle.lock`. Each fresh island world had 310 ambient characters,
16 extra walkers and 96 spear fighters, for 422 living characters at both
endpoints. The sampled local census stayed at 96 within 30 m at all 61
checks in each run. Facing and 2.5D cover were on in both arms. The mixed
arm selected approximately 80% Middle, 10% Far and 10% Near for the fighters.
The remaining characters used Middle when gaze was enabled.

The paused setup put fighters in 48 pairs with fixed stations. They used the
authority's attack, block, dodge, face and return-to-station behavior.
Both arms used 0.01 spear damage to sustain the fixture. One owner CLI
subscription read deliberations and experiences. There were no player
connections or model requests in the battle.

Six five-second windows per run supplied the tick durations, sight timing
and visible-set churn. Cadence and subscription rates use the whole run.
Sight counts `scene.sensed`, the profiled complete-scene path. All gaze
changes happened during setup, so both measured arms had no attention writes,
human gaze calls or senses-view traffic. WASM measures the database at the
start and end. The host load samples and exact window timing are in each run's
`acceptance.json`.

Off mean 4.26. Mix mean 4.10, -3.6%. Cadence within off spread False. Late ticks within off spread False.

Across the off runs, the authority recorded 1,167–1,478 hits, 4,313–4,852
dodges and 2,731–3,084 blocks. Across mixed runs it recorded 1,314–1,502 hits,
4,065–4,518 dodges and 2,888–3,203 blocks.

The mixed sight cost passed the +15% budget. One mixed run reached
59.90173 ticks/s against the off maximum of 59.87707. Another had 0.16816% late
ticks against the off minimum of 0.22396%. Both departures were improvements,
and both were outside the required two-sided spread. The battle therefore
missed the strict tick checks. The switch remains off.

The first unrestricted battle control was excluded after the local count
fell to 79 and an ambient resident killed three fighters. Its traces and the
interrupted following arm remain in the archive alongside the final eight.
The profile collector pilot with continuous detailed logs was also excluded.

In the [Cullet comparison](2026-11-19-0507-gaze-cullet.md), the reducer counters
cover the approximately 30-second measured interval. SDK acknowledgement
latency and BSATN senses row traffic cover the load client's 42-second
interval, which includes the client-only tail. This is a loopback cost
comparison. The laptop's camera and Shade integration, a live old-module
migration and the full product performance gate are outside these results.
