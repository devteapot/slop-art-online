# A yield that outlasts the threat

The refusal-tell gate at `dbf839482` failed the threat check and passed its fresh-world rerun. Its authority and mechanics driver matched `origin/main` at `6b71513d5`. The first run recorded two threats, both perceived by Walker7, and no yield. The snapshot showed Walker7 in the fallback wait. The prior gather was explicitly broken off by placement. The gate saved neither walker’s knack nor the activity rows during the threats.

## Reproduction

SpacetimeDB 2.14.1 ran on a private Spark container with the default world and its shipped script. The main script hash matched the gate’s `eb9edd34b10a` prefix. No model service, observer or client subscriptions ran.

- The original force setup passed 12 of 12 focused runs at `6b71513d5`, with 20 live default-seed databases and 12 synchronized force scenes. The cast foraged for 120 seconds before its force scene. Three fresh quiet runs also passed, one at a time. All 15 yielded on the first threat.
- A diagnostic chose two naturally spawned walkers from 225 by their `threaten` and `yield` knacks and placed them at (130.5, 302.5) and (131.5, 302.5) after stopping both. At `6b71513d5`, the original graph failed with the selected pair. It started yield in the same tick as each of the two threats. The first threat lasted 1,544 ms, the second 1,529 ms, and the yield needed 2,499 ms. At threat expiry G returned to wait before yielding. F’s experience recorded the refusal to threaten the same target again within 30 seconds. A pair with a longer threat and shorter yield passed.
- The same targeted comparison at pre-fair-ties `85a7478c6` also failed with the selected slow yielder and passed with the control. The first threat lasted 1,471 ms and the yield needed 2,267 ms. The cause can occur before fair-ties, humans-map, relation-fight and heed. The matched unselected series passed 12 of 12 under the same parallel load and 3 of 3 quiet.
- With the changed graph on `6b71513d5`, the exact same slow main pair completed its 2,499 ms yield after the 1,515 ms threat expired. In a second run the installed graph stayed in wait across six observations before F's graph was installed, then yielded after the threat ended.
- With the changed driver, 12 of 12 fresh worlds passed all 23 mechanics checks (276 of 276). The unmodified authority at `6b71513d5` ran 12 full `verify_mechanics.py --gate-world` drivers with eight additional default-seed worlds ticking. The largest authority clock gaps by world were 26–74 ms.

The old graph put yield under a continuous `if`. [The evaluator](../../../../../living/authority/src/brain.rs#L668) tries the first branch on each evaluation and advances a sequence cursor when a leaf starts. [The condition](../../../../../living/authority/src/brain.rs#L1402) reads active phase-one rows by victim. The [activity starter](../../../../../living/authority/src/act.rs#L1329) wakes the victim and moves both participants to combat cadence. The [tick](../../../../../living/authority/src/tick.rs#L67) expires activities before processing wakes and cadence. Ordinary slots are once per 60 ticks. Combat slots are once per four ticks by default, with a gap of at most seven ticks across a phase reshuffle. The wake can evaluate the victim in the same tick, as it did in the targeted failures.

The shipped [skill script](../../../../../living/scripts/skills.rhai#L1554) gives base durations of 2,000 ms for threaten and 1,000 ms for yield. [Skill duration](../../../../../living/rules/src/script.rs#L2916) divides both by the actor’s skill factor because neither is a timed combat move. The old guard allowed the fallback wait to replace the unfinished yield when the threat expired. The scene now checks for the live threat at the first step of a sequence and lets its yield step run to completion. The assertion requires the matching threat and yield pair.

The two-start limit comes from [the threat cooldown](../../../../../living/authority/src/act.rs#L1126). The one-second fallback governs when the graph tries again, while the 30-second cooldown governs when the same pair can start another threat. Heed and relation-fight ship off on the default seed. The explicit ID target bypasses friend/enemy selection. The humans-map index change does not affect the AI threat check with heed off.

The test uses the existing admin reducer and scheduled tick in SpacetimeDB 2.14.1. The fixture reads the chronicle and experience to assert effects and reads activity and mind state on failure. See [SpacetimeDB reducers](https://spacetimedb.com/docs/functions/reducers/) and [schedule tables](https://spacetimedb.com/docs/tables/schedule-tables/).

The targeted traces establish a scene-staging failure matching the gate signature. The deleted gate world’s original activity durations cannot be recovered from the saved files.

The Spark archive `~/slop-evidence/force-investigation-20261126.tar.zst` contains the gate failure and fresh-world rerun, both sets of activity traces, and the full mechanics outputs. `just living-check` reused passing checks for identical inputs. The focused scripts ran under the shared trial activity lock.
