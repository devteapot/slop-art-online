# fair-ties: exact ties in a per-tick fair order, and the combat cadence's phases turned every second

Branch `fair-ties` from `origin/main` `4b9fa89cc`, Spark (aarch64), rustc 1.99.1, SpacetimeDB 2.14.1 on private containers (`slop-fairties-scn`, `127.0.0.1:3591`, for the scenes; `slop-fairties-deta`, `127.0.0.1:3581`, for the duel fixture and det templates). Both containers were pinned to the A725 cores (0-4, 10-14). No mind service, no model calls. The change and its proof are in docs/COMBAT.md, "Two blows due at the same millisecond, and the cadence's order". `tick.rs` alone changed: no table, reducer, view, subscription or law. So no skill's map drifted, and `features/tick.md`, `battle.md` and `README.md` name the new rules and their controls.

## det_compare and the fairness measurements

See docs/COMBAT.md. In short:
- main against the tie-break is identical through tick 30 of the Cullet battle of 200, and first differs at tick 31, a 20-body motion tie taken in another order.
- main against the tie-break compiled out is identical at the stop; the only per-tick difference is the known `deliberation` far-handle artifact.
- On Cullet with no fighters, main and the tie-break are identical to the final build over 3,600 ticks, through 87 ticks with ties.
- In forced-tie duels, the first-written windup resolved first in 208/208 on main and 99/209 on the branch.
- In paired forced-reach duels, the cadence-favoured side won 57.0% on the tie-break alone and 48.0% with the cadence turn.

## Not covered

- The other verify-core scenes: they touch no tick ordering beyond what these drive.
- Ties among missiles (`combat::missiles`) and the wake table's storage order (`wake().iter()`), which the change leaves as they were.
- Battles beyond 200 fighters, and the >512-row case, reasoned from the source rather than driven.
- Performance: the sort's cost was not measured. It sorts at most the due rows of a tick (111 in the battle of 200).
