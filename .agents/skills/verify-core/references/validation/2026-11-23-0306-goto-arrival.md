# A goto to a kind of ground arrives standing on it (`goto-arrival`): the core surface

Validated on the Spark on branch `goto-arrival` from `origin/main` `5655887af`; the change is commit `fe792ef53`. Server `slop-gotoarr-20261123` (private, `127.0.0.1:3613`, `clockworklabs/spacetime:v2.14.1`, pinned to CPUs 0-4,10-14); host load 2–20 over the runs. No paid model calls. The change is `act::begin_with`: a `goto` whose resolved target is a kind of ground (`on_ground_kind`: class 4, id 0, a name in `catalog::TERRAINS`, that is grass or forest) steers without `motion::FLAG_EARLY`. Docs: [features/steering.md](../../features/steering.md#a-goto-to-a-kind-of-ground), `docs/LIVING_CORE.md` ("No stop-and-go"), and a note in [features/mechanics.md](../../features/mechanics.md) ("Clearing from the forest's edge"). No table, reducer, view, subscription or launch command changed.

## The cause

`brain` resolves `{"nearest": "forest"}` (or grass) to the centre of the nearest tile of it in sight. A goto there took `Goal::point(target.at, sp, FLAG_EARLY)` like every point goto, and `motion` reported arrival as the final approach began (within `slow_radius`, 1.2 m). From a straight approach that is the tile beside, while the body glides on. The next step then ran on that tile: `clear_check`, `plant_check` and `graze_check` read `a.actor.terrain`, the ground underfoot, and refused. A graph retried and usually recovered. A player's client sends the next act on the goto's `done` outcome, so a player was refused every time.

## Recommendation: the truthful fix

The fix is scoped to tile-kind targets.
- The skills' "where you stand" stays the rule for every controller. A "within reach of" exception would have changed what clear, plant and graze mean, and each skill would have needed it.
- Places, points, creatures, `wander` and `follow` keep the early arrival, so their glide into the next movement is unchanged; `verify_steering` "two walks flow into each other" passes.
- The cost: a ground goto now ends about 0.5–1 s later, when the body stops on the tile's centre, and the work after it starts from a standstill (it roots the body anyway).
