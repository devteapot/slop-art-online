# Gaze cost in Cullet

Eight locked runs held 216 living characters. Each run used a fresh Cullet
world, with 64 residents and 152 walking Rust SDK clients, and a 30-second
profile. Facing and 2.5D cover were on in every arm. Clients subscribed to
scoped game rows and both senses views, with fixed terrain and lot windows
around their starting positions. There was no separate observer or inference
service. The mixed arm assigned approximately 80% Middle, 10% Far and 10% Near
before measurement. Far also set its band during setup.

The rapid arm offered a changed band per character every 250 ms, nominally
608 human requests and 256 mind requests per second. Timing jitter and the
strict limiter reduced actual writes. The table counts an insert or update as
one attention write. Human latency is the server's reducer-plus-query mean
and the SDK acknowledgement round trip. Senses traffic counts BSATN row
payloads from both senses views, with both rows in an update. Subscription
traffic uses the server's database-scoped bytes counter. Sight time is the
sum of `sight.player_candidates` and `scene.sensed`. Adds and drops combine
player contacts and the profiled graph scenes.

The Cullet mixed arm passed the +15% sight budget and both tick-spread checks.
All-Far and rapid toggling are separate stress results. The first series
before the daylight glow fix measured +28.0% mixed sight time. The archive
retains that series, the two post-fix calibration controls and the final
locked runs. All measurements are loopback diagnostics outside the
30-minute product gate.

Run records, host samples and profile excerpts are under
`.local/living/verify/gaze-accept-cullet-*/` in the evidence archive. The Spark
reported 20 CPUs (Cortex-X925 and Cortex-A725) and SpacetimeDB 2.14.1 without an
explicit container CPU or memory cap.
