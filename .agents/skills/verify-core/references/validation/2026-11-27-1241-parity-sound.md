# parity-sound: a sound out of sight known only by its way and band (`perceived_sound_on`, off)

Branch `parity-sound` from origin/main `3d41ec73d`, driven on `b18c39366` (the change) and `09e365e1c` (the scene). Spark (aarch64), private servers `slop-psp-stdb` (scenes, `127.0.0.1:3497`), `slop-psp-deta` (det_compare, copies on 3492–3495), `slop-psp-trials` (trials, `:3485`, with its own Neo4j `slop-psp-neo4j`), SpacetimeDB 2.14.1. Builds through `build-slot` with private target dirs, pinned to CPUs 0-4,10-14; launches under `trial-activity.lock`. No paid model calls.

**Change.** Under the new ordinary law `perceived_sound_on` (off as shipped), a graph's target `sound` and an unseen speaker turned to resolve to `living_rules::sight::heard_point`: the compass way and distance band a player's `heard_sounds` row carries, at the band's middle (`brain.rs` `Ev::heard_at`). Off, the creature's exact position. Rules test `sight::tests::a_heard_point_is_its_way_and_band`: the point measures back to the same way and band, and one way and band is one point. features/perceived-sound.md.
