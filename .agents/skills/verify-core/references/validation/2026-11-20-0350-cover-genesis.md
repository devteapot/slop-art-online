- Switching off mid-derivation stops it ("stopped: cover sight was switched off …"); switching on again finishes it.
- An update to another build mid-derivation restarted generation once (`restarts` 1); the bands were kept and identical (sha256 `278e39c1…`, plan-test).
- The non-land refusal reads "cover sight requires a land world (a seed whose map kind is "land"), once it is made".
- The tick compiled the mask in the transaction after the last step.
- Bands are byte-identical to main and between creation and switch-on: see the [generation record](../../../verify-generation/references/validation/2026-11-20-0350-cover-genesis.md).
