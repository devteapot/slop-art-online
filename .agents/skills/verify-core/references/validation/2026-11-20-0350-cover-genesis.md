# Cover geometry derived at switch-on (cover-genesis)

Validated on the Spark on branch `cover-genesis` (first run on `e3a076c3`, on `origin/main` `bf96d0a5`). Server: a private `clockworklabs/spacetime:v2.14.1` container, `slop-covergen-20261120` on `127.0.0.1:3391`, builds through `build-slot` with a private `CARGO_TARGET_DIR`, rustc 1.99.1. No model service, no Neo4j.

## Scenes

- `client-law-044344`: `law_override.py checks` on the island passed 61 of 61. It includes the new derivation scene:
  - `cover_sight_on` true is accepted on a land with no bands, and `cover_law` stays false while `cover_step` derives them.
  - Then all 256 bands are kept, `cover_derivation` reads done, a further law revision is written and `cover_law` turns true.
  - `cover_derivation` is private (anonymous SQL refused).
  - Then the cover laws' value-only checks run on the derived geometry.
  - The first attempt, `client-law-043249`, failed only its two client first-result checks: the verify-client SDK probe had not been built in this worktree. Every cover check passed.
- `core-cover-043249`: `cover.py` on Cullet passed 23 of 23. Cullet's bands come from its creation, in one staged band step.
- Cargo: `living-check` passed, including `cover::tests::ground_in_bands_is_ground`.

## Behaviour checked by hand (measurement worlds, same server)

- Switching off mid-derivation stops it ("stopped: cover sight was switched off …"); switching on again finishes it.
- An update to another build mid-derivation restarted generation once (`restarts` 1); the bands were kept and identical (sha256 `278e39c1…`, plan-test).
- The non-land refusal reads "cover sight requires a land world (a seed whose map kind is "land"), once it is made".
- The tick compiled the mask in the transaction after the last step.
- Bands are byte-identical to main and between creation and switch-on: see the [generation record](../../../verify-generation/references/validation/2026-11-20-0350-cover-genesis.md).
