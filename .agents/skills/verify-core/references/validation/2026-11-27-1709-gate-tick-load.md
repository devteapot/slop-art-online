# Tick smoke check under CPU contention

The merge gates for familiar-cap and neural-body-clone-2 each failed the first `tick` run and passed on retry. The copied neural-body-clone-2 result, `gate-1127170343-b39c3b-a-core/tick-results.json`, records 272 ticks over 5.262 s (51.69 Hz). The original scene reads the authority's `clock` singleton before and after a 5 s driver sleep and divides the change in `tick` by the change in `last_ms` in seconds. Its inclusive bounds were 52–65 Hz. Client scheduling changes where the interval starts and ends. The denominator comes from the authority. SpacetimeDB skips expired intervals when rescheduling delayed work, so contention can reduce the committed count. [Schedule tables](https://spacetimedb.com/docs/tables/schedule-tables/), [v2.14.1 scheduler](https://github.com/clockworklabs/SpacetimeDB/blob/v2.14.1/crates/core/src/host/scheduler.rs#L754-L788).

The candidate on `gate-tick-load` keeps the same two authority reads, waits 20 s, and requires at least 18 s of clock progress at 50–65 Hz. The longer interval reduces the effect of a brief stall and the lower bound gives room for the observed slowdown while still rejecting a persistent half-rate loop. The elapsed-time floor rejects a clock that ticks briefly at a healthy rate and then stops. The gate still requires the cadence check to pass under its existing first-run rule.

Both drivers ran on authority source `a6722d2e66fb50dd378a60da8c482b5591bc0520`, one fresh default-seed world per attempt, in a private Spark SpacetimeDB v2.14.1 container. Quiet runs added no synthetic load. Loaded runs used 16 CPU workers sharing CPUs 0–4 and 10–14 with the scene and server, active throughout the scene. Fresh launches and cleanup ran outside the synthetic load. This exercised CPU contention and the full tick scene. Other parallel gate scenes, shared server pressure and performance acceptance were outside the experiment.

The baseline completed without reproducing the reported failure. All ten loaded candidate
attempts passed the entire tick scene on their first run. Controlled clock rows also exercised
nominal, bounded slowdown, half-rate, doubled, stopped and early-stall cases. The gate self-test
passed, including the assertion that a cadence failure followed by a pass blocks the gate.
