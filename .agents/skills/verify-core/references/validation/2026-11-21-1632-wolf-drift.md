# Mechanics: the wolf check's flee before the attack (`wolf-drift`)

Validated on the dev-box on branch `wolf-drift`, from `origin/main` `e1f380909`, before its commit. Test-only: `living/tools/verify_mechanics.py` (the wolf scene installs the attack loop before placing the wolf) and `features/mechanics.md`. Server: a private `clockworklabs/spacetime:v2.14.1` container, `wf-stdb` on `127.0.0.1:3342` with its own `/wasm` mount, started in its own systemd scope and removed with its volume afterwards. Module: main's, built into `.local/wolf/target`. No mind service, no Neo4j.

## Cause

Placed beside I before its attack loop was installed, the wolf's own ways ran for the gap between the two calls; `stay safe` flees the nearest person within 7 tiles (hunger under 90), so the wolf ran 10 to 16 m off, beyond sight, and its attack never resolved I: no balk. Details in `features/mechanics.md`, "The wolf that fled before it was told to attack".

## Runs

- **Probe** (`.local/wolf/probe.py`, the wolf step repeated with a fresh wolf per trial, 4 default-seed worlds at a time): round `w1`, the driver's order: 3 of 36 without a balk (wolf 11.7-12.1 m off); `w2`, alternating: place first 7 of 24 without a balk, attack loop first 0 of 24 (wolf 1.6 m from I at the balk). One `w1` world's launch failed (`install_script failed: 530 admin only`), not investigated; its probe did not run.
- **The fixed driver** (`verify_mechanics.py --gate-world`, as the scene gate drives it), three rounds of 4 fresh worlds (`gate-wd-a1..a3-{1..4}-mechanics`): 12 of 12 exit 0, 23/23.
- Evidence: `.local/living/verify/wolf-w*-*` and `gate-wd-*`, `.local/wolf/` (probe, round scripts and logs), archived in `~/archive/worktree-evidence-20261121/wolf-drift-local.tar.gz`.
