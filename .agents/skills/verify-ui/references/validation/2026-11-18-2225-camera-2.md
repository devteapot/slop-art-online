Cursor starts with instant turning, zero head/chest/eye lead, full 180° body range and a 90° backpedal threshold. Natural defaults to Chest with 50° head, 40° chest and 12° eye lead. Body follow uses a 45° range, 0.35 s easing time constant, 450°/s drawing cap and a 135° backpedal threshold. Top-down defaults to Cursor and Orbit to Natural until a player picks a preset.

The authority's `look_walk_deg` Rhai law is 75° in `skills.rhai:180`. `motion(a)` at `skills.rhai:373` sets `turn_rate` to 6 rad/s (343.8°/s). `motion.rs:376` applies the angle cap through `walk_facing` and takes the standing turn through `turn_to`. The existing Rust 0.5 rad/s floor in `motion.rs:663` is a minimum. An immediate Cursor turn exceeds the rate cap, and full lateral or backwards facing exceeds the walking angle limit. The local body pose predicts the held look for drawing. The server's row governs gameplay, perception and other clients, and the local drawing returns to that row when the look is released.

The client continues to use the existing rate-limited `human_face` intents and the same subscribed motion rows. The new tune and presentation data live in the client settings, with no added tables, subscriptions or retained authority data.
