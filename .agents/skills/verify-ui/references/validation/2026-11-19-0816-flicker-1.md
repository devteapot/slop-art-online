# Flicker-1 rendering validation

## Setup and measurement

- Baseline `485507207` and fix `c8d5fcad0`. The production change is in `living/game/src/edges.wesl`. It uses a fixed hash of fragment coordinates to stagger fog-bank march samples. The six march taps, fog drift, Shade, film, AA, samplers, and authority code have identical configurations across the production builds.
- Apple silicon Metal, real headless Bevy 0.20.1 clients at 1600×900, with SpacetimeDB 2.14.1 at `http://127.0.0.1:3300`. The user-authorized scratch worlds were `ui-flicker-1-island` (Brackenford) and `ui-flicker-1-cullet`. Scene placement and hours used the existing verify-ui admin setup. The fix itself is client-side and introduces no database operations. [SpacetimeDB subscription documentation](https://spacetimedb.com/docs/clients/subscriptions/).
- Reproduction started at (420.5, 255.5) on the island plaza, camera facing north with the default 58° preset and context framing off. After warm-up each stop had a 3 s stationary interval, 2 s eastward walk, and a 1.5 s interval around a five-notch zoom. Logs and raw recorder frames/binary checks are in `.local/living/verify/` and `.local/flicker-1/`. The recorder requested 20 Hz. GPU readback can drop frames. Cadence, resident motion and the moving sun vary across runs.
- Luma is 8-bit grayscale from the recorder JPEGs. Stationary measurements use mean absolute pixel change on the paving crop x=540–999, y=130–314. During movement, a RANSAC homography from optical-flow tracks registers the crop to the previous frame, then mean absolute change is measured along Canny edges. This includes interpolation residuals, moving shadows and occasional residents entering the crop. The camera transform in the Middle stationary baseline and A/B arms was identical throughout the sampled interval. The MP4s show the full frame.

## Framing the diagnosis

- **Fog-bank finishing pass.** The six-step ray march moved its sample offset every frame using a hash of pixel coordinates plus shader time. This produces high-frequency noise on any surface visible through a bank. The Middle stationary control was 0.4092 luma levels per frame pair. Stable jitter with the fog still visible measured 0.00016 in the probe. Disabling fog banks measured 0.05231. Animated fog drift is independent of the jitter and remains visible. The patch keeps the spatial stagger and removes the temporal term.
- **Mipmaps and filtering.** All inspected environment kits (including 142 shared and 434 Cullet models) have no glTF image entries. Ground and kit detail are built from the painted PNG strips into mipmapped arrays with trilinear filtering and 4× anisotropy, and both use gradient lookups. Turning painted kit detail off measured 0.4030 in the Middle stationary paving crop. The separate generated character result is below.
- **AA.** The production camera has SMAA with MSAA off because the finishing pass reads single-sample depth and normals. At Middle, removing SMAA changed motion-registered walking edges from 1.8096 to 1.9494. This supports retaining SMAA. TAA with the current outline pipeline stopped with a Metal validation error (`skinned_motion_mesh_layout` vs `skinned_mesh_layout`) in `ui/flicker-taa-middle/client.log`. The TAA probe did not produce comparable frames.
- **Shadows.** Bevy 0.20.1 snaps the light-space XY center of each cascade to its texel size in `calculate_cascade`, using a rounded enclosing diameter that stays fixed during fixed-FOV translation. There are three cascades, a first bound of 18 m and a maximum of 90 m. The default PCF is Gaussian, with stable sample taps. Camera zoom changes the projection, and the daylight system rotates the sun as time passes. Turning shadow maps off at Middle measured 0.2051 still and 1.7106 walking, compared with 0.4092 and 1.8096 for the baseline. Shadows affect the signal, but broad flicker persisted with the shadow map off. The fixed-jitter probe retained shadows.
- **Shade and film.** The Shade field uploads when its input changes and samples a filtered mask. Fade mode bypassed the Shade overlay (0.3117 still), and film off removed the screen blend (0.1953 still). Neither produced the same suppression as fixed fog jitter. The film gradient itself has no frame clock. The fog pass uses shader time separately.
- **Coplanar surfaces.** Plaza street and paving are classifications of a shared terrain surface, rather than separately drawn tiles. The A/B kept identical terrain and GLB placement rules while removing the broad static texture signal. This rules out overlapping surfaces as its source on the measured paving. It does not establish the absence of local depth conflicts in every asset or fixture.

## Character textures

- The controlled asset is the composed `youth-f-c` player (look `lk_0122a44543fe984b`), including the `long` hair and blue `smock_58-f` outfit. The skin/base atlas has 11 levels at 1024². The face-shadow KTX2 has 10 at 512². The hair `strands.ktx2` has 10 at 256×512. The sampled outfit and the 28 generated outfit GLBs in this bundle have no image texture entries. The KTX2 inventory and sample glTF references are in `asset-inventory.json` and `character-sampler-inventory.json`.
- The generated glTF atlas and hair base-color samplers request linear magnification and linear mipmap interpolation. Bevy's glTF and default image samplers have `anisotropy_clamp = 1` in the baseline. The face-shadow shader explicitly samples LOD 0. The numerator and seven contrast taps used by the hair highlight also explicitly sample LOD 0. Those two shader lookups bypass the supplied mip chains in the baseline and fix. The diagnostic 4× test changes only the glTF image sampler for the experiment.
- At 1600×900 the steep top-down view exposes only a small skin patch, especially at Far. Character crops contain animation and lighting changes, so the measured pixel changes cannot be attributed to individual texture reads. Holding fog jitter steady in both arms and forcing 4× anisotropy on glTF images provided no consistent benefit across these three stops. The fix did not change character texture sampling.
