# heed-client-2: throng colours, per-kind counts, centroids and `pin_at` throng-card clicks

**Branch** `heed-client-2` from `origin/main` `a2daf9842` (follows `2026-11-26-2011-heed-client.md`). Laptop, shared SpacetimeDB `v2.14.1` at `127.0.0.1:3300`, release client built in `$HOME/.cache/heed-client-target` via `build-slot` (`BUILD_SLOTS=1`), assets linked from the main checkout.

**What changed** (game client only; [features/heed.md](../../features/heed.md), docs/ATTENTION_BUDGET.md "Built: the client side"):
- **Throng-card click → `pin_at`** (`heed.rs`, `throng.rs`, `combat.rs`, `live.rs`): removed the first pass's `in_throng` stand-in. Clicking a throng card (left-tap when no perceived individual is under the cursor, suppressed from striking/drawing in `combat.rs`, or pressing T on a throng card) sends `human_heed` with `pin_at` = the click's ground point (map m, clamped to the card's square), keeping current pins and focus. On acceptance, `heed::find_picked` resolves the newly admitted creature in `perceived`, adds it to `pins` and `sender.acked`, and displays its pin ring through the normal perceived path. Added `heed:at:X,Y` live-script probe for testing refused `pin_at`.
- **Cloth colours and swatches** (`throng::swatches_of`, `throng::tops_with`, `throng::tops`): derives one RGB swatch per `living_rules::look::CLOTH` word lazily as the mean of `assets/characters/looks.json`'s top-garment outfit RGBs for lines naming that word (matching the named garment stem so aprons never contaminate smocks). Words absent from the book (`grey`, 0 lines) log once (`throng: cloth word "grey" has no outfit in the look book; using neutral tint`) and stay neutral. Person cards take `colour_a/b/c` in proportion to `colour_a/b/c_count` (the rest neutral), with skin and dark trousers; animal cards never take cloth colours.
- **Per-kind counts** (`throng::card_kinds`): apportions cards by `people`, `wolves`, `deer`, `others` instead of round-robin over the `kinds` bitmask, and updates `Mesh3d` when a card slot changes between person and animal.
- **Centroid placement** (`Row::centre`, `throng::places`): centres each square's cards on `(cell corner + centre_x/centre_y + 0.5 m)` within `spread`, compressed per side inside the square (a lone card stands directly on the centroid).
