**What changed** (game client and `verify-ui` script only; authority untouched; [features/heed.md](../../features/heed.md), docs/ATTENTION_BUDGET.md "Built: the client side"):
- **Opaque steady-state cards & mound render-order fix (`living/game/src/throng.rs`):** `Card` tracks `masked: bool`; steady-state cards (`alpha >= 0.99 && target >= 0.99`) render opaque via `AlphaMode::Mask(0.5)` (writing depth in `AlphaMask3d` before `bevy_mod_outline`'s `ExtrudeReal` pass, eliminating both translucent ghosting and remains mounds' hollow black outlines drawing over cards), switching to `AlphaMode::Blend` only during fade-in / fade-out transitions.
- **Crisp dark-outlined silhouettes & fighting poses (`living/game/src/throng.rs`):** Person card texture upgraded to `72 × 144` (`CARD = (1.15, 1.85)`, hit-test `PICK_W = 0.85`) with a `0.028 m` dark ink outline (`#2b1c1a`, matching `Style::default().outline_color`). `Pose::Fight` uses a braced lunge stance with a thicker wooden spear shaft and bright steel spearhead.
- **Principled look-book swatch calibration (`throng::swatches_of`, `throng::calibrate_swatch`):** `assets/characters/looks.json` stays the sole colour source (missing words such as `grey` stay `None`). Aggregates per `CLOTH` word in HSV (saturation-weighted circular mean hue, mean S and V), multiplies linear value by the toon shader's noon lit-band gain (`TOON_SUN_GAIN ≈ 2.3536`, matching `toon_light.wesl` `sun_color()`), and applies a smoothstepped chromatic lift (`smoothstep(0.14, 0.22, s)`) keeping neutrals (`black`, `tan`, `cream`) faithful while separating chromatic garments cleanly from grey stone.
- **Minimum card separation, mound avoidance & bolder ground rings (`living/game/src/throng.rs`):** `places()` packs cards with `0.85 m` pairwise repulsion (clamped inside the square) and `avoid_obstacles()` nudges card targets clear of `"remains"` structures (`MOUND_CLEAR_M = 0.62 m`, clamped inside the square). Pin (`r = 0.78`) and focus (`r = 0.95`, amber) ground rings add crisp dark inner/outer ink rims and sit at `ground + 0.08 m` (`depth_bias: 80.0`).
- **`F Talk` prompt range & viewport clamping (`living/game/src/ui/prompt.rs`):** Suppresses `F Talk | hold F · more` for distant (`> 8.0 m`) or unhovered (`> 3.5 m`) bystanders and flips/clamps the pill cleanly inside viewport bounds (`8 px` margin) clear of notes via `place_prompt`.
- **Compact pin HUD & immediate label refresh (`living/game/src/ui/heed.rs`, `living/game/src/looks.rs`):** Splits `Strip` into `StripText` (`Looks::short` labels) and a `LineBreak::NoWrap` `StripHint` (`T pin or unpin · Y clear`) so key hints never wrap mid-phrase, and bypasses `looks::update`'s 0.25 s throttle for newly perceived creatures lacking a label or receiving a new name/look key.
- **`verify-ui` check tolerances (`.agents/skills/verify-ui/scripts/check.py`):** Updated `heed-pins` `throng_setup` to hold B, C, D at 3.5 m (3.8 s wait) so `heed_ceiling = 2` leaves a slot open for `pin_at`, and updated `squares_ok` `reach` (`0.55 * sqrt(n) + 0.70`, `0.70` for `n == 1`) to match the new minimum card separation and `0.62 m` mound clearance.
