# bond-buttons: Accept first on a courting card, Withdraw on one's own

**Branch** `bond-buttons`, on `origin/bond-decline` (`a02096010`, the Spark's rules branch, not yet on main). It follows the [take-up record](2026-11-27-1320-parity-kin-take-up.md). Laptop, shared SpacetimeDB `v2.14.1` at `127.0.0.1:3300`. The release client was built in `$HOME/.cache/bond-buttons-target` through `build-slot` (`BUILD_SLOTS=1`). The authority module was built from this worktree into `.local/living/verify/ui-bb-1127-1611/target` and published by `verify.py launch` (commit `a02096010` plus this branch's uncommitted `living/` changes).

**Why.** bond-decline puts `decline` on `bond` and on the new `own_bond`. Because `decline_spec` comes before `conceive_spec`, the incoming card read `[Decline, Conceive]`, with Decline in the primary slot. One's own card had no button.

**What changed.**
- `living_rules::ui`:
  - `on_place(specs, place)` orders a place's buttons. Skills with a declared `order` come first, by it; the rest keep the script's order. `order` was already "position within its place or list" (docs/INTERFACE.md); until now only the bar read it.
  - A new `_ui()` trait, `labels` (appended to `UI_TRAITS` and to its golden list), maps a place the skill is `on` to its label there; `UiSpec::label_at(place)` reads it.
  - The reader refuses a place the skill is not on, an unknown place, an empty label, a non-map, and `labels` on a way.
- `skills.rhai`:
  - `conceive_ui` adds `order: 0, labels: #{ bond: "Accept" }`;
  - `decline_ui` adds `labels: #{ own_bond: "Withdraw" }`.
- `ui/kin.rs`:
  - `card_acts(sc, place)` builds both cards: `bond_acts` for an offer to me, `own_bond_acts` for my own;
  - each button sends `act_node(skill, ActTarget::Bond { other, bond: offer id })` through `send_node`, so the outcome or refusal comes back as a note, as on a trade offer card;
  - the dump has `kin.own_acts`.
- `ui/people.rs`: `offer_acts` uses `on_place` and `label_at(On::Offer)`. The trade cards declare neither field, so they are unchanged (Accept, Decline).
- `check.py kin`, `features/kin.md`, `features/README.md`, `SKILL.md` and `docs/INTERFACE.md` are updated.

**Presentation only.**
- The specs' order (`ui_specs`, which the rules and `det` follow) is untouched; only the client sorts a place's buttons.
- The authority reads only `label` from `_ui()` (`perception.rs`, `heed.rs`), and `label` is unchanged: "Conceive" and "Decline". The new rules test checks this.
- `ui::tests::the_rules_are_the_same_without_any_declaration` passes with the new declarations in the shipped script.
- The world installed the changed script (install refuses any `ui_errors`).
- No det comparison was run.

## Findings for the rules (not changed here)

- Withdrawing needs the addressee in sight. `human_act`'s target `{"id": N}` resolves only someone perceived, so the act is refused before `decline`'s rules run. A player can withdraw an offer only to someone they can see.
- The refusal's words name an id ("no #5 in sight").
- An act's outcome line is worded by `label`, so a withdrawal reads "Decline › person (…)".
