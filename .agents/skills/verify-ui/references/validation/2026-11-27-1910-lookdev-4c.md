# lookdev-4c: the render-scale composite in gamma 2, with an anti-ringing clamp

**Branch** `lookdev-4c`, from `origin/lookdev-4b` `294633a78`. Laptop, shared SpacetimeDB `2.14.1` at `127.0.0.1:3300`. The release client was built in `$HOME/.cache/lookdev4-target` through `build-slot` (`BUILD_SLOTS=1`), with the pinned render set `~/slop-assets/a7c606d204bb` linked into the checkout's assets.

**What changed.** Only `upscale.wesl`, the `Upscale` resource in `graphics.rs`, `check.py` and the feature map. `edges.wesl`, the shadows, the Auto cap, `living/scripts`, `living/authority`, `living/rules` and `living/bindings` are untouched. The details are in [features/graphics.md](../../features/graphics.md).

**The shader.** Below 100%, the composite filters in gamma 2: the square root of linear, squared back at the end.
- **Order:** Catmull-Rom first, then CAS at full strength, then a clamp to the min and max of the 2x2 nearest source texels.
- **Fetches:** three gathers give the 2x2 exactly. The four ring taps serve both Catmull-Rom and CAS, so a pixel takes 7 fetches, against 4b's 9.
- **Dump:** `extra.upscale.method` is now `catmull-rom+cas-clamped` (4b: `catmull-rom+cas`) and `sharpness` is 1.0.
- **A/B filters:** `LIVING_GAME_PERF=upscale=clamped|clamped-cas|bilinear`.
