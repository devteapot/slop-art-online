## The change

COMMUNICATION.md §21 (reply-timing) says a reply is now said as soon as it is ready, so its `at_ms` can fall inside the time the answered line takes to say. The client now delays a reply's bubble until the answered line has been said: that line's showing plus `living_rules::saying::saying_ms`. This is a new shared rule: 330 ms a word, 1.5 s minimum, 8 s maximum, the same as the mind's private `talk.rs` `saying_ms`, which was left unchanged because the mind is out of scope. The answered line's bubble, the player's own included, ends in the same frame. The doc's paced time is used rather than the answered bubble's own end (`bubble_life`), which is longer.

The answered line is inferred, because the rows carry no reply-to link (`heard::paced`). It is the most recent line before the reply from the person the reply is addressed to, to the replier, with no line from the replier to that person in between.

- **Unpaced lines:** room lines, the player's own lines, and lines to someone who said nothing to the speaker show at their `at_ms`.
- **The Log and the person window:** they show every line at once, in `at_ms` order.
- **The mouth:** the speaker's mouth starts with the bubble.
