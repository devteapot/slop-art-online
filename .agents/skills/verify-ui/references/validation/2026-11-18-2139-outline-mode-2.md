# Flat head-plane outline experiment

**Result.** The crate supports the plane override. On the installed composed meshes, moving the flat plane to the posed head did not give mode 1 crown coverage and introduced overlap marks. This branch records the experiment. Client rendering stays at the stock baseline.

Run `ui-outline-plane-223137` used the tracked island seed, scoped SpacetimeDB 2.14.1 and the installed character assets from the main checkout linked read-only. The stock client is `cd2afe42ef52bb66db2caaa3922aea7ff1894f1a` and the real-depth reference is `757bda4dac0308bd4d5553f91e8a008b17888f7f`. The prototype was built from stock through the one-slot build wrapper using the existing private target directory. The trial used no mind service or model calls.

## How the plane works

The pinned `bevy_mod_outline` 0.14.0 exposes `OutlinePlaneDepth.model_plane_origin` and `model_plane_offset`. Its `computed.rs` transforms the origin into world space. In `outline.wesl`, `FLAT_DEPTH` changes the fragment depth to that of the plane while rasterizing the posed, skinned geometry. Both stencil and volume use the same flat plane. The stencil pass writes into a cleared depth attachment ahead of the outline volume pass, which uses a strict greater comparison in reverse-Z. The API does not give one primitive independent stencil and shell origins.

The prototype located `J_Bip_C_Head` once for each posed far primitive. After transform propagation, it mapped the bone's world position into the primitive's model space and assigned it as the plane origin with zero offset. Stock logical widths and ink were used. One arm changed both `FarHair` and `FarSkin`. The other moved `FarHair` only. `FarSkin` includes more geometry than the head.

## Stencil isolation and stills

- The ground-only toggle did not uncover extra crown coverage consistently and left the chest marks intact. Ground did not account for the missing coverage in this fixture.
- The body-only toggle disabled the stencil for `FarSkin` and the worn far body pieces while leaving the volumes and hair stencil active. Much of the lower face and outfit filled with ink. These body stencils mask the shell over the visible body, and switching them off did not rescue crown coverage.
- The shared head-plane arm reduced the real-depth interior patches on the tested braid and tousled fringe but added a dark collar and braid contour over the chest. Dark outlines at the hands and sleeves were still visible during turns.
- The hair-only arm kept the skin shell flat at its original origin and removed the conspicuous hand outlines in the sampled turns. It allowed fringe and braid marks to cross the face, and the braid still drew through the chest. At Middle and Far these marks remained visible on the crops.
- The verify-ui `camera-faces` near-LOD control passed 3/3 in both clients on `ui-outline-plane-control-223448`. The full-resolution Near still shows the stock face and neck rendering with the prototype active.
