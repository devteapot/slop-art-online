# battle-spikes: a kept body comes back with the character's state now

Follows [2026-11-27-2011-battle-spikes.md](2026-11-27-2011-battle-spikes.md), which left out a scene that takes one person out of sight and back.

**Branch** `battle-spikes`, on `268bdb239`. Laptop (Apple M4 Pro). Runs used the shared SpacetimeDB `2.14.1` at `127.0.0.1:3300` on scratch worlds, each deleted afterwards. The release client was built in `$HOME/.cache/battle-spikes-target` through `build-slot` (`BUILD_SLOTS=1`), with the pinned render set `~/slop-assets/a7c606d204bb` linked into the checkout's assets. Drives used private copies of the binary. Evidence is in `/Users/carlid/dev/slop-art-online/.local/living/battle-spikes/laptop/body-return/`.

**What changed** (game client and verify-ui only; `living/authority`, `living/rules`, `living/scripts` and `living/bindings` are untouched):

- `live.rs`:
  - A body shown again from a kept one runs its ordinary frame at once: place, facing, standing loop, one-shot, fade target and seat. Before, it was placed and left with a default `Motion` until the next frame.
  - `take_kept` also compares dress (the projection's folk and style, which a VRoid recipe takes without a key).
  - A body that died in sight is not kept.
  - A live script or `diag` logs `bodies: #ID shown again …` and `bodies: #ID drawn anew: …`.
- `anim::afresh`, on a body shown again:
  - its clips stop and its `Anim` goes, so `build` rebuilds it from the current `Motion` the same frame;
  - `ik::Legs::let_go` releases its feet's holds;
  - `characters::Springs::restart` restarts its chains from rest.
- The UI dump has `bodies` (each drawn body's entity, standing loop, one-shot, `Anim` standing loops and weights, seat, fade, and the kept bodies) and `plates` (each plate's health band). The feet log has `stand`, `stands`, `fade` and `entity`.
- `check.py body-return` (new, explicit) and [features/body-return.md](../../features/body-return.md).

## Per state

| State | Finding | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gear: weapon drawn or sheathed, items held | Not drawn on any body. `weapon.rs` is the player's own client state; inventory is not drawn | Nothing to refresh |
| Wounds, health | The overhead plate's band, read by id each frame (`ui/live.rs` `band`) | Per-frame; checked (A's plate 0.30 after the wound in both arms) |
| Pose: sitting or standing | `Motion::stand` is set each frame, but the `Anim` kept its old standing loops. It blended from them for `STAND_FADE` (0.22 s) while the body faded in | Fixed by `afresh` |
| Activity: one-shot or loop clip | Same as pose: `Anim::onces` and `spent` were kept | Fixed by `afresh` |
| Seat | The anchor was removed on reuse already. `Shown::new` starts unseated and the frame sets it again | Per-frame, plus the removal |
| Death | A corpse leaving sight was kept with its held `death` pose | Fixed: not kept |
| Look | `take_kept` checked the key and stage | Dress added |
| Feet, cloth | IK locks are world positions; springs kept their rotations | Fixed: released and restarted |
