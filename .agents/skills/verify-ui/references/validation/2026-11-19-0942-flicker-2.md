## Method

- **Fixed view (offline):** look-dev island tour (`LIVING_GAME_LOOKDEV=1`) with the camera posed by `LIVING_GAME_SEQ_VIEW`:
  - Far `0:58:22.40:46`, Middle `0:58:14.15:44`, Near `0:58:9.43:42`;
  - 40 native PNGs 0.05 s apart, 5 s after the cover lifts;
  - clear weather, hour 10;
  - clock stopped (`LIVING_GAME_DAY_MINUTES=0`) or a 12-minute day.

  The metric is mean absolute 8-bit luma change per frame pair (`seqstat.py`). A per-frame camera and sun log (`camlog`, probe only) confirmed the camera transform was bit-identical through every sequence.
- **Live:** flicker-1's drive (`sample.sh`, `temporal.py`), unchanged apart from paths. Each stop has 3 s still, 2 s walking east and a 5-notch zoom, recorded at 20 Hz to JPEG. Still uses the mean absolute change on the paving crop x=540–999, y=130–314. Walking and zooming use RANSAC-registered Canny-edge residuals. A wider scene crop (x=200–1399, y=170–729) includes residents. Two repeats per arm and stop.
- **Hair:** asset view (`LIVING_GAME_VIEW=look:<key>`) of four decor lines (`bob_parted`, `tousled.3`, `braid.2`, `long.3`):
  - 58° pitch, yaw 150, zoom 4.5/2.9/1.9 (a person's on-screen height at Far/Middle/Near);
  - 16 consecutive 60 Hz frames of `walk`, so the poses are identical in both arms;
  - a close-up at zoom 0.6.

  Also the fixed-view tour's residents with the camera still.

## Causes and changes

1. **Finishing pass unordered against bloom (the main cause).**
   - **Cause:** `post.rs` added its pass to `Core3dSystems::PostProcess` with no order against Bevy's `bloom`, so the render schedule ran it sometimes before bloom and sometimes after. With the camera, clock and sun bit-identical, the paving crop's mean alternated between two values (178.87 and 178.99) frame to frame. The difference between the two states is about ±1 level over 91% of pixels, embossed along the paving joints, and strongest on lit white surfaces (`seq/flip-diff.png`, `seq/flip-zoom.png`).
   - **Confirmation:** turning off bloom alone took the fixed-view paving change from 0.36–0.47 to 0.008; no other single switch did. Shadows, film, banks, detail, edges, SMAA and sway removed singly did not.
   - **Fix:** the pass is ordered `.before(bloom)` (and so before tonemapping, as Bevy's `FullscreenMaterial` default is).
   - **This explains flicker-1's attributions.** "Shadows off" and "film off" each appeared to halve the shimmer because they changed what the two states differed by. "Kit detail off" did nothing because the flip is not a texture.
   - **Film:** the Soft film has no per-frame term (no grain, no time-seeded dither), so it needed no stable variant and no Settings option; a single frame's look is unchanged.
2. **Sun turning every frame (shadows).**
   - **Cause:** Bevy's `build_directional_light_cascades` builds `world_from_light` from the light's rotation only, so the cascades' texel grid is anchored at the world origin. A sun turning every frame slides that grid across a town at R·ω: about 4 cm a frame at the plaza (R ≈ 420 m) with a 12-minute day, more than one texel of either cascade.
   - **Fix:** `daylight::apply` holds the key light's direction until the sun has moved `world::SHADOWS.sun_step_deg` (0.25°): one shadow-map change every 0.75 s with a 12-minute day, every 3 s with Cullet's 48. The held direction is written every frame, because look-dev's `lookdev::sun` also writes the light, earlier in the frame.
   - **Measured with the bloom fix in place, fixed view, 12-minute day, whole frame:**

     | Stop | Turning every frame | Stepped 0.25° | Frozen sun |
     | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
     | Far | 0.022 | 0.009 | 0.006 |
     | Middle | 0.020 | 0.013 | 0.010 |

     The largest single pair with steps (0.043) is no larger than with continuous turning (0.041). A 0.1°, 0.25° or 0.5° step measured alike.
   - **Cascades:** the shadow numbers moved into one documented `world::SHADOWS`; the split is unchanged (3 cascades, 18 m, 90 m). The top-down stops see ground from about 9 m (Near) to 31 m (Far) along the view: the first cascade covers Near and most of Middle, the second Far. The 90 m reach serves context framing's views toward the horizon.
   - **Not changed:** depth/normal bias, PCF mode and map size. With the sun stepped and the flip gone, the still-camera paving signal is at the noise floor at all three stops, so acne and peter-panning are as before.
3. **Hair strand taps at mip 0 (dev-box's request, lower priority).**
   - **Change:** `strand_light`'s eight taps now use `textureSampleGrad` with the UV's screen derivatives, taken in uniform control flow at the top of the fragment.
   - **Unchanged:** the band's placement and edge (normals, `_RING`), its width, 1× anisotropy, and the face-shadow lookup (still mip 0, as asked).
4. **TAA:** not enabled. flicker-1's Metal validation error is `bevy_mod_outline`'s skinned pipeline using `skinned_mesh_layout` where the motion-vector prepass needs `skinned_motion_mesh_layout`, so a fix means patching that crate. With the flip gone there is no measured need.
