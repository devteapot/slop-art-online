Camera-related `verify-ui` upkeep found a missing input recipe and a stale fixed-bearing description. ## Controls and scope

- Middle drag starts turning after 6 px, at 0.3° per px at Normal speed. A short middle tap snaps north when Middle is free. Orbit retains its middle-tap alignment.
- N snaps Top-down north. The drive rebound the action to Y in Settings › Controls and snapped with Y. Older profiles keep an existing N assignment.
- The pinned `bevy_input` and `bevy_winit` 0.20.1 and `winit` 0.30.13 sources show that macOS delivers AppKit `rotateWithEvent:` as `RotationGesture` with a signed degree delta. The driver sent that Bevy message. Physical trackpad and window mouse delivery require a desktop play-test.
- Settings › Camera saves rotation on/off, Slow/Normal/Fast (0.5×, 1×, 2×) and invert. Disabling rotation resets north. Camera tuning's export includes `bearing` in degrees.
- The existing world-space movement and facing calls use the new display axes and cursor ray. The compass reads the displayed bearing, picking reads the same camera pose and the cutaway uses the eye-to-player segment. Shade samples the existing authority-derived field in world space. The run used the pinned SDK and server 2.14.1 with the usual scoped replication. [SpacetimeDB subscription model](https://spacetimedb.com/docs/clients/subscriptions/).
