**What changed** (game client only; authority, bindings and `net.rs` untouched; [features/heed.md](../../features/heed.md), docs/ATTENTION_BUDGET.md "Built: the client side"):
- **Upright camera-aligned billboards (`living/game/src/throng.rs`):** Replaced radial Y-axis cylindrical yaw (`atan2(eye.x - base.x, eye.z - base.z)`, which leaned flank cards 45–80° sideways under the 58° top-down camera) with camera-aligned upright quads (`cam.pose.rotation()`, feet anchored at `base + cam_up * (half * scale + lift)`) scaled uniformly by `billboard_scale(cam_fwd) = (cos θ + 0.22 sin θ).clamp(0.68, 1.0)`. Updated `card_under` hit-testing to match the drawn upright quad identically.
- **Compact upright fighting pose, slender proportions, softer outline & grounded contact shadows (`living/game/src/throng.rs`):** Narrowed card dimensions to `CARD = (0.88, 1.68)` (`PICK_W = 0.62`, texture `64 × 128`) matching 3D character proportions; redesigned `Pose::Fight` with a narrow athletic stride, tucked arms, and diagonal upward-forward spear + steel tip; softened outline to `OUTLINE_M = 0.015` (78% ink blend); strengthened elliptical dark contact shadows (`alpha = 0.56`, `0.76 × 0.52 m` at `ground + 0.03 m`, `depth_bias: 35.0`).
- **Perceived & pin/focus ring clearance (`living/game/src/throng.rs`):** Extended `throng::draw`'s obstacle avoidance to nudge cards clear of perceived 3D characters (`PERCEIVED_CLEAR_M = 0.80 m`) and pinned/focused rings (`RING_CLEAR_M = 1.10 m`), in addition to remains mounds (`MOUND_CLEAR_M = 0.62 m`), clamped inside each square.
- **Compact 2-row max HUD & stable pinned labels (`living/game/src/ui/heed.rs`, `living/game/src/heed.rs`, `living/game/src/looks.rs`):** Replaced single-flow `Strip` with a 2-row max column (row 1: pins, `max_width: 960 px`; row 2: warm-amber `Focus: …` + non-wrapping key hints), eliminating 3-line wrapping and orphan words (`teal`). Added `Heed::sync_pin_labels` / `Heed::pin_label` (and `Looks::look_key` / `Looks::is_known`) to freeze pinned HUD labels at pin time unless the look key or known name actually changes.
