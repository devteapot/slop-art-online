What it shows:
- **The assets are bounded**: files rise and fall with who is in sight (at most 87–93 against 116–122), GPU textures up to 120 MiB lower.
- **Process memory is not**: round 1 ended 455 MiB lower with freeing, round 2 224 MiB higher. Freed allocations go back to the process's allocator, not the system, and re-reading allocates again; resident and footprint follow the allocator, not the assets.
- **Re-reading is heavy**: on a loop of homes the same files are freed and read again (243 frees of 80 files in 10 minutes). The 2.4 s frame (round 2) came 27 s after the last free with 2 meshes arriving; with the load at 15.8 it is not pinned on freeing.
- **The bound without freeing is the book**: all 887 lines between them name 165 files, so a crowd of any size holds at most those (what the whole-book prewarm measured, about +480 MiB resident over VRoid bases on Cullet).
