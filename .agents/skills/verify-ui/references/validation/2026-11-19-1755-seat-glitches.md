## Causes found

1. **Rest renewal, and the "camera far" rise.** The authority renews a rest by deleting its `doing` row and writing the next one at the resident's next evaluation (`tick.rs` slots, once a second). The client read the gap as getting up (`stand_up_ground`), then replayed `sit_ground_down` from standing, because the new row's `since_ms` was recent.
   - **Main's binary:** #2 rose at 4.33, 27.87 and 36.32 s (8.4 s apart), each followed by a full descent with the hips up to 0.83 m.
   - **The "far" rise:** in verify-sync-match's first run the rise came at 31.61 s, before that run's Far zoom (32.x s). Far/near only switches mesh visibility (`lookdev::far_models`); the animation runs on both. So the rise first blamed on the zoom was this renewal.
   - **The fix:** `town.rs` keeps a still sitter seated across a gap of up to 1.6 s, and continues the sit from the first row's start. Gaps measured with the fix: 0.39–1.05 s across 16 renewals.
2. **Spring pop at far/near.** The far cloth mesh is skinned to the spring joints. `FarCloth` puts 12.3% of its weight on `J_Sec_*` in `smock_58-f.glb`, against 12.2% for `Cloth`.
   - **Before:** resting springs went back to the rigid pose, so the hem popped at near → far. Waking re-settled it 16–35° away, so it popped again at far → near. Main's `springwake` showed 23.9–34.9° at waking.
   - **Held rotations alone:** a diagnostic confirmed the joints kept their rotations exactly while far (0.0°). Re-settling them still moved them 5–27°, and resuming from them unsettled swung hair 17–33° a frame. The seated loop turns the head 15–30° (feet log `head_yaw` 79–114°) while the springs are paused.
   - **The fix:** resting springs keep their drawn rotations. On waking they settle from those rotations, and what is drawn eases from the held pose to the simulation over 0.3 s.
3. **Turn takes while seated.** `live.rs` gave the standing turn target as `heading + turn`, but `turn` is a rate. In this world, 94 of 127 standing bodies had turn ends more than 55° away from that target (`ui/fix/standing-turns.json`). verify-sync-match-r2's feet log shows held #2 playing `turn_left_90` every 1.3 s, standing and seated, lifting its hips from 0.11 to 0.9 m.
   - **The fix:** the target is now `turn × turn_s`. Seated and rising bodies play no turn take and keep their legs' yaw.
   - **Not reproduced:** the loop did not show on #2 in this world's runs. The fix rests on the code and the row values.
