# People drawn from their look (look-1)

A person is drawn from the look the authority projects for them (docs/LOOK.md §5, §7; the authority half is look-1-auth): their row in `seen_characters` carries a line key (`look`, `lk_` and 16 hex digits) at every distance at which they are seen, and the client draws the line it finds under that key in the look book shipped with it (`assets/characters/looks.json`, git-ignored, from char-4's bundle). The line is composed from shared type and style files ([crowd.md](crowd.md) `compose`). Code: `living/game/src/characters/book.rs` (the book), `characters/seen.rs` (the row's column and `world.look_book`, in one place), `live.rs` (`recipe_for`, `swap_look`, `Live::swaps`), `looks.rs` (`clothes`, `place`), `loading.rs` (the prewarm), `island.rs` (`person_recipe`), `view.rs` (`look:<lk_…>`).

## Sub-features

- `composed`: a key the book holds is that line on its type, at the line's own height (`stage_scale` scales only the VRoid fallback). My own character is drawn from my own row, as anyone's; `LIVING_GAME_PLAYER` is look-dev only.
- `labels`: someone drawn from a line is named by its words as `living_rules::look::phrase` says them, the words a mind is told: made out, "a woman in a black smock with an apron and dark trousers, grey-haired"; too far to make out, the Log keeps the projection's far words and plates and hover say "a tall figure in a yellow coat and dark trousers" (no sex, hair or beard). The band is the line's type's. A VRoid base keeps colour words.
- `fallback`: no key, a key the book lacks, a line naming a file not on disk, no book, or composing off (`LIVING_GAME_COMPOSE=0`): today's VRoid recipe from the land seed and the id. In a world with looks each case is one warning line a session; a world made before looks (`world.look_book` empty) falls back quietly, with one line at connect ("this world was made before looks"), and no warning for a missing key. A missing or broken book still warns once when it is read (`book::book`: "looks: <path>: …; people are drawn as VRoid bases"), even in a world made before looks. A world generation the book does not hold (`generation`, `generations`) is one warning at connect.
- `redraw`: a new key (a band change, a `relook`) redraws the person where and as they are. The new body takes the old one's transform and motion; the old stays drawn and moving with it until the new is fitted (`Live::swaps`, `drawn()` gives the old meanwhile), then the two cross-fade (`fade.rs`). The person is never off the screen.
- `chips`: a line's chips are its outermost upper and lower pieces' own colours (`colors.outfit` by piece). Which piece is which comes from each outfit style file's scene extras, `outfit: {piece, slot, layer, trim}`: `upper` and `lower` give a chip, the higher layer worn over the lower (jersey 0, smock or coat 1, apron 3; trousers 0); `feet`, `head` and a `trim` (a smock's bands) give none. A file without them falls back on its name with the same values. The book's `files` lists the same and is only a cross-check.
- `prewarm`: under the loading cover, the fewest book lines that wear every type and style file of the looks perceived when the cover is up (`LookBook::covering`; connected: the keys in `seen_characters`, waiting up to 5 s for the rows, `LOOKS_WAIT_S`, on a world with looks; offline and in look-dev: the book's decor lines), so no composed material or mesh layout is first drawn when someone comes into sight. Not the whole book: every file a prewarm loads stays resident for the session, and the whole book's held 140 MiB more GPU textures (`loading.rs` `prewarm`). `LIVING_GAME_PERF=nolooks` leaves them out, to measure that hitch.
- `decor`: an island's grown (adult) background people (offline and look-dev only: a live island plans none) take a grown line from the book's `decor` by their own seed; giants and small folk keep their random recipe in their settlement's style (`living_rules::look::pool_pick`), never a line a character wears; their preload and their spawn take the one recipe (`person_recipe`).

## How to get to it (user POV)

Connect to a world whose people have looks, with the book on disk: the people in sight are drawn from their keys. There is no control.

## Driving it with drive.py

Preconditions: fresh scratch worlds made by this branch's module (never a world updated in place: a module with look-1-auth's schema change panics one tick after an update over main's), the book from char-4's bundle at `living/game/assets/characters/looks.json` and the bundle's types and styles under `lookdev/`. A Cullet world's genesis writes 64 `look` rows whose keys all resolve in the book (`world.look_book` `c4-aa393793`); since look-1-island the island's seed carries the pool's looks too (docs/LOOK.md §9), so a world without looks is made for `looks-old` (`looks_seed.py $R --old`).

- `check.py --run $R looks` (Cullet, 14 checks): the world's generation is the book's and every row's key is in it; two residents beside me (`sync:scene`, `--place-free`); a test copy of the book with B's line left out (`LIVING_GAME_LOOK_BOOK`); every person drawn carries the key the authority holds for them (owner SQL on `look`), composed exactly when the book holds it; B a VRoid base with one warning; chips by the style files' extras and the extras against the book's `files`; my own look from my own row; the cursor on A, the hovered label equals `phrase` of A's line (check.py's `line_phrases` and `look_phrase`, a copy of `living_rules::look::phrase`), and no plate carries a seed name; a second drive with a book of another generation (one warning at connect); a look-dev island tour (`LIVING_GAME_LOOKDEV=1 LIVING_GAME_TOUR_ONLY=island`, `RUST_LOG=…,living_game::island=debug`) whose background people wear decor lines only.
- `check.py --run $R looks-old` (2 checks): a world without looks (`look_book` "", no rows): everyone a VRoid base without a key, one line at connect, no warning. `looks_seed.py $R --old` writes the island's seed without its `looks` (`.local/living/verify/$R/island-old.json`; `--old cullet` for Cullet's); launch with it fresh (`--seed .local/living/verify/$R/island-old`). On a world with looks the first check fails and says so.
- `check.py --run $R looks-band` (6 checks): a Cullet world at a lab pace, a year every 20 s: `looks_seed.py $R` writes the seed, launch with it fresh (`--seed .local/living/verify/$R/cullet-pace`) and drive at once. Three residents whose bands change in the first minute are held beside me, and 350 dumps 0.2 s apart are taken; each key change seen is the authority's new `look.line`, composed before and after, in view in every dump around it, never more than 60 px from one dump to the next, and logged ("redrawn for a new look").
- The dump's `looks`: each person drawn, its key (empty for none), whether composed, whether a redraw is under way, and its chips.
- Run talk, windows, names, houses and death on the same worlds: labels and plates come from the line's words. `far` and `death` accept the line's words (made out, or the far phrase) as well as the colour words.
- `LIVING_GAME_VIEW=look:<lk_…>` shows one line in the asset view, as the game draws it (stills of a type band's faces).

## Known

- A redraw whose files are already loaded is fitted within a dump or two (under 0.3 s): the cross-fade is short. Judge it in the burst's shots (`s00`…`s42`) whether it reads as a change in place or as pop-in.
- `names` failed once in eight Cullet runs, before the rebase onto look-1-auth (no plate among the names on screen; residents wander under town life), and passed in every run since.
- Brackenford's people have no looks until the island seed carries look data: they stay VRoid bases.
