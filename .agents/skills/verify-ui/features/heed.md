# Heed for players on a connected client: the throng, pins and focus

Since `heed-client` (2026-11-26), `heed-client-2` (2026-11-27) and `heed-histogram` (2026-11-27; [docs/ATTENTION_BUDGET.md](../../../../docs/ATTENTION_BUDGET.md), "Built: the client side"). With the world's `heed_on` and `heed_players` laws both on (both off as shipped), a player perceives as individuals only whom its character heeds; the rest it sees is its **throng**, counted per 6 m cell in the public table `seen_throng` (row-level security `viewer = :sender`, at most 48 rows a viewer). The client draws the throng as simple figures coloured from the look book using each cell's full ten-word cloth histogram, placed around each square's centroid by per-kind counts, and lets the player pin up to four individuals (including by clicking a throng card via `pin_at`) and focus one, through `human_heed`. The authority side is verify-player's [heed.md](../../verify-player/features/heed.md) and verify-client's [perception.md](../../verify-client/features/perception.md).

## How the client takes it

- **The subscription** (`net.rs`): `Feed::throng`, bit 6 of the perception set's key, set by `Delivery::throng` = `heed_on && heed_players` from the compiled script with the `law_override` rows laid over it (`delivery_laws`). On, `Feed::queries` adds `SELECT * FROM seen_throng` after the rest; nothing is replaced. The switch is `perception_law`'s make-before-break, like the other delivery laws (no table to wait for). **Both laws off: the same queries in the same order, the same key and the same log lines as before heed** (`heed_off_leaves_every_set_as_it_was_and_on_adds_only_the_throng`). `net::throng` reads the rows only while the set in place has the table (`PlayerConnection::throng_table`).
- **The figures** (`throng.rs`): per row, `count` slender upright cards (a person card standing, walking or in a compact upright fighting pose with a diagonal steel-tipped spear, or an animal stand-in by `kinds`), with a subtle dark silhouette outline and grounded contact shadow under their feet. Cards orient upright to the camera (`cam.pose.rotation()`) with pitch-aware uniform scaling (`billboard_scale`) so every card stands vertical across the viewport at 3D-character proportions. Placed on a sunflower spiral centred on the row's centroid `(cell corner + centre_x/centre_y + 0.5 m)` within half the `spread`, packed with minimum pairwise separation inside the square and nudged clear of nearby remains mounds (`0.62 m`), perceived 3D characters (`0.80 m`) and pinned/focused rings (`1.10 m`), all inside the square; the `fighting` share fights, then the `moving` share walks (mirrored toward `heading`, stepping along it), the rest stand. Per-kind counts (`people`, `wolves`, `deer`, `others`) apportion the cards across people and animal stand-ins (`throng::card_kinds`). Cards are kept per square (`(cell, size)`): a row changed in place moves its cards (and swaps mesh when a card changes between person and animal), rendering opaque via `AlphaMode::Mask(0.5)` at steady state (writing depth before cel-outline passes) and switching to `AlphaMode::Blend` while fading in or out over the person fade's times (`fade::IN_S`, `OUT_S`), so a person moving between the individuals and the throng crossfades. Settings › Play › The throng (`settings:throng`, `Prefs::throng`, on by default) turns the drawing off.
- **Colours** (`throng::swatches_of`, `throng::calibrate_swatch`, `throng::tops`, `throng::tops_with`): one RGB swatch per `living_rules::look::CLOTH` word is derived lazily from the look book's (`assets/characters/looks.json`) top-garment outfit RGBs for lines naming that word (matching the named garment piece so an apron never shifts a smock's colour), aggregated in HSV (saturation-weighted circular mean hue, mean S and V) and calibrated with the toon shader's noon lit-band linear gain (`~2.35×`) plus a smoothstepped chromatic lift for chromatic words; a word with no outfit in the book (such as `grey`) logs once and stays neutral. Person cards apportion across all ten `CLOTH` words from the cell's `cloth_yellow`..`cloth_grey` histogram (largest-remainder scaling when the histogram sum exceeds the person-card count; only a remainder of person cards with no word left — lookless people — stays neutral; deprecated `colour_a/b/c` are not read), with skin and dark trousers; animal cards never take cloth colours.
- **Pins, `pin_at` and focus** (`heed.rs`): T (`Act::Pin`) on whoever the cursor points at (`pick.rs`) pins or unpins them; Y (`Act::ClearPins`) unpins everyone; a fifth is warned about first and asked for on a second press within 3 s. **Clicking a throng card** (left-tap when no perceived individual is under the cursor, suppressed from striking or drawing in `combat.rs`, or pressing T on a throng card) sends `human_heed` with `pin_at` = the click's ground point (map metres, clamped to the card's square), keeping current pins and focus. On acceptance, the authority admits the nearest throng member in that square to `perceived` and updates or deletes the square's `seen_throng` row in the same transaction; the client resolves the newly perceived member (`heed::find_picked`), adds it to its pins and `acked`, records the square's same-frame count drop (`heed.last_pick_cell`), drops the card nearest the promoted individual (or the deleted square) into a smooth fade-out on that same frame with no pop, and displays its pin ring through the normal perceived path. Focus is whom the open text box speaks to, else the creature my last creature-aimed skill went to (10 s). One call carries the whole choice; calls are coalesced, at most one in flight and 0.3 s apart (the authority takes one per 250 ms), carry only those I perceive now, and are never sent when they change nothing (`heed::Sender`).
- **Refusals** are the authority's words, exactly, in the notes (`verbs.rs` toasts), and in the dump's `heed.refusals`.
- **The strip** (`ui/heed.rs`): compact 2-row max pill at the top left — row 1 names pins (`Keeping track N/4: …`, labels frozen at pin time via `Heed::pin_label` unless look key or known name changes), row 2 shows amber `Focus: …` (when set) alongside the non-wrapping key hint (`T pin or unpin · Y clear`). Ground rings: dark-rimmed green under a pin, larger amber under the focus (`throng.rs` `rings`).
- **Settings rows** for heed (the throng's switch, the Keeping track keys) carry `ui::settings::HeedOnly` and show only while heed applies.
- **The live script:** `heed:ID` asks the authority to pin ID as it is, and `heed:at:X,Y` asks the authority for `pin_at` at `(X, Y)` as it is, past the client's own checks, so checks can see the authority's refusal words.
- **The log:** `rules: heed for players true|false (heed_on and heed_players; …)` when it changes (never while it stays off); `heed: players heed true|false`; `heed: pin #ID (…)`, `unpin`, `cleared every pin`, `throng click at (x, y) …`, `picked #ID (…) from throng at (x, y)`, `focus … → …`, `sent pins […] focus … [pin_at (x, y)]`, `accepted …`, `refused: <words>; …`, `refused (paced): too many inputs; …`; `throng: cloth word "grey" has no outfit in the look book; using neutral tint`; `settings: the throng drawn true|false`.
- **The dump** (`dump:`): `heed` (`on`, `pins`, `focus`, `last_picked`, `last_pick_cell`, `acked`, `pending`, `out_of_sight`, `refusals`, `log`) and `throng` (`on`, `drawn`, `rows`, `count`, `cards`, `leaving`, `tint`, `under`, and per square its row's fields with `by_kind`, `cloths`, `centre`, `centroid`, `mean_card`, `sample_card`, `card_kinds`, `rgb`, `cards`, `stand`, `walk`, `fight`, `people`, `animals`, `inside`, `tinted`); both null while heed is off.

## Drive

On an island world (`verify.py launch --seed island`), all explicit. `heed-throng` and `heed-pins` set noon for their scenes (the island's night sight is short) and put the world's epoch back after. Run them after the ordinary scenarios: `heed-throng` leaves 200 fighters and their remains by the arrival.

```sh
S=.agents/skills/verify-ui/scripts
$S/check.py --run "$R" heed-throng    # a 200-fighter battle, laws off then on (colours & centroids) then heed_players off
$S/check.py --run "$R" heed-pins      # five residents: four pins, 5th refused, unpin, focus, clear, throng-card click (pin_at), refused pin_at, mixed people+wolves cell
$S/check.py --run "$R" heed-refusal   # an id not perceived, and one nobody has: the authority's words
```

- **`heed-throng`**: `spawn_battle_sides(200, 2)` (admin) and the player put 9 m south of the fighters' centroid; laws off: no `throng`/`heed` in the dump, the first set without `seen_throng`, no heed rows in Settings; both laws on (`set_world_law`, admin): the set subscribed again with one query more and switched to, then at the play camera and zoomed out (`notches:-12`) every square drawn as its row says (cards, poses, look-book cloth tints in proportion to the 10-word `cloth_*` histogram including >3 distinct words in dense cells, centroids, inside) and the client's rows the authority's (owner SQL `seen_throng WHERE viewer = 0x<identity>` right before and after the dump, including `cloth_yellow`..`cloth_grey`); Settings › The throng off and on; `heed_players` off: no rows of mine on the authority, no throng in the dump, the set the first one again. Shots `off`, `on`, `far`, `hidden`, `after`.
- **`heed-pins`**: the player moved to a quiet open spot (`UNHEARD_SPOT`: a fight nearby would take its places, so the residents would not all be individuals), five residents held 1.6 m round it (`place_at`, `set_behavior`); T on four: the client's pins and the authority's `heed_choice` row hold the four; T on a fifth: the warning note, nothing sent; T again: refused with `You can keep at most 4 others pinned at once.` in the notes, the row still the four; T on a pinned one unpins; the box opened on one: focus on them in the row; closed: no focus; Y: no pins. Then `heed_ceiling` lowered to 2 and resident E placed 6.5 m east in cell `(51, 55)`: E enters `seen_throng`, left-clicking (`tap`) E's throng card sends `pin_at`, promoting E into `perceived`, removing E from its throng cell in the same update (`last_pick_cell` `{cell: [51, 55], before: 1, after: 0}`, square gone on client and authority), and pinning E here and in `heed_choice`; `heed:at:` at an empty point is refused with `There is no one in the throng there to pick out.` while keeping E's pin; finally, 3 residents and 2 wolves placed in that cell verify per-kind card counts (`people: 3, wolf: 2`) and that only people take cloth tints. Every `heed: sent` at least 250 ms after the one before, none paced. Shots `five`, `focus`, `pin_at`, `mixed`.
- **`heed-refusal`**: a resident put 80 m off; `heed:ID` and then an id nobody has: both refused with `You can only keep track of someone you can see now.`; the row names neither. Shot `refusal`.

Each restores the heed laws (`heed_on`, `heed_players`, `heed_ceiling`) to the world's own values (`law_values.own_laws`, else the shipped ones), and the borrowed residents/wolves.

## Not covered

- `You are already keeping track of as many as you can.` (`pin_at` when heed has no place for the picked member, e.g. capacity 0 or all places held by higher-ranked threats): covered on the authority by verify-player `heed.py` and in client refusal handling by unit tests (`heed::tests`).
- Frame time with the throng drawn, a real window and the owner's eye: a rendering branch's dev-box render pass, and a play-test.
