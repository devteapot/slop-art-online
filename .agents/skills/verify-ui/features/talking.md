# Talking

Enter talks to the person under the cursor (the owner's rule, 2026-11-11), and with nobody under the cursor to the room (`to = 0`). The box lives in their window ("To a stranger in orange"), or in Log and people ("To the room"). Tab goes to the next person within earshot, then back to the room. About 10 s after a line to the room that nobody took up, a note says so ("Heard by 2 — no one took it up. …", or "No one in earshot heard you. …" when no one I perceive was in earshot). A line nobody could hear (speech-truth, the `speech_truth` law: a `silence` row and an act outcome with code `reach`) gets the authority's words once, as a quiet note ("A stranger is too far away: they could not have heard you.", "… is out of earshot: …", for the room "Nobody was near enough to hear you.", which replaces the client's own "No one in earshot heard you" note); the Log does not repeat them, and my line in the window is marked "addressed …, not heard by them". Each person has a window keyed by character id: how I name them and how they look, how we met, acts (Give ›, Trade…, Wave, Look over, Introduce yourself) and our conversation from experience rows. Strangers are named by looks only; a name shows only when the projection gives one.

## Sub-features

- `room`: Enter with nobody under the cursor: to the room, in Log and people; the line goes out with `to = 0`; the room note after 10 s.
- `address`: Enter while hovering someone: to them, in their window; the line goes out with `to`.
- `pick`: who is under the cursor is one pick (`living/game/src/pick.rs`) for the nameplate (hover), Enter, a tap and combat's target: the body whose outline on screen holds the cursor, the one drawn in front where two overlap; with no outline under it, the body within 1.2 m of the cursor's ground point. Before 2026-11-11 each asked its own way, and with two people overlapping Enter spoke to the one behind.
- `tab`: Tab cycles people in earshot, then the room. The client asks who is in earshot every 0.25 s, so each press goes by the list of that moment: the dump's `typing.tab` is the last press's `prev`, `earshot` and `chose`.
- `unheard`: a line nobody could hear: the authority's words once as a note, not in the Log, no client room note; none for a heard line (`check.py unheard`).
- `room-detail`: how a line to the room was said (speech-address: "to the room, naming you and a stranger", "… while talking with Lorcan"; the way's tail, "from about 12 m", when it has one) shows beside the speaker in the Log and their window ("A stranger (to the room, naming you and a stranger)"), in the player's own labels; bubbles keep the words only; a line to someone gets none (`heard::how`: whatever follows "to the room" up to the words, so "calling" and "naming" read alike). `check.py room-detail`: a fresh "Listener", residents A and B held beside it, A says to the room "Come and look, <B> and Listener!" then a line to the player (admin `mind_say`); the dump's `extra.lines` (speaker, to, how, text) and `bubbles` (speaker, text). A line to someone that names others (talk-turns; kin words count) carries the detail after the addressee in the authority's row ("Sissly Fenner said to a stranger, naming you: “…”"): `heard::detail_to` keeps it from the first comma (labels have none; any verb or way), and the Log and the speaker's window show "Sissly Fenner (to a stranger, naming you)", the addressee in the player's own label (`Looks`). The scenario then has A say to B "Ask Listener, she will know." and checks the row, `how` ", naming you", the heads the window and the Log show (`extra.lines`' `head_window` and `head`, from `people::line_head`), and the bubble.
- `bubble-once`: a line is shown once per speech row: each speaker's newest line only, within its life (`ui/live.rs` `bubble_picks`); an older line never comes back when a newer one's bubble ends (play-test 6: Sissly's long reply re-showed 7.6 s after it was said, once her short line 2.1 s later had gone). `check.py bubble-once`: a resident beside a fresh player says a long line (10 s) then a short one 2.1 s later (`mind_say`); the dump's `bubbles` over them: long, short, short, then none at about 8.4 s and 9.9 s, within the long line's own life.
- `earshot`: the authority's earshot (the `earshot` law, off by default; `my_earshot`, rewritten at most four times a second): with a row, Tab's order and the box's "(out of earshot)" come from its `say` list, and its sentence (since earshot-short "Within earshot: Ife Okoro, a stranger and 4 others; a call would also reach 3 more", in the player's own labels, people one sees only; every id stays in the lists) takes the box's hint line while the box is open; with none, the client's own reckoning as before. The box cannot switch to whisper or call (no place for them yet): their lists are read, not used. `check.py earshot`: at noon (sight 11 m by day, 6 m by night: the lists hold only people one sees) on open grass at (300, 330), anyone within 25 m put away first (offline player characters of earlier drives stand at the arrival), residents at 2 m and 6 m, the law on (`set_world_law`), then B at 10 m (out of a say's 9 m, within a call's reach and sight; at 12 m B was out of sight and in no list), then the law off; the dump's `typing.told` (text, ways). `check.py names` turns the law on, so the sentence is scanned for names too.
- `ways`: the world's ways of speaking (`speech_card`: label, order, `needs_to`, `reach_m`, `carry_m`, `max_chars`, `gap_ms`) in the talk box: a small chip shows the box's way (the first by order until another is chosen); a click on it or the Way key (Up; Settings › Controls › "Change how you speak") moves to the next; a line goes by `human_speak(text, to, way)`; Tab follows that way's reach (the authority's list for it with the `earshot` law, else its carry by the rules, `WaySpec::carry_for`, capped by `reach_m`); the box holds no more than `max_chars` (cut each frame, scripted words too); the chip greys while `gap_ms` runs; what the rules refuse ("a whisper needs someone to whisper to") comes back once as a note in their words. No way is named in the client (`ways()` sorts the cards; the rules' ratchet test). `check.py ways`: A at 2 m, B at 20 m; say reaches A not B; the close way (needs someone within a reach) to no one refused once; the far way (the widest carry) reaches B; a 131-character line cut to its `max_chars`; Settings offers `bind:Way:`. The dump's `typing.way` (name, label, max_chars, gap_ms, needs_to).
- `far`: someone too far away to make out (far-label: beyond the world's recognising range, Cullet's `known_within` 24 m; the projection's label "someone too far away to make out", no name). The Log, the talk target, act titles and notes keep those words; the plate, hover and target plate say "someone in blue" from the colours drawn for a VRoid base, and the line's far phrase ("a tall figure in a green smock") for someone drawn from a look book line (`Looks::plate`; plates show for the hovered or targeted out to 40 m). Presentation only: speech, addressing, acts and offers carry my words and ids. `check.py far` installs verify-client far_label.py's overlay (far beyond 4 m) for the scene and the shipped script after: close (2 m) "a stranger in …", far (6 m) "someone in …" with the talk target "To someone too far away to make out" and the line sent with my words and their id, close again "a stranger in …".
- `notes-and-prompt`: notes keep their lane above the bar; the F prompt ("F Talk · hold F · more") sits right of the head, or left when a note is in the way, or waits while the note shows (the note wins; the prompt returns after). Every scenario that shows notes checks the dump's `rects`: no note and prompt intersect while both show; `unheard` also checks the moment arises (a note while I point at them) and that the prompt is back after the note.
- `modes`: the box's modes from `talk::MODES` (a person, the room; whisper greyed, not built).
- `person-window`: opened by talking, by a tap on them, by F, from the list of acts (Their window) or from Log › People.
- `repeats`: the same words said again in a row by the same speaker to the same one are one bubble with "×N" after the name (`people.rs` `collapse`, unit test `the_same_words_again_are_one_line_with_a_count`; in the person window and the log). In Glass a person's window stops at 42% of the screen's height (`Theme::person_cap`) and its conversation scrolls to its end.
- `conversation`: my `said` lines to them and their `speech` lines; "did not hear you" from a `silence` row.
- `introduce`: "Introduce yourself" puts "I'm <name>." in the box, editable, to them.
- `labels`: someone drawn from a look book line is labelled by the line's words as `living_rules::look::phrase` says them ([looks.md](looks.md)); a VRoid base by colour ("a child in red"; a second colour when two read alike); never a name the rules have not given.
- `pending`: `heard_by` (under my line) and `introduced` ("They told you their name") show nothing until the authority writes them (`living/game/src/heard.rs`).
- `mouth`: while a line is said, the speaker's `Fcl_MTH_A` moves (`living/game/src/mouth.rs`, read by `lookdev::face`): my own line, and every line I hear or see said, for each rigged person drawn. It moves for the line's length at 14 characters a second (open on vowels, shut on punctuation), never past its bubble, then shuts. How wide comes from the world's way of speaking (`Carry::Hearing` share: a call wider, speech 0.8; a way for a few metres or one ear, a whisper and its onlookers' rows, 0.3), found from the row's kind and heard words. Unit tests `the_mouth_over_a_line`, `loudness_comes_from_the_ways`; the dump's `extra.mouths` (character id → opening now) and the record's `state.jsonl` `mouths`.
- `prompt-tip`: the F prompt beside the person ("F Talk · hold F · more") and the first-time tip ("New here").

## Driving it with drive.py

Preconditions: baseline; two residents placed beside the player with `--place-free A:-2.5:1.0 --place-free B:2.0:-1.0` (each on a free walkable tile 1.5–3 m from the player, in sight, across the view; since arrival-open (2026-11-12) the island's arrival point is on open ground, 3 m from any stall, crate or well; before, it was inside a market stall and the stall side was blocked from sight), held still, and put back on those tiles by `--sync hold=refree` at `sync:hold` just before the cursor goes to the first: in the 11 s of the room's note they may walk (town life), once onto one spot, stacked exactly on screen. The scene is checked first: both residents 1.5–3 m from me and in view (the `04p` dump's `seen`), and at least 1.5 m apart in the world and on screen. `place_at` keeps one body per tile (`free_spot`): beside the old arrival stall both landed on one tile; on open ground they stand apart (the exact tie is `pick::tests::two_bodies_on_one_spot_give_one_answer_in_any_order`).

- **The room.** `cursor:0.5,0.12;key:Enter:tap` → dump `typing.to` null, `typing.window == Log`, `typing.label == "To the room"`. `type:Good morning, all.;enter` → log `say: "…" to no one in particular`; after 11 s the log has `talk: room note "Heard by N — no one took it up…"`.
- **A person.** `point:<ID>;key:Enter:tap` (the cursor on them) → `typing.window == Person<ID>`, `typing.label` "To a …" (looks, no name). `type:Hello there.;enter` → log `say: "Hello there." to #<ID>`; owner SQL `SELECT kind, object, text FROM experience WHERE kind = 'said'` has the line with `object` = the addressee (and the room's with 0).
- **Tab.** `key:Enter:tap;tab;tab;tab` → each dump's `typing.tab`: from whom the press went (`prev`), the earshot it went by and whom it chose; `to` is the next after `prev` in that list, the room after the last, the first from the room or from someone gone from the list (the window follows). People walk in and out of earshot between presses at a busy hour; the check goes by each press's own list, and asks for the room within the eight presses only while the list held still.
- **Unheard lines.** `check.py unheard`: at noon (a scratch fixture; sight 11 m by day, 6 m by night) on open grass at (300, 330), anyone within 25 m put away, one resident held 10 m off (seen, out of the 9 m speech carries); a line to them, a room line, then the resident beside me and a heard line. Proof: one `reach` act outcome each for the two (owner SQL), their words once in the notes, not in `extra.events`, no "talk: room note" in client.log, and no reach row or note for the heard line.
- **Esc.** Closes the box and its window.
- **Log › People.** `key:KeyL:tap;click:@log:tab:1;click:@log:person:0` → a `Person<ID>` window.
- **Mouths.** After `type:…;enter`, dumps at 0.3, 0.6 and 0.9 s → `extra.mouths[<me>]` above 0.05 in at least one. `sync:answer` makes resident A answer through admin `mind_say` (A's controller is the admin; scene setup, the speech path minds use, no model) → dumps at 0.3–0.9 s have `extra.mouths[<A>]` above 0.05; 5.5 s later both are 0 or absent.
- **Introduce yourself.** `click:@person:intro` → `typing.words` "I'm Verifier.", `typing.to` = ID.
- Scenario: `check.py --run $R talk`.
- **Two overlapping (`pick`).** The view faces north (`frame:0;yaw:0`); A at `-3.0:-1.0`, B at `-3.0:-2.5` (1.5 m behind A along the view, where the ray through A's chest meets the ground). `point:A:1.3` (A's chest) → the dump's `under` and `hovered` are A only; `key:Enter:tap` → `typing.to == A` in `Person<A>`, `targeted == A`; the line goes to #A (log and `said` row); `tap` on the same spot also opens the box to A. Last, `sync:stack` asks both onto one spot (`place_at` puts the second on a neighbouring free tile, so their outlines overlap on screen); with the cursor on A, dumps `05a`–`05c` 0.5 s apart must give the same `under` every frame, the nearest the camera of those whose outline holds the cursor. Scenario: `check.py --run $R pick`.

## Gotchas

- Face is in no list of a player's (`face_ui() { on: [], tip }`, play-test 5): a player faces where it looks. The talk scenario checks More… leaves it out.

- Someone behind a stall or a house is not perceived (sight is a rule), so Enter skips them; place people in the open.
- `point:ID` puts the cursor 0.3 m above their feet. Someone standing in front of them on screen can cover that spot, and then the pick is rightly the one in front; the dump's `seen` shows where each body was.
- A run's character keeps its old experience rows: "First seen 12 min ago" can predate the run.
- No mind runs: nobody answers on their own. Their lines appear only with `verify-minds` or a play-test; the talk scenario's one answer is the scene's `mind_say`.
- A mouth sampled in a dump can be near 0 at a pause or a flap's trough: the checks take three samples.
