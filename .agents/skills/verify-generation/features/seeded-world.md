# A seed produces its world

## Sub-features

- Realm and valley dimensions, complete 16×16 terrain chunks and valid terrain bytes.
- Towns, open settlements, household backgrounds, membership and named buildings with signs. A town seeded with no people (no `residents`, no `occupations`: Cullet's grey city) has no backgrounds or places to check; its named buildings are checked as a structure of their kind and, on a plan, by their sign at their lot.
- Authored names and counts, sheets with resolved people and linked parents.
- Explicit people, structures, artifacts and communities.
- Wild-site and valley-homestead bands, family counts and camp structures.
- Species counts, resource presence and settlement resource promises within 24 tiles. A seed's `resource_scale` scales each kind's wild spawn (0 turns it off); a plan's authored `resources` are placed before the promises are topped up ([plans](plans.md)).
- Seed packs (`packs`, `seed::packs`): every animal of a named pack placed `near` an animal kind starts within the pack's reach of one (`to_m` plus the spread); on a land world a pack with a `band` starts within it of the arrival point, and one with `road_clear_m` starts that far from any road tile (less the 6 m spread). The island has two: the wolves of the deer meadows (near deer) and the bold wolves of the low hills (near deer, 370–800 m from the arrival, 40 m clear of roads).
- No sheet `secret` field or exact nonempty secret string in exported public tables.
- Land worlds (`map.kind = "land"`, `living/seeds/island.json`): the side from `map.size`, the `land` row (seed, size, digests, walkable arrival point inside a settlement within half its radius of its square: `living_rules::host::arrival`, the first town's square), a `settlement` row per settlement linked to its seeded community and site kind, `lot` rows 0..n with the right structure exactly at every building's door, walkable doors, unwalkable tiles under blocking lots (`blocking lots stand on wall, low or drop tiles`: `Wall` where the lot's model hides at eye height, `Low`, 9, where it is open there or stands below the eye, `LandParams.sight` and the table's measured `eye_cells`, since 2026-11-11; a drop's edge under a part that does not hide stays `Drop`), every tile a class 0..9 (`terrain chunks contain 256 valid tiles`, its detail counting wall, drop, low and rock tiles; 8 is a drop between ground levels), house structures that are lot houses and owned by households, residents' `folk` and `body_scale` (both checked the other way, none, when the seed has no people), and with `rules.minds` false no AI or admin-controlled character. Every seed: its `rules` are kept as the world's own law values, one `world_law` row per entry (`the seed's rules are kept as the world's laws`; since `laws-2`, 2026-11-11, docs/WORLD_RULES.md).
- A land with a city (`map.params.metro`, `living/seeds/floor-megacity.json`, docs/FLOORS.md), published at 2048 m as the derived `floor-megacity-2k` (created inside `init`): one settlement per ward (the core plus every ring band and sector, `1 + rings × arterials`), a ward for every district style the seed names, at least six lots of 100 m or more in the core's district, every other settlement outside the city, and gate tiles where the arterials cross the wall. Town life works round a square whose middle is built on (the core's arcology): residents take the nearest reachable point of its edge.
- The same city at 4096 m as a floor: see [floors](floors.md).
- A hand-authored settlement (`map.params.plans`): its seed town names the plan's id as its `site` and stands on the settlement in the plan's style; its lots, `homes`, lot-bound named buildings and `lot:<id>` places: see [plans](plans.md).
- Every land world's `land` row digests and arrival point equal the same seed regenerated natively by `living/rules/examples/floor_preview.rs` (WASM authority against a native client; the layout digest includes the arrival point, and `floor_preview` prints it as `arrival x y`).
- Town life on a land world with `town_life` and `ways` (docs/TOWN_LIFE.md): residents know the seed's places (every name in `town_life.places`, on the island `square`, `inn`, `berries` and `store`, plus `evening`, as `place` rows, at most 5% missing), a place listed in `town_life.spread` (the island's `berries`) is shared out like work, so at least half its holders have distinct spots, workers have a `work` place on walkable ground in or by their settlement (unless the seed gave that resident its own `work`), and every resident with an occupation starts with the seed's own `common` routines (`routine` rows).
- People's rhythms (every seed; the private `rhythm` table is snapshotted, docs/SEEDED_LIVES.md "Rhythms at any hour"): a person the seed gives no `rhythm` has a drawn one (rising 04:00–09:00, to bed 19:30–02:30, an evening of 1.5–4.5 hours, no working hours, no second sleep), and a seeded one is as given (rising at any hour, `works` and `second_sleep` spans, bed at the given hour or at the 8–22 hour limits). Cullet's 64 residents are all seeded.
- Seeded lives on the island (docs/SEEDED_LIVES.md): hamlets (`settlement.style = hamlet`) laid out, and peopled exactly where the seed has a `site: hamlet_village` entry (chosen by `near` and `region` in `island::match_seed`); every authored sheet relation between two people of the world is a `relation` row; every authored relation across communities is an introduction (`acquaintance`), and none marked `knows_name: false` is; the places an author gave a resident (`residents[].places`) exist as `place` rows, with `<name> 1..n` for a known way, each stage within 220 m of the last (`living_rules::ways`).
- Seeded records (docs/WORLD_RULES.md, since `rules-2`): a seed's `records` list makes `record` and `record_party` rows at genesis, after its communities, with parties by role naming a community or a person. A record naming a party that is not there is skipped and logged. The island has 13 `regard` stance records between its communities (`world.json` `records`, copied by `author_world.py`). Proved on an island run by verify-core `scenes.py dealings`: the records exist, and a relation filter reads one. This driver does not check them yet.

`living/authority/build.rs` selects `seeds/$LIVING_SEED.json` (or none: a floor module). `init` (`living/authority/src/lib.rs`) parses it and calls `floor::begin` (`living/authority/src/floor.rs`), which runs `seed::seed` in the same call when the land is at most 2048 m (`INLINE_MAX_M`), else schedules the staged creation ([floors](floors.md)). `seed::seed` (`living/authority/src/seed.rs`) generates the map (`realm`, `land` through `island::generate`, or the classic valley), applies `city::lay_out` to realm towns, inserts chunks, writes the world row (`world_row`), hosts a land (`island::build`), places wild resources (scaled by `resource_scale`) and calls `populate` for the seed's content, which first places a plan's authored resources (`plan_resources`, kinds the rules know at the nearest walkable tile; others logged and left). `living/rules/src/map.rs` sets the classic valley to 96×96. `living/authority/src/island.rs` hosts a land world: `params_of` reads the side and parameters and refuses bad hand-authored plans (`living_rules::settle::validate_plans`) and bad authored relief (`living_rules::land::validate_relief`: an outline or stamp polygon of fewer than three points, a stamp that sets nothing, an unknown `ground`); `generate` runs `living_rules::land::generate` (all of `land::Gen`'s steps) and `host`, which runs `living_rules::host::hosted_map`, refuses a plan whose doors no one reaches (`settle::stranded_doors`) and plans settlements and lots; `build` writes `land` and `lot` rows with door structures; `settle` writes `settlement` rows and folk backgrounds (see `docs/ISLAND_AUTHORITY.md`), then `town::seat` the residents' places (the seed's `town_life` and each resident's own `places`, a known way's stages along `Hosted::ways`). `seed::settle_authored` writes each sheet's relations as `relation` rows. A land's city comes from `living_rules::settle::metro` (`lay_out`, `ward_lots`, `ward_fill`, `ward_close`, `pave`), driven by `settle::Staged` when `LandParams.metro` is set. Animals go where the seed's `animals.habitat` says (wolves in the megacity's wastes), else forest for wolves and grass for the rest. `SETTLEMENT_REACH` in `seed.rs` sets settlement resource reach to 24 tiles.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Select a seed when building and publish its module. The world exists after `init`; no model service is needed. The browser observer receives the generated public tables.

## Driving it with generation.py

```bash
.agents/skills/verify-generation/scripts/generation.py --run generation-worlds-$(date +%Y%m%d-%H%M%S) --feature worlds
```

The twelve seeds cover drawn towns and wild bands (`realm`), explicit people, structures, artifacts and communities (`valley`), authored households (`authored-test`), authored named buildings and resource promises (`aske-coast`), family bands with camps (`stage3-village`), the km-scale land with its settlements, lots and instinct-only residents (`island`; about 3 s of `init` and 20 MB of SQL snapshots), and the megacity floor at 2048 m (`floor-megacity-2k`, derived from `floor-megacity.json` in the run's directory and published through an absolute `--seed`; about 3.5 s of `init`), the same city at 4096 m as a floor (`floor-megacity-4k`, [floors](floors.md)), a hand-authored harbour town (`plan-test`, [plans](plans.md)), Cullet, a hand-authored city on an authored island with no people yet (`cullet`, [plans](plans.md)), and a seed with its own day and laws, compiled in and given to a floor module (`cullet-laws`, `cullet-laws-floor`, [floors](floors.md)). A second `realm` follows them. Each land child also runs `cargo run --release --example floor_preview` in the run's target dir for the native digests (`native-digests.log`, `native/`). Read each child's `checks.json` and table JSON files. All assertions must pass. The second `realm` also drives repeatability.

## Gotchas

- The seed is compiled into WASM. Changing the JSON without rebuilding changes nothing.
- `init` starts timers. Doctor runs before pausing; count checks include dead founders because a wolf can kill an animal before snapshots; memberships are compared for the living, since an elder founder can die in those seconds and death ends membership.
- Realm towns, villages and bands use `zip` or indexed sites. If a seed describes more settlements than sites, the missing rows fail these checks.
- Drawn `occupations` specify workers. `drawn_households` in `seed.rs` also adds random children. The driver checks worker occupations, child parent links and the resulting total separately.
- Land worlds place no `bands` (their homestead search does not scale to 4.2M tiles); the module logs a warning instead.
- A land over 2048 m is not created in `init` (one reducer's fuel budget is 120 × 10⁹ instructions; the 4096 m megacity needs about 2.9 of them). It is created in steps after publication; the driver drives that through the floor module ([floors](floors.md)).
- Explicit structures move to nearest walkable ground. The bounded position assertion allows three tiles in these fixtures.
- SQL exports are diagnostic snapshots of these small scratch worlds. They are not a routine gameplay data path or a subscription proof.

## The animals' own draws (since `island-pack-check`, 2026-11-13)

`seed::populate` places the animals, then the seed's packs, from a stream of their own: `Creating::start(seed, STEP_ANIMALS)`, after the people.

**Why.** Before, they drew from the population's stream right after every person. A founder draws a knack for every skill its body has (`genes::founder`), so a skill added to the catalog shifted every later draw and moved every herd.

**What it broke.** move-intents (`a07f5919`, five skills) moved the island's deer from 732 m to 915 m from the arrival point at their nearest (later skills moved them again). The low-hills pack must start 370–800 m from the arrival point (its `band`) and within 120 m of a deer, 40 m clear of roads. That had no free spot left. `seed::packs` placed it "by the band alone", 473 m and later 816 m from any deer, so the check failed. A git bisect over main's first parents found the commit: `~/slop-evidence/island-pack-check-20261113.tar.zst`.

**Now.** With their own stream, the island's nearest deer is 434 m from the arrival point, and the pack starts within about 50 m of one. The layout is identical with and without move-intents' skills: on `9a062fc6` with this change, the nearest deer is also 434 m from arrival. A deer's own gene draws still come between placements, so a skill added to the deer's body would move the herds; one added to people's or wolves' would not.

**Gotcha.** A pack with both `near` and `band` can still be infeasible for a seed's layout. When it is, `seed::packs` logs "placed by the band alone" and this check fails. That is a seed-content question (the band or the habitat), not a reason to loosen the check.
