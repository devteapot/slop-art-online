# Hand-authored plans: a settlement laid out as written

## Sub-features

- A land seed's `map.params.plans` (`living_rules::settle::PlanSpec`, docs/AUTHORED_PLANS.md): levels at exact heights, streets, stairs, a square, a harbour (`sea`) and lots with explicit kinds, sizes, models and heights, in the plan's own frame (`origin`, `rotation_deg`).
- The planned settlement is one settlement row in the plan's `style` and `kind`; a seed town takes it by `site: <plan id>` (`Site::id` is the plan's id; `island::match_seed`).
- Its lot rows are the plan's lots in plan order (contiguous ids), each with its kind, `width_m` × `depth_m`, `model` ("" without one), `height_m` (6 for a building without one) and its level's height as `floor_m`; every door is walkable; blocking lots stand on unwalkable tiles like any lot (`Wall` where the model hides at eye height, `Low` where it does not; a lot naming no model in the table, as Cullet's and plan-test's do until Cullet's kit enters it, is `Wall` throughout above the eye).
- `homes` (household key → lot id): that household's members live at the lot's door (`character.home_x/y`) and own its house structure.
- `buildings[].lot`: the named building's sign stands at that lot's door (and its structure is the lot's own when the kinds match).
- `town_life` kinds `lot:<id>` (places and `work`): residents' `place` rows stand at that lot: on one of its spots, else at its door. A spot is stood on as written, so each must be ground one can stand on. Since `layout-2` a court, a plot or a yard is an open lot (`lamp`, no height, no model) with spots anywhere on it; Cullet has 138 open lots.
- `fixtures[]` (since `layout-2`; docs/AUTHORED_PLANS.md, "Fixtures that block"): a line (`points`, `gaps`) or a rectangle (`at`, `size`, `facing`) with a `height_m`. One with `blocks: true` stands on tiles no one walks on: `Wall` (7) where it rises above the eye and is solid, `Low` (9) where it is seen over or through (`eye_solid` under `sight.solid`); a gap's middle tile stays open. A fixture that does not block changes nothing.
- `resources[]` (kind and point in the plan frame, anywhere on the land; optional `max`, `regrow_per_h`, `pool`): a `resource_node` of its kind stands at the nearest walkable tile to its point, holding the plan's `max` and regrowing its `regrow_per_h` turned into real minutes for the world's day (`regen`), else its kind's; the nodes naming one `pool` all carry the first one's id as `pool` (one stock).
- `structures[]` (since `rates-2`; `kind`, then `at` in the plan frame or `lot` with an optional `spot`; `fuel`, `contents`): a `structure` of its kind stands there, lit from creation for `fuel` units at its kind's `burns.rate` a game hour (`lit_until_ms - built_ms`), holding its `contents` (inventory rows owned by `STRUCTURE_BIT | id`). `lots[].contents`: the structure at that lot's door holds them.
- A plan naming a resource kind, structure kind or stored item that the world's tables (its script with the seed's laws) do not have is refused by `floor::check` before anything is written, for `init` and `create_floor` alike (it used to be left unplaced and logged).
- Authored land (`map.params.relief.outline`, `relief.stamps`: a coastline and stamps of height, region kind and ground; docs/AUTHORED_PLANS.md): checked here only through the land digests against `floor_preview` and the refusals; the shape is proved natively (`land::tests::an_authored_outline_and_stamps_shape_the_land`, `settle::plan::tests::the_cullet_seed_is_valid_and_reachable`).
- The `land` row's digests equal a native regeneration (`floor_preview`), as for every land.
- Refusals before anything is written: `create_floor` (and `init`, through `island::params_of`) refuses a plan the generator cannot honour (`settle::validate_plans`: a stair whose rise disagrees with its levels, overlapping lots or levels, unknown levels, anything off the land, a resource off the land or without a kind, ...) and bad authored relief (`land::validate_relief`); hosting refuses a plan whose building doors no one reaches (`settle::stranded_doors`). `seed::tests::the_cullet_seed_is_hosted` runs those same checks on Cullet's seed natively.
- The drops between its levels are `drop` tiles (8: unwalkable, see-through; docs/PERCEPTION.md "Drops"): `a planned settlement's drops are drop tiles` (at least one on the hosted tiles). That sight and the foot agree across them is proved by `verify-core`'s `drop` scene and `verify-player`'s `drop.py`.
- Lands without `plans`, `relief.outline` or `relief.stamps` keep their pinned digests (`host::tests::pinned_digests_hold`, `cargo test -p living-rules`, not this driver). Since eye-2 the pinned tiles digests are those of the eye-height cells; the test also checks that the height rule (no model's `eye_cells`) still gives the digests before eye-2 exactly, and that every moved tile went from `Wall` to `Low` or `Drop`.

`living/rules/src/settle/plan.rs` (`resolve`, `validate`, `Ground`, `stamp_macro`, `site`, `build`, `retaining`, `stranded_doors`, `FixtureSpec`, `line_tiles`, `Laid.fixtures`); `living/rules/src/host.rs` (`hosted_map`, `fixture_hides`); `living/rules/src/land.rs` (`hydro_pass`: the authored outline and relief stamps, then the plans' stamps; `macro_pass` places the plan's site first and applies region stamps; `tile_rows` ground stamps; `connect_pass` leaves a planned site's disc as it is; `validate_relief`); `living/rules/src/settle.rs` (`Staged` step `Plan`, the generated steps skipping `Settlement::planned`); `living/authority/src/island.rs` (`params_of`, `host`, `PlannedLot.key`/`spots`, `Planned.lots`, `Hosted.resources`); `living/authority/src/seed.rs` (`authored_households` with `homes`, `buildings` with `lot`, `plan_resources`, `plan_structures`, `put_authored`); `living/authority/src/floor.rs` (`check`, `plan_kinds`); `living/authority/src/town.rs` (`spots_of` for `lot:<id>`).

## How to get to it (user POV)

Write a plan into a land seed's `map.params.plans` and publish it like any seed (`launch --seed plan-test`, or `create_floor` on a floor module). `living/seeds/plan-test.json` is the test seed: a harbour town of 9 buildings and one open lot (the drying garth, inside a 2 m wall with a gate; since `layout-2`, with a low fence, a net rack and a bench that does not block) on seed 3 at 1024 m with two villages, two households bound to lots, a named building at a lot, `lot:` places three authored resources (two fishing spots sharing one stock of 8 regrowing 1 a game hour, and reeds of their kind's own numbers), a lamp at the hall's door, a store by the inn's first seat and the loft's contents (since `rates-2`; before, the third resource was a `kelp`, a kind the shipped script lacks, which would now refuse the seed). `living/seeds/cullet.json` is the small city (written by `living/tools/cullet_plan.py`; its laws are `worlds/cullet/laws.rhai`): 203 lots (65 buildings and 138 open lots; 93 before `layout-2`) and 261 blocking walls on an authored island, five named buildings at lots, 158 authored resources of its laws' kinds (four pooled fishing grounds, kelp, sand, peat, roots, greens), 34 authored structures (the Cone's furnace and its peat store, the Batch's oven, 22 hearths, 2 smokehouses, 7 lamps, all lit), the Store's food, and no people.

## Driving it with generation.py

```bash
.agents/skills/verify-generation/scripts/generation.py --run generation-plans-$(date +%Y%m%d-%H%M%S) --feature worlds --seeds plan-test,cullet,floor-megacity-4k
```

The `plan-test` child runs every land check plus `check_plans` (`plan plan_test: ...` lines in its `checks.json`): one settlement in its style, its lots in order with their kinds, sizes, models, heights and level floors, walkable doors, `its N blocking fixtures (M lines) stand on tiles no one walks on`, `a blocking fixture above the eye hides (Wall tiles), a lower one is seen over`, `every gap in a blocking line is open at its middle`, `every one of its lots' N spots is ground one can stand on` (the last four since `layout-2`), each `homes` household owning and living at its lot, the named building's sign at its lot, each `lot:` place `at lot <id>, on one of its N spots` (within 1 m of a spot; before `layout-2`, near the lot's door), its town's community on it, `its N authored resources stand where written` (within 2.5 m; kinds counted in the name), `authored resources hold and regrow what the plan says, per real minute for the day`, `each of its N pools is one stock its spots share`, `its N authored structures stand where written`, `authored fires are lit for as long as their fuel lasts at their burning rate` (the rate read from the seed's laws) and `authored structures and lot stores hold what the plan says`. The `cullet` child runs the same checks on a town with no people: the people checks read `(none: the seed has no people)`. `floor-megacity-4k` adds `create_floor refuses a plan whose stair disagrees with its levels` (the plan-test plan with `rise_m` 3 on its 4 m stair, refused with "rises 3 m"; `create-floor-bad-plan.log`) and `create_floor refuses a plan naming a resource or structure kind the world does not have` (the plan-test plan with a `kelp` resource and a `furnace`, refused with "names kinds this world's tables do not have"; `create-floor-unknown-kinds.log`).

## Gotchas

- The rules' `settle::plan::tests` cover the frame, every refusal, the lay-out as written, continuous ground with drops closed to walking, sight but not walking across the drop (`people_see_across_the_plans_drop_but_cannot_walk_across`) and repeatability natively; this driver proves the authority's rows and refusals, not the ground's shape.
- The plan is part of the compiled seed: editing `plan-test.json` without a rebuild changes nothing.
- The fixture checks sample a blocking line every 0.25 m, 0.75 m clear of its gaps, and take a wall as hiding when its `height_m` is above 1.6 and its `eye_solid` at least 0.8 (the default `sight`); a seed that sets `sight.eye_m`, `solid` or `hides_m` needs the check to read them. That a wall has no leak and that the way in passes its gate is proved natively (`settle::plan::tests::a_blocking_fixture_blocks_walking_and_the_eye_rule_decides_sight`), not here.
- Planned lots have no style margin: a row of houses sharing walls blocks with no gap between them.
- Every planned resource must now be placed (an unknown kind refuses the seed), so the driver keeps no list of known kinds. `seed::tests::a_plan_naming_kinds_the_world_lacks_is_refused` proves the refusal natively on Cullet's seed with its laws, and that plan-test passes.
- The fuel check reads each burning kind's rate from the seed's laws text (`name: "<kind>", ... burns: #{ ... rate: N`); a kind whose `burns` is built another way gets no expected burn time and is checked as unlit.
- `floor_preview` does not run the plan's validation, so a plan it previews can still be refused by `init`; run `cargo test -p living-rules the_cullet_seed` (or the authority's `seed::tests`) after editing a plan.
