# The effective cover policy and private geometry

The public singleton is `cover_law`. A player cannot read the private `cover_ground` bands, nor `cover_derivation` and `cover_timer` (a derivation after a switch-on, `cover-genesis`; in the private-table inventory). `cover_law` reads on only when the land's cover geometry is complete. Cover changes the scoped seen rows at the authority.
