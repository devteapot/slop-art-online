# Staged genesis: the `world_preparing` refusal (verify-client)

Branch `staged-genesis`. A stepping build (`LIVING_GENESIS_STEPPING=1`, default world) published on the worker's `sg-stdb` stays at step 0 of 1 until `resume_genesis`. While it is being made: a fresh player's `join` and `human_move`, and the owner's `mind_where` and `mind_judged`, all answer HTTP 530 `world_preparing: the world is still being made (step 0 of 1: world: terrain, resources, people)`. After one `resume_genesis` the `genesis` row is `done` and the same player's `join` succeeds (its character exists). A world made before staged genesis has no `genesis` row and is always ready (`floor::ready`). Evidence: `.local/sg/refusal-check.txt`. The suite's other checks were driven by the scene gate on this branch (`cues`, and `motion_tables` 27/27 alone): `.local/living/verify/gate-1120022029*`, `client-sgmt`.
