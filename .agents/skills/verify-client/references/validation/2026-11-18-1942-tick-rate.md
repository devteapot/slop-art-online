# Tick-rate client comparison

The Cullet baseline is `29735dbe9` with the shared profiling diagnostic and
the candidate is the authority patch in `.local/tick/evidence/branch-authority.patch`
on that baseline. Both used real scoped Rust SDK connections to
`tick-stdb` (SpacetimeDB and SDK 2.14.1). Each scene used a fresh world
and its own player identity. The profiling flag was off.

- `sweep_rows.py --expect-kept` passed on both arms. Its comparator found
  zero differences across seven scene checkpoints and printed the two
  generated values of the joining player's health separately.
- `look_rows.py` passed 14 checks in each arm. All three fixed people rows
  and their look keys matched in the first and reconnect snapshots, with the
  separately generated player health set aside. The look tables were refused
  to players and anonymous readers, and the unseen person was absent.

Baseline files are under `.local/tick/evidence/verify-main/`. Candidate files
are the `client-tr-branch-s-1118b` and `client-tr-branch-l-1118b` runs under
`.local/living/verify/`. The comparison results are
`.local/tick/evidence/sweep-rows-compare.txt` and
`.local/tick/evidence/look-rows-compare.json`. These checks cover the fixed
perception fixtures and reconnect. Crowd delivery cadence is a separate
measurement.
