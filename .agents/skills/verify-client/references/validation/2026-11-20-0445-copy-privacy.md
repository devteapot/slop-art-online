- **What the negatives fail:** every isolation check, including the anonymous connection, the peek, both spectator subscriptions, SQL, the paused snapshots, the "on again" comparison and the viewers' own run-long counts. The SDK helper stays refused, so that check passes in both negatives.
- **The SDK helper (the GC's addition to probe (b)):** the spectator's `subscribe_to_all_tables` is refused whole in every arm, with `no such table: \`acquaintance\` … may be marked private`. The helper names every table in the generated bindings, private ones included, so a player cannot use it. The check requires that refusal, or no foreign row if it were ever accepted.
- **The kill switch with every viewer connected:** both laws off by row, and the tables are empty to the owner and in every viewer's cache (216 of 216). With the laws on again, the owner's counts recover within about 1.5 s. The paused comparison of eight sampled viewers' tables with their views is equal after the switch, as before it.
- **Gate case `copy_privacy`** (`scene_gate.py run --cases copy_privacy --force --no-main-check`, island module, 16 viewers, 60 fighters, 30 s window):
  - passed in 153 s on `fb942dec2` (`gate-1120053356-1e84c3`);
  - passed again in 133 s on the head merged with cover-genesis, `350316fa3` (`gate-1120054020-73ca92`, load 2.9 at start).
