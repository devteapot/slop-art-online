# law-override: the public `law_override` table

This record covers branch `law-override` (Spark), from main 3df6e1f1 ([docs/LAW_OVERRIDE.md](../../../../../docs/LAW_OVERRIDE.md)). The branch adds two tables:

- `law_override { name, value, revision }`: public. A world's own law values, which clients lay over the compiled script.
- `law_revision`: private.

Both runs used a private server, `slop-lawoverride-stdb` (v2.14.1, 127.0.0.1:3473). The bindings were regenerated in `slop-trials-stdb`, adding only the two tables and their types.

- **The fixed suite (`run.py`), once per build:**
  - scoped (`client-law-suite`): 45 PASS;
  - open (`client-law-open`, `LIVING_PERCEPTION=open`): 45 PASS and the known XFAIL (the open build's public `experience`).
  - In both:
    - the source inventory found `law_revision` private (`PRIVATE` updated) and `law_override` public;
    - the private-table refusals passed (106 tables in the scoped build);
    - the permission matrix (303 calls, 0 mismatches) refused `set_world_law` "admin only" to the anonymous, both players and the token-less caller, and accepted it from the admin;
    - `cues.py` and `near_tier.py` passed within the suite.
- **`cues.py` 21/21 and `near_tier.py` 7/7** also ran on their own launch, `client-law-cues`. Both toggle `cue_transport_on` and `near_tier_on`, which are value-only on this branch: delivery switched for live frame-view subscribers with no install.
- **verify-core's `law_override.py checks`** (`client-law-island` 45/45, `client-law-cullet` 49/49):
  - anonymous SQL reads every `law_override` row;
