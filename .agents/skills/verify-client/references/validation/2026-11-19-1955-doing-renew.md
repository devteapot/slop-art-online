# doing-renew: the probe's scratch prefixes, the suite, and the cue timing (verify-client)

Branch `doing-renew`, code commit `b1874726` on main `3df6e1f1`. Server: the worker's own `slop-doingrenew-stdb` (v2.14.1, 127.0.0.1:3441). The probe was built in the worker's target (`.local/living/verify/target-client` → `~/slop-doingrenew-target/client`).

## Changes in this skill

- The probe now also connects to `verify-player-*` and `verify-core-*` databases, besides `verify-client-*` and `verify-gate-*`. The new streams are verify-player's `rest_seen.py` and verify-core's `doing_deltas.py`. They subscribe named views only (`seen_doing`, `seen_doing_near`, `seen_doing_far`, `seen_doing_cue`; the owner's `doing`), never every table.
- No table, view, reducer or subscription of the authority changed. The `doing` row is now renewed within one transaction (one update to every subscriber) where it was a delete and, up to a second later, an insert.

## Runs

- Suite `run.py`:
  - `client-renew8`: 45 PASS, 0 FAIL, on a container with no other world.
  - `client-renew1`, with about a dozen of the worker's own worlds ticking on the same container and host load around 13: everything passed but `cues.py`. Its "law off: each of B's windups reaches A by its near tier within 150 ms of act_ms" failed, one windup arriving after 879 ms.
- `cues.py` alone, timed checks ("A receives each of B's strike windups first by a cue, within 100 ms of act_ms"; the law-off one at 150 ms):
  - **Crowded container** (several worlds of about 190 residents each, host load 10–30):
    - Branch: 3 of 4 runs late. `client-renew2`: a cue at 282 ms and a near copy at 2607 ms. `client-renew4`: 593 ms. `client-renew1`: 879 ms.
    - Main `3df6e1f1` (`client-main2..4`): 0 of 3.
    - Branch with `renew_in_place` off (`client-renew5..7`): 0 of 3.
  - **One world at a time** (host load 4–7):
    - Branch, law on: `client-alta-on`, `-altb-on`, `-altc-on`, `-altd-on`, 4 of 4 clean, every windup 8–53 ms.
    - Branch, law off: `client-alta-off`, `-altb-off`, 2 of 2 clean.
  - The authority did not stall: `bench.py` on the same branch world had tick p99 3.54 ms with the law on (3.18 off) and no tick over budget.
  - So the late windups go with a crowded container. A cost after the reducer (view refreshes, subscription evaluation) that `bench.py` does not see is not ruled out for crowded servers. The alternating runs found no difference once alone.
