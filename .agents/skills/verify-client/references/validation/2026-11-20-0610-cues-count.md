# cues-count: `cues.py` counts B's accepted strikes, not a fixed five (verify-client)

Branch `cues-count` (from `origin/main` `5560f0080`), dev-box, `sg-stdb` (v2.14.1, 127.0.0.1:3344), default world (`client-cc1`).

**Why.** In the gate of `bv-defaults-on`, under load 19, `cues` failed its first run and passed its retry. Only 4 of B's 6 strikes fell in the act window. All 4 came by cue first, within 0.6–0.9 ticks, but both windup checks required `len(first) >= 5` (the coordinator's evidence: `gate-1120062854-c582f4-a-client/cues.json`). The fixed count failed; delivery didn't.

**Change.**
- B strikes until the authority has accepted 5 strikes, at most 10 tries. The accepted ones come from B's private `skill_use` rows via owner SQL; `accepted_ms` is the windup's `fighter.act_ms` (the same transaction's `now`).
- Both windup checks now hold every accepted strike, and need at least 3, so a run without strikes still fails. Each accepted windup must reach A first by cue, before any tier row, within `MAX_TICKS` (2) ticks of `act_ms`.
- The law-off phase counts the same way: 4 wanted, at most 8 tries, at least 3, each by the near tier. Its streams run 20 s instead of 12, to cover the retries.
- Tick-based correctness is unchanged.

| Run | 1-minute load | Checks | Accepted strikes (on / off) | Cue lateness (ticks) | Receipt by cue (wall ms, measured only) |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| Normal | 4.5 at launch, 5.3 at the end | 22 / 22 | 5 / 4 | 0.12–0.9 | (in `cues.json`) |
| Heavy: busy loops on all 32 CPUs plus 8, each stopped by its PID after | 15.8 at the start, 35.6 at the end | 22 / 22 | 5 / 4 | 0.12–0.66 | 27–42 (near tier off: 29–46) |

Not run: a run where the host refuses so many strikes that fewer than 3 are accepted. That must fail; the check's minimum covers it. `maintain-verification-skill`: `features/perception.md` (the `cues.py` paragraph) states the accepted-strike count.
