# Perception Parity Audit: Behaviour Graphs, Heed, and Skills vs. Human Players

**Baseline:** `origin/main` (`e220bea8e`, read-only audit). **Owner rule ([AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md)):** *"Human-controlled and AI-controlled characters share authority, capabilities and physical rules … the same rules for players and minds; minds know only what they perceive."*

**Pilot context (`94bfe6f0c`):** `living/tools/nb_pilot.py:405-425` (`docs/NEURAL_BODY.md` "Pilot run") excluded 6,807 teacher decisions on `doing { phase: "about to release" }` and `stamina` because the pilot's minimal combat encoder omitted both. Against the **real player surface on `origin/main`**: `stamina` **is** delivered in `my_vitals` and rendered as a live HUD bar (`skills.rhai:2974`, `ui/cluster.rs:276`), whereas `doing.phase = "about to release"` is a real **(B) precision gap**, and multiple other graph, `heed`, and `skills.rhai` conditions are true **(C) leaks** reading private tables or unperceived world state.

**Classes:** **(A)** in player views/public tables & on screen (`A*` = exact in player view/table, coarse or unshown on HUD); **(B)** inferred from animation/visible phase (`B!` = AI has sharper timing/coords than a player); **(C)** hidden internals unavailable to players (`C-int` = AI-only internal cognitive state). **Counts:** canonical non-overlapping seeds = `repertoire.json` + `instincts.json` + 4 world seeds (`cullet`, `island`, `floor-megacity`, `plan-test`; `worlds/*/ways.json` duplicates `cullet`/`island` and is omitted from sums). `heed` = `living/seeds/heed.json`; `trials` = `living/trials/**/*.json` (84 files).

## 3. Uncertainties & How to Check
- **Visual readability of `about to release` (`f >= 0.5`)**: Static inspection confirms `combat.rs:824-836` scales human weapon clips so `peak` lands on `release_ms`, while `animal_clip` (`combat.rs:737-743`) uses fixed `rate: 1.0` and no HUD windup meter exists. Confirm how early a human can visually distinguish windup midpoint across varied durations by running `living-game` look-dev / `verify-player` against nominal (750 ms) and fast (350 ms) strikes and recording frame-by-frame clip poses.
- **Runtime law overrides**: Default laws in `skills.rhai:86,116,131,230,239` have `fov_on`, `stranger_by_name`, `cues_on`, `heed_on`, `heed_players` all `false`. Any experiment database overriding these (`script` table) should be checked via SQL (`SELECT * FROM script`) before relying on default-law risk notes.
