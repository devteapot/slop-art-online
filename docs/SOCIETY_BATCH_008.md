# Batch 008: repeat and controller cadence

The exact m1-16/world/model repeat at a fifteen-second post-completion interval finished eight minutes with three survivors: Tovan 42, Iri 4 and Renn 76 health. Mira died after failing to replenish her initial two food units, despite available communal food. The faster five-second variant finished with two: Mira 10 and Iri 84; Tovan died outside camp and Renn never established a working feeding policy.

The ordinary repeat made 56 calls (948,663 reported tokens); the faster variant made 69 (1,137,893). Both have two unfinished calls at shutdown. Both passed engine, scope and resource-conservation checks. The faster run had more generated-output errors, and this pair offers no evidence that spending more calls improves society behavior. Keep the ordinary cadence for the next test.

The ordinary repeat retained actual provision and shared work: Tovan gathered 55 and deposited 30; Renn gathered 49 and deposited 38; Iri gathered 14 and deposited six. These are gross counts including reuse. Mira built five shelter units and Renn seven, protecting surviving occupants after her death. Completed summaries include per-site net flows and speech-grounded learning.

Together with batch 007's four survivors, this supports useful common mechanics with interpretable variation. It does not establish the expanded roadmap's renewable settlement gate.
