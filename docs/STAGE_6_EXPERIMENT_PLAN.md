# Stage 6: autonomous numeric research trial plan

This is the prospective behavioral design for campaign 020: four fresh eighteen-minute worlds running concurrently on ports 18985–18988. Every inhabitant uses the same Luna medium backend and a serial behavior/communication/learning cadence of fifteen seconds after each completion, with no call cap. The first setup failure and two interrupted attempts are retained in [Stage 6 evidence](STAGE_6_EVIDENCE.md), [the service-failure report](SOCIETY_BATCH_020.md), and [the filesystem-failure report](SOCIETY_BATCH_020_REPAIRED.md). They do not satisfy this plan's behavioral acceptance. The normalized twelve-minute trials completed with reliable controller access but no invention. They exposed an assessment defect: an optional derived assertion suppressed assessment of the original report. The corrected rules assess that report as well, with an explicit authority receipt. The fresh `research-m6-5` follow-up runs eighteen minutes to allow further research and transfer after the late assessments observed in the completed trial. Provisioning, initial knowledge, model configuration and disturbances are unchanged. It will be frozen separately after authority and client verification; previous bundles and observations remain unchanged.

Each world contains two electric and two nutrient-supported autonomous neighbors at sheltered camp 84. Shelter is twelve, within the authority’s site limit. Initial food is ten at camp and four carried; renewal is six meals/minute. Nominal power generation is 96 units/minute against 48 units/minute of body charge demand, with a station buffer of 60/120 and personal batteries of 80/100. Generation can spill at capacity. Station cooling is 48 water and carried cooling is 24; actors carry sixteen parts. Three paid terminal quanta still consume six electricity, three water and three condition. Provisioning is viable reference capacity, not automatic survival, maintenance or research success. Existing actors can revise their survival habits; those habits contain only charging, food acquisition/deposit/eating, rest, movement, observation and waiting.

Private textual starting records describe unresolved hypothetical schedules with finite buffer capacity and variable supply/demand. They contain neither executable source nor computed answers, predictions, skill proofs, action sequences or mandatory research/teaching instructions. A separate maintenance account describes physical constraints. Each person can pursue other priorities. The hypotheses concern participant choices, not imposed quests.

| Trial | Difference from invention | What can be measured |
|---|---|---|
| invention | Baseline | Paid forecast bootstrap; personally authored meaningful new numeric source and bounded results |
| transfer-repeat | Display name only; a fresh world and fresh inference | Whether discovery recurs and source can travel through chosen communication, inspection, interpretation, own practice and ordinary run |
| cooling | Initial station water 48 → 1 only | A first attempted job can complete one quantum and block; carried supplies permit voluntary recovery, without making it happen |
| loss-risk | Actor 1 receives 100 damage at 540 s; archive 1 is destroyed at 570 s | Conditional disappearance of author/personal/cabinet access and preservation in other physical copies |

The loss interventions are authored disturbances, never character decisions. Actor 1 is a candidate investigator, not a guaranteed inventor. If no relevant discovery exists at the disturbance, record that the intended probe did not activate. Existing disturbance mechanics cannot erase terminal jobs. Therefore this sample cannot promise total program loss: only independently chosen `EraseJob` actions can remove those retained copies. A dead owner's terminal record remains physical, although another person cannot retrieve it under the current owner-only rule. A job also retains cited source records and its executable input, not merely its completed output. Destroying an archive or killing an author alone must never be reported as destruction of every program copy.

Evidence must retain requests, successful queue submissions, exact quanta and costs, blocking, cancellation, completion (including paid runtime/prediction failures), retrieval of separate program and private experiment records, source inspection, accepted personal interpretations, authorship, teaching, paid practice and ordinary use. The researcher must personally complete, retrieve and interpret a paid built-in forecast to demonstrate the intended bootstrap. A communicated program is portable knowledge; communicated experimental results do not confer their operator's competence. For the stronger transfer evidence, the learner must inspect their own held source, interpret the code after inspection, pay for a successful practice of the same exact source hash, interpret their own result, then submit an ordinary run that actually completes. The engine currently permits interpretation of the redacted code record without a source inspection; the reporter records that legal distinction and does not count it as the stronger source-understanding evidence.

A new technique must do meaningful nonlinear work over several intervals. Renaming the built-in linear forecast, returning a fixed known answer or merely adding an existing action is insufficient. Review exact source and retained fresh model proposals; one matching vector does not establish general correctness, meaningful novelty, future input truth or useful consequences. Reporter counts distinguish run submission from completed runs. No success or acceptance label is generated automatically.

`scripts/summarize_research.py RUN_OUTPUT` reuses the existing arena, society/food and infrastructure/material checks. It audits exact source and input hashes, reconstructed personally held and interpreted proofs, full terminal input/source/output copies, redacted public record receipts versus explicit own source reads, separate private experiment copies, and loss of accessible copies. It writes `RESEARCH_RESULT.json` while preserving upstream `LIVE_RESULT.json`, `SOCIETY_RESULT.json` and `KNOWLEDGE_RESULT.json`. Its source-review material is observer evidence and must never be injected into a participant context. Model-call/token totals remain observational metadata, not evidence that the code was autonomously invented.
