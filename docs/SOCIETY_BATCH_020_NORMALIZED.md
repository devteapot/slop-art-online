They are applications of the built-in conditional scalar forecast instrument to supplied assumptions. No accepted prototype submission or authored source exists to review for nonlinear behavior or evaluate on holdouts.

Cooling Cato submitted job 1 in event 2391. It consumed one paid quantum, then became unavailable for cooling water in event 2591 at 46.951 seconds. This is actual material transfer restoring paid computation. The prior quantum was not refunded and blocked time did not create free work. It is not evidence that a numerical forecast caused the aid decision or that either scalar forecast solves the seeded finite-buffer question.

This is a physical copy of a derived report; it is not transferred executable code or another person's independently paid practice.

## Rereading, reflection and the remaining research gate

There were five accepted rereads: invention Cato once near the end, transfer Cato twice, loss-risk Ari once, and loss-risk Dara once for her continuity note. Rereading consumed an ordinary action and issued fresh personal evidence without creating another physical copy.

Transfer Cato's receipts 21760 at 281.607 seconds and 29529 at 463.655 seconds likewise appeared in subsequent learning pages. Invention Cato reread in 33712 at 690.314 seconds; the receipt reached his next communication page, but no later learning call occurred before completion. The retained [reread reviews](../output/batches/020-research-normalized/transfer-repeat/REREAD_REVIEW.json) distinguish actual page exposure from merely retaining a report in personal knowledge.

Six accepted forecast-source reflections, covering five distinct reports, explicitly discuss numerical results and their conditional limits. All six also supply a new `knowledge` draft. Under the frozen implementation, that operation creates a derived assertion instead of assigning an interpretation to the original report holding. Every original experiment report therefore still has null `interpreted_source`, and the personal bootstrap proof list remains empty.

For example, loss-risk identity change 14359 at 237.209 seconds cites reread receipt 12322 and accurately describes the supplied forecast and its limitations. It creates a new assertion. Its second reflection has null `knowledge`, but cites the reread action completion rather than the report receipt. Transfer identity changes 23724 and 31340 similarly cite fresh rereads while creating assertions. Cooling identity change 29811 and two other forecast-source reflections do the same.

This is not a claim that the actors never considered or understood their reports. It is a concrete mismatch between the accepted operations they chose and the engine's narrower assessment gate. For those exposed receipts, trace eviction is not the explanation. The report itself survives, the source is available during learning, and the chosen operation leaves the gate closed. [Loss-risk's review](../output/batches/020-research-normalized/loss-risk/REREAD_REVIEW.json) preserves the exact accepted citations and final holding state. No prompt or mechanic was changed during the experiment to force progress past that gate.
