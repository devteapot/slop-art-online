# Lid ink for hand-made face parts — proposed client hook

The GC requested a second high fit limited to the lid stroke. The first-fit iris and opening are the baseline. The current GLB’s eyeline uses the `eyes` slot and the upper-eye vertices all have UV0 = (0, 1). The client maps that slot to `FacePart` and draws its albedo with cast shadows. Only `HandHair` samples the distance field and computes screen-pixel coverage. [Slot routing](../../../living/game/src/handmade.rs) · [Material binding](../../../living/game/src/lookdev.rs) · [Fragment shader](../../../living/game/src/toon.wesl)

**Laptop decision requested.** Allow an opt-in lid-ink profile on a dedicated `eyes` material. Bind a linear, mipmapped per-character sidecar in the existing auxiliary texture binding. Sample UV0. One proposed encoding uses G for clamped UV distance to the nearest stroke centreline and R for its local width fraction. Width is zero at a tapered tip. The profile specifies the encoded UV range and maximum stroke width in screen pixels. Include the profile and map in the material cache key. Apply the hair shader’s derivative-based, one-pixel coverage calculation to the local width and alpha-blend that coverage over the eye or skin. Keep the eye material’s cast-shadow response. Audit the map, positive range and noncollapsed UVs on the opted-in primitive.

**Asset work after approval.** Give the existing upper and outer lower lid strokes padded, nonoverlapping UV islands on morph-following carrier strips. The carrier must cover the whole wing at three-quarter and include a transparent filtering margin. Generate the field from curves for the upper stroke (thin inner corner, widest over the outer third), pointed wing, two or three lash flicks and a short outer lower stroke. Replace the corresponding opaque strokes to avoid a double outline. Keep the mouth and brows on their existing materials and preserve the first-fit eye disk and aperture vertices.

**Proof.** Compare matched front and three-quarter portraits, then inspect the native 58° Near and Middle frames in motion for crawl. Repeat the blink, eye-joy, 15-key sweep and exact neutral return, all 20 colour checks, both audits, rear plait frames and factory replay.
