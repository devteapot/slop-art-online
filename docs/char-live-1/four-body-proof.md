**Start with the braid.** It tests whether an authored silhouette survives a new head, without also depending on sleeve or skirt weighting. Fit the same cut on all four recipes before drawing another session. Use the back view to measure the nape anchor, braid centreline, tie and tip. Examine the face opening in front and three-quarter views, check behind both ears, then rotate the neck and run a gait. Keep each body’s original face and outfit as the control.

The jersey master includes the complete torso, back neckline and sleeves for wear on its own. For the coat-worn variant, mark the faces covered by that fitted coat for removal as in the generator’s existing hidden-face pass. Cora’s front insert is the fitted example for that variant. Preserve a narrow underlap along the opening and verify each body in motion.

The exact fringe silhouette, lapel fold, plait crossing order and paint strokes need an artist’s master. The parameter controls should deform those decisions. A seed that draws new strokes or new topology cannot guarantee the same design. The earlier hybrid spike found that valid weights alone missed a seated hem failure and that repeated fine contrast could outscore an attractive face. Judge the first proof from actual clips and portraits as well as measurements. [Hybrid spike verdict](../character-hybrid-spike/verdict.md)

## First proof and acceptance

Use the three crossing paths and tie from the accepted back view as the braid master. In hair.py, place the nape anchor on the deformed scalp and derive the tip from the wearer's back length. Space the three crossings along arc length. Bound the diameter by head width, and the pitch and crossing count by usable braid length. Retain each recipe's age, type, build and seed from the table above. Run the same geometry and clip checks on the four saved outputs. Put the four back views and four turned heads beside one another before authoring a second cut.

For each body, record total triangles, hair primitives, root-to-scalp distance, braid-to-collar overlap, ear visibility, and a Near and Middle still. Run idle, walk, run and both seats with a neck turn. An exposed root, a braid clipping the collar or a tie disappearing under the coat fails that body. The accepted plait has one toon material. The blunt lining shape behind Cora’s ear belongs to the known profile defect and should be excluded from the braid master.

The clothing prototype uses one shared cloth material with linear vertex dyes. Export must explicitly select the joined mesh's active colour attribute and verify COLOR_0 in the GLB. The first Bevy capture lost this attribute and rendered white. The fitted hands reuse the generator's weighted palm, phalanges and thumb. Fit thumb splay and palm rotation before adjusting finger lengths. On the rig, bake the fitted rest space for export, preserve J_Bip joint paths, and test the front, side and underside of the seated coat. The two live seated fits showed that normalized weights alone cannot establish coverage.
