# Face fit on the generator rig

**The portrait gate remains open after two high-effort fits.** The [second portrait and overlay](face-b-portrait-review.png) show the wide green eye and large catchlight, a visible brow on each side, freckles and the position of the mouth. At this angle the lower cheek turns sharply into a narrow chin, the upper lip ends in two vertical marks and a strip of background shows behind the ear and under the opposite temple. The drawing has a softer continuous jaw plane, a fuller cheek and a defined lip contour. The sampled [orbit and 58° play cameras](face-b-review.png) retain the outfit's values. The [expression row](face-b-expression-review.png) shows both lids closed on blink, separation on A, lifted mouth corners on smile and a return to the original open eyes and neutral mouth.

The first fit used the same expression rig, with the original warm iris-light palette. Its [portrait](face-a-portrait-review.png) exposed an amber lower iris, overlapping blockout neck and excess shell shading. The second fit used green iris-light values, joined the collarbones to the generated head's 56-vertex neck ring, moved the rig forward by 5 turnaround pixels and broadened the lower cheek. The second studio check uses a fixed normal-based ramp, generator paint and client-style backface culling. The saved model retains the original material outputs. The revised review brings out a problem at the temples that needs checking with backface culling on the accepted hair as well as on the head.

The [checkpoint](cora-face-b.blend) carries the adult-f-c cage with identity and eye joints, all 15 generator expression keys and 6,756 face triangles. The fitted sections and feature heights are recorded in the [design and map hashes](face-final-design.json). The procedural atlas, iris and translucent tint maps were generated locally from the fitted head. The eye and paint routines were loaded from the project's char-4-art revision 6cab88bcd; the journal records the fitted eye opening and iris-light palette. The portrait reference is opened only by the external sheet compositor. The final audit verified all three packed maps against their generator output hashes and found zero violations.
