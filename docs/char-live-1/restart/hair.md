# Hair fit

**The hair stage passed at high.** The [orbit and play cameras](hair-finish-review.png) show a swept auburn mass framing an open forehead, an ear opening in profile and a woven plait dropping down the centre back. The play views use the 58° Blender framing at 8 and 12 m. The [close-ups](hair-finish-detail.png) show the darker under-layer below the sweep, the single tapered copper highlight and the rear masses gathering toward the braid. The head and ears are fitting guides. Face-rig fitting and moving-hair clearance remain for their later passes.

The mesh follows the scalp-cap, solid combed sheet, temple-lock and gathered-braid construction in hair.py. Three tapered paths change sides and depth to weave the plait; they share the same mesh as the cap, locks, binding and brush. One generator hair material, M00_000_Hair_00_HAIR, carries the colour through the Color corner attribute. The restricted dye values and highlight are authored directly on the geometry. The hair has 6,270 vertices and 12,332 triangles before export. [Mesh counts](hair-stats.json)

The [first fit](hair-review.png) left air under the far temple and a rectangular end on the highlight. The second fit pulled both layers inward together, softened the highlight tips and redirected the rear bands toward the nape. Both stage audits and the final saved-state audit found zero images and zero violations. Workbench used temporary corner colours on the garments to display their existing dyes; those preview attributes were removed before the final save. The final blend opens with material preview; the live bridge retains the Workbench renderer for the next stage. [Checkpoint](cora-hair-finish.blend) · [Audit](audit.jsonl)

The restart replay instructions in clothing.md also apply here. Run inside the repository so the replay can import the generator material implementation. A factory-startup replay reproduced the six final views and four head close-ups pixel for pixel. The stage used two high-effort fits and about 4½ minutes including replay. External image calls and game builds were zero. Token and billing totals were unavailable.
