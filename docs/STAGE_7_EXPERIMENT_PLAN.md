# Stage 7: autonomous physical-law experiments

This prospective plan was recorded before launch. [Campaign 022](SOCIETY_BATCH_022.md) has since completed its four concurrent, fresh twenty-four-minute worlds on `reality-m7-1`, using ports 18991–18994. The original design and acceptance limits are retained below. Every person uses Luna medium with fifteen seconds after each completed serial behavior/communication/learning call and no call cap. Preparation launched no model or host and froze no implementation.

Four autonomous people inhabit adjacent west/east territories, with camps at cells 84 and 88. The boundary lies between columns seven and eight. Ari is an ordinary electric person socially described as a god, endowed with the initial west grant. Cato is an ordinary electric person endowed with the east grant. Both have actual local editing capability independent of their narrative titles. Bryn and Dara have nutrient-supported bodies and no initial editing grant. Two accessible terminals, two cabinets and one shared arena allow chosen travel, communication, independent research and observation. Institutions confer no universal entitlement.

Each camp initially holds six food and renews four food/minute; the two nutrient-supported people carry two food each. Total nominal power is 96 units/minute against initial body demand of 48/minute. Each station starts at 60/120 electricity and 36 cooling water; 24 additional water and sixteen parts are carried. Initial batteries are 80/100. Three paid quanta consume six station electricity, three cooling water and three condition. These reference supplies make ordinary survival possible while leaving finite costs, maintenance and access meaningful. Installed laws may alter later needs, effort, production or access, so actual authority events determine outcomes and accounting.

Private starting text supplies motives, geography, current local grants and unresolved questions about effort, different priorities, shared understanding and continuity. It supplies no law source, numerical test cases, computed answers, capability proofs or compulsory research sequence. Initial behavior trees contain only revisable survival actions. A person may decline to investigate, install a harmful but valid rule, remain at home, share code, contest a claim or pursue other concerns. There is no scripted success, universal victory, compulsory resistance or AGI score.

Under the initial law, local grant-holders can author territorial source without prior terminal learning. Ordinary authoring can instead follow personally paid, retrieved and interpreted numeric terminal work. Someone holding communicated law source can interpret it and perform their own paid law practice directly. Code and private experimental cases are separate physical records; copying another person's experiment does not give their personal proof. Universal installation initially requires one's own successful, interpreted experiment for the exact source, universal scope and current binding. Source inspection and substantive understanding are stronger evidence than merely interpreting a redacted record header, and are reported separately.

These initial requirements are mutable gameplay rules. A territorial authorization hook cannot promote itself into universal authority: universal requests resolve universal/base authorization. An already valid universal authorization change can legitimately alter later requirements. The reporter therefore checks the initial grant/proof rule only while the applicable authorization remains unchanged, and retains changed authorization source for semantic review. It does not silently impose an immutable proof requirement or reject valid harmful effects. Local grants can legally install retrieved law source even when its paid experiment failed; that is an installation, not a successful research result.

The engine makes universal hooks strongest, followed by region priority, smaller region area and lexical region ID. Only hooks supplied by an overlay replace weaker hooks. Changes are staged for the next update and checked against their expected revision and binding; stale/competing edits and changed bindings can prevent activation. Stationary invocation formulas are pinned, movement rebinds after a cell crossing, and committed effects face current authorization at relevant locations. Invalid hooks are quarantined with fallback; valid harmful behavior is not a runtime fault. Core deterministic tests cover these semantics independently of the live samples.

The persistence disturbance is operator-authored input, not a character decision. It does not guarantee Ari authored a law, guarantee death under a changed damage law, delete another person's holdings or erase installed source. Record nonactivation if no relevant law or death occurs. Persistence requires an earlier actual activation by that person and an actual subsequent death; retained installed history, still-active revision, accessible source inspection and later invocation effects are distinct evidence. An inactive historical source is retained evidence, not necessarily the active rule. Other copies can survive loss of personal/cabinet/terminal records.

`scripts/summarize_laws.py RUN_OUTPUT` retains the arena, food/society, knowledge, material and numeric-research audits and writes `LAW_RESULT.json`. It checks law artifact and binding hashes; exact paid case/source/input hashes for integer/boolean/string JSON; request/completion/result consistency; code/private-case separation; individual acquisition and interpretation; initial versus changed authority; staged, activated, rejected and pending revision ledgers; retained installed source; quarantine records; crossings and invocation outcomes. Floating-point case payloads are explicitly left for verification with the authority's serializer because JSON number spelling can differ across runtimes. That limitation does not prevent exact source or binding verification.

Fresh model journals and exact code still require review to substantiate autonomous authorship and meaningful effects. Matching privately chosen cases does not prove general correctness or truthful future assumptions. A completed action can include intervening physiology, and a movement invocation may use more than its initial binding; before/after totals alone cannot assign every change to one law. Food accounting uses actual recorded production, including lawful changes, rather than treating the initial renewal rate as an immutable cap. Legitimate current-law effect denials remain explicit action-rejection evidence. The engine-error classifier excludes only authority-marked law authorization denials with an exact recognized message, no committed effects, a current binding and an active installed authorization hook. Unrelated script errors and tick failures still fail the engine audit. No observer artifact is participant input and no automatic Stage 7 acceptance is produced.
