# Stage 5: physical utilities and the first faction seed

The bounded infrastructure and authored faction-world slice is accepted with the limitations below. This stage introduces versioned body support profiles, local utility equipment, material inventories, explicit equipment permissions and physically paid computation. The current core is `m5-4-scaled-publication.1`. [Campaign 016](../configs/experiments/campaign/016-infrastructure.json) and its repeat test four small worlds before the authored faction seed is run. Prior settlement, knowledge, renewal and inter-settlement evidence retains its original limitations.

## Execution and material contracts

Electricity is separate from stamina and food. Electric bodies consume configured charge per physiological pulse. A fully funded pulse does not injure an exactly emptied battery; subsequent unfunded pulses produce damage under the active law. Unfunded support debt survives reload and is settled at the hazard boundary, independently of update size. A later top-up cannot retroactively fund missed support. Rest restores stamina and eating cannot recharge a battery. Costs follow the body profile, not faction, controller, office or displayed identity.

Stations contain generation, charging and terminal modules, electricity buffers, parts, water and equipment condition. Initial machinery has an explicit endowed embodied-parts balance. Construction moves carried parts into a new module; repair consumes carried parts and restores configured condition. Transfers, charging losses, body consumption, generation, compute energy, discharged cooling water and repair sinks have separate authority events. Unused generation at capacity is recorded as spill; disabling equipment cannot bank missed generation for later.

Local actors require explicit asset rights. Use, maintenance and administration are distinct; cultural membership or council office does not grant them. Operations validate locality, living actors, arena scope, quantities and rights inside the existing transactional adapter. Invalid effects roll back stocks, work, job IDs and perceptions together.

Each station runs its own persisted FIFO queue. Work proceeds only when a whole quantum's electricity and cooling are available; it also wears equipment. Revocation, disabled equipment, wear, absent terminal, insufficient power and insufficient cooling have distinct recorded blocking reasons. Authorized submitted work may continue after its author dies, leaving a physical result. Owners or authorized administrators may cancel work, without refunds for completed quanta. One blocked station does not freeze another.

The initial bounded Rhai program computes a conditional resource forecast from explicit supplied assumptions and optionally cited personal records. It cannot read world inventories, other minds, audit history or network resources. Input and program hashes, source and outputs remain reproducible. Results are private physical terminal records until their owner retrieves them locally; ordinary teaching or archive copying can then communicate them. The arithmetic does not certify input truth, confer mastery, buy model calls or perform actual backend training. Useful decisions after computation require separate live evidence.

## Small experiment

Four neighbors share camp 84: two electric and two nutrient-supported bodies. Food renews at four units per minute. Baseline electricity generation is 72 units per minute against 48 units of body demand, with finite buffers and cooling water. Actual generation can be lower at capacity. The power control reduces generation to 24/minute; the cooling control reduces station water from 20 to one; the access control removes only one electric person's use permission. Carried parts and water reserves stay fixed, permitting chosen repair or provisioning.

All four worlds retain ordinary revisable survival habits, including charging for electric bodies. No starting tree performs computation, grants rights, repairs equipment or supplies cooling. Individuals have different motives and private method/maintenance accounts; those are starting information, not imposed task sequences. All controllers use fresh Luna medium calls, a fifteen-second post-completion cadence and no call cap.

The evidence must separate requested, queued, paid, completed, retrieved and interpreted computation. A claim about allocation is not a changed permission; a forecast is not useful merely because it exists. Record failures, deprivation, absent work and unsuccessful proposals alongside completed effects.

## Authored geography and institutions

The proposed integration seed has 36 people across four homelands, SF, an independent city and a mixed settlement, connected through wild land. Hugging Face and NVIDIA each contain multiple people and member-owned facilities. Cultures and equipment are scenario data. The engine checks references without recognizing any faction name.

SF has four faction representatives and two local representatives. The independent city has voluntary associations and no sovereign office. Civic offices expose no script installation or reality-editing capability. Two SF residents have explicit fictional addiction/housing-insecurity starting situations; both retain ordinary agency, including an electric resident under this world's fictional physiology. Detailed consumption and treatment physiology remain undesigned; their seed descriptions are not evidence of an implemented addiction simulation. Material exposure, food, access and chosen care retain the same shared mechanics.

Territory boundaries and initial editor designations are retained as seed metadata here. They confer no operative edit power until Stage 7. The intended execution contract is explicit spatial scope, deterministic overlap precedence, versioned law changes and pinned in-progress work; crossing and conflicting edits must receive direct tests before scoped editing is accepted. Universal rules currently govern the entire seed. Initial affiliations describe institutions at creation, not immutable roles or compulsory political outcomes.

## First live result and executable intent correction

[Batch 016](SOCIETY_BATCH_016.md) passed all material, copy, scope and engine audits, but submitted no compute jobs in any of its four worlds. Its cooling contrast was therefore not activated. Each sample lost one person: two deaths followed unfunded electric support, and two nutrient-supported inhabitants starved. No grants, construction, repairs, parts transfers or cooling-water contributions occurred. Those absences are retained outcomes, not demonstrations of the unused capabilities.

One baseline controller explicitly declined to submit because it could not safely express a single job in a repeating tree or predict its future retrieval ID. Version `m5-2-deliberate-compute.1` adds the general `once {child}` node and `retrieve_ready {station}`. A once subtree runs until first success, then yields failure on later visits so a priority can select other work. Completed intent persists across cycles, guard changes and reload; explicit replacement of that subtree or its child re-arms it, while unrelated patches preserve it. Failure may retry. Retrieval selects only the caller's oldest completed, uncollected physical result at the local station.

[Campaign 017](../configs/experiments/campaign/017-infrastructure-repeat.json) repeats the four samples with scenario and controller inputs identical to frozen 016. Starting habits still contain no compute, once, retrieval, grant or maintenance task. Tests prove one chosen job/retrieval across repeated cycles and reload, private ready-result selection, and precise patch behavior. This fixes an observed expressive limitation without supplying a character's decision. The repeat and subsequent faction-world run remain under evaluation.

## Transport interruption and faction-run preparation

During 017, SpacetimeDB 2.1.0 panicked in its subscription route (`buffer should be unique`) and restarted around simulation time 180 seconds. All eight built-in controller connections then remained closed; their existing physical policies kept running, while external controllers reconnected for fresh calls. This is an infrastructure interruption, not evidence that those eight people chose to stop deliberating. The two completed baseline forecasts predate the restart. Their assumed inputs and absence of demonstrated allocation consequences limit claims of useful prediction independently of the interruption.

The harness now reconnects between deliberations with its retained original session. It does not replay commands, grant authority or change control epochs. A real-server isolated probe (`output/society-lab/sim-reconnect-proof-1788654969887.json`) forcibly closed the client transport and verified unchanged identity, control and policy/learning revisions, personal state and idempotent prior receipts. Reconnection after revocation failed without recreating the grant. Failed replacement connections are closed. This contains transport loss; it does not claim to fix the upstream server panic.

Before campaign 018, all 184 Rust tests passed (141 simulation, 32 bridge, eight host, one compatibility and two client), as did 20 reporting fixtures. Prior orchestration/enrollment checks remain retained. Native binaries, server WASM and the client built successfully. Surveyed region boundaries are now visible on the map, and the inspector shows the selected person's initial affiliations and offices. Participant surveys are clipped to their arena; no editor assignments or foreign member state are exposed by the geography projection. Campaign 018 retains all 36 authored people and runs for twelve minutes with the same Luna-medium controller profile and fifteen-second cadence.


## Scale diagnosis and a fresh faction run

Batch 018 first hit the legacy 100 KB scenario transport limit, before any world or model call. The compact 36-person seed is approximately 117 KB; a bounded 2 MiB transport allowance now accommodates the existing 256-actor core capacity. Its next attempt exposed quadratic subscription work and was stopped as a failed diagnostic. The final authority state reached 28.068 seconds and five updates; a stale observer export alone did not identify that endpoint. Cleanup confirmed a paused clock and retained the failed run separately.

Each participant subscription previously reparsed the whole world whenever anyone read or acted. Private per-actor cached status now supplies control, health, revisions, receipts and exact captured read responses, authenticated through the same grant. Full subjective observations still come from an atomic read. Immutable evidence leases share their data when transactions clone the world; captured contexts remain serialized JSON during world loading, and publication streams them without rebuilding their object graphs. No audit data or foreign state becomes participant input.

Three no-model 36-connection diagnostics exposed and checked the fixes. The final diagnostic completed 108/108 concurrent reads without receipt failures or identity/epoch/privacy-validation failures, with round median latencies 1.288, 1.033 and 1.762 seconds. Simulation time advanced at 0.9884 of sampled wall time. Actual update cadence averaged 5.065 Hz, below the nominal 20 Hz target: this is a successful short capacity check, not a sustained 20 Hz claim. All diagnostics retained snapshots and ended with their clocks paused; the automated final two stopped their hosts and revoked only their own grants.

[Campaign 019](../configs/experiments/campaign/019-faction-world-scaled.json) starts a fresh twelve-minute, 36-person world using the same authored physical and controller inputs. It tests sustained play after the delivery fix. The failed 018 outputs and successful no-model transport tests remain distinct from live society evidence.


## History growth and capacity follow-up

Batch 019 is a failed capacity trial, explicitly interrupted after history growth slowed the authority. Its final paused state is 259.291 simulation seconds and 324 updates; it does not satisfy the planned twelve minutes. All 36 grants were revoked and its host stopped. The raw supervisor metadata was retained because the original cleanup path incorrectly labeled the interruption completed. A separate interruption correction records that provenance.

The retained 15.75 MB live world contained 11.45 MB of evidence leases and 3.59 MB of current experience entries. The implementation now shares unchanged participant state when cloning a world and keeps immutable historical payloads as validated raw JSON, parsing individual bodies only when evidence checks need their fields. Serialized state and participant APIs retain their existing JSON shapes, trace limits and lease duration. A branch/reload test verifies that evolving one world cannot mutate the original world's receipts or captured evidence.

On the same retained state, native release profiling measured approximately 19.5 ms to deserialize, 0.45 ms to clone, 6.05 ms to serialize and 11.94 ms to publish all participant responses. One hundred consecutive 50 ms simulation steps averaged 6.28 ms with a 56.51 ms maximum, compared with the earlier 68 ms mean and 440 ms maximum. These are CPU diagnostics, not sustained database or controller throughput claims. The complete Rust suite passes 186 checks (143 simulation, 32 bridge, eight host, one compatibility and two client). Campaign 021 keeps the same 36-person scenario and controllers for the required live repeat.


## Bounded Stage 5 decision

[Batch 021](SOCIETY_BATCH_021.md) completed the full planned interval on the frozen `faction-world-m5-5` implementation: 740.323 measured active wall seconds, 720.675 simulation seconds, 856 updates and 172,892 authority events. The new completion protocol verified the actual duration and final authority hash. The final state retained all 36 identities, with 29 survivors and seven permanent deaths. Infrastructure, food, knowledge-copy, geography/scope and engine audits found no violations. The browser rendered the live geography and labels without console errors. Completed hosts now serve preserved archive views.

All eighteen electric bodies survived; 234 charging operations used physical stations and existing rights. One NVIDIA member submitted, paid for and retrieved a conditional forecast, consuming six electricity and three cooling water. He communicated its stated result, but did not personally interpret the record through the learning operation. No changed allocation followed that number. Three actual one-food gifts reached Dai in SF. Local deposit activity did not produce a net provider at any site. Hugging Face's members discussed a terminal but did not build one. Labels, offices, religious language and proposals did not independently create world effects.

The final accounts reconcile: food `90 + 324 = 187 + 227`; electricity `1710 + 5391 = 1914 + 5181 + 6`; cooling water `396 = 393 + 3`; parts remained 229. Finite stocks, local rights, physiology, individual policies and geography had material consequences despite nominal aggregate supply. This accepts the shared body/utility/queued-computation contracts and the first authored faction seed, supported by the smaller power, cooling and access controls in 016–017 and the full 36-person sample.

This is not acceptance of sustained 20 Hz capacity or a stable faction economy. Late cadence was approximately 0.7 Hz; 36 external attempts failed their five-second initial subscription deadline, with a worst surviving-character gap of 198.997 simulation seconds between model observations. No command receipt timed out and survivors continued deliberating, but transport overhead and delayed control opportunities remain material limitations. Seven people died amid uneven provisioning and unsuccessful policies. Useful compute allocation, broad aid, institutional cooperation, autonomous construction, detailed addiction/care physiology and sustainable population renewal remain unproven. Stage 6 now tests whether physically paid computation can support participant-created techniques and transferable practical capability.

The final verification set is 186 Rust tests and 42 Python fixtures, plus the retained actual-server reconnect probe, 108/108 short simultaneous read checks, live browser checks and all final 021 audits. Final authority SHA-256: `6e1ef582b8c778f2079723a82f281c95a4695153444fcc1dfaadbf00b80d37c1`.
