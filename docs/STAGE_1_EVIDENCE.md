# Stage 1 evidence and the next smallest mechanism

This maps retained experiments to [Stage 1 of the world roadmap](WORLD_ROADMAP.md#1-a-viable-settlement), following the expanded [iteration plan](SOCIETY_ITERATION_PLAN.md). It does not restart M1 or mark later world stages complete.

## What the retained trials establish

| Roadmap concern | Evidence | Current limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shared authoritative entities and capabilities | Mixed built-in/external controllers in the six-arena matrix and four-character trials; scope/transaction tests | Representative biological needs only; no distinct charging body yet |
| Executable intent and recovery | [Batch 006](SOCIETY_BATCH_006.md) resumed a delivery after urgent rest; deterministic reload/preemption tests | Generated plans can still oscillate, hoard, or fail parsing |
| Material interaction | [Batches 006–007](SOCIETY_BATCH_007.md): net food transport, shared construction, later collection and meals | Gross gather/deposit counts include reuse; no specialization is guaranteed |
| Communication and private learning | Speech-grounded accepted beliefs/trust changes in batches 006–007; bounded private evidence and isolation tests | Delayed learning can be rejected after intervening harm; no physical archive or durable social knowledge store |
| Consequences and permanent death | Batch 007 shortage consumed all ten starting units, then all four died; cold still damages exposed actors | Finite reserve trials do not prove a renewable economy |
| Repeatability and inspection | Pinned implementations, isolated databases, common start gates, immutable final captures, live/archive dashboard | Model decisions remain stochastic; same seed is not an exact model replay |
| Group survival | m1-16: 4/4 in batch 007, 3/4 in batch 008 at the original cadence | Low surviving health and finite starting stocks keep Stage 1 open |

Batch 008's shorter five-second post-completion cadence produced 2/4 survivors versus 3/4 at fifteen seconds. It made more calls and had more output errors. This pair does not justify adopting faster scheduling as an improvement; retain the existing fifteen-second cadence for the next mechanism test.

## Mechanics, balance and seed ownership

| Existing field or artifact | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Rhai law/skill definitions, effect validation and controller authorization | Reusable rules and execution |
| Needs rates, action nutrition/costs, growth rule and permitted parameter bounds | Reusable balance definitions |
| Scenario geography, sites, initial stocks/shelter, weather parameters, initial people, motives, beliefs and relationships | Seed content/configuration |
| Scenario food-source location, interval, yield and growth ceiling | Configured physical assets; no faction-name dependency |
| Implementation bundle plus resolved scenario/controller files and hashes | Current versioned seed composition boundary |
| Later inventories, observations, beliefs, decisions, transfers, damage and production | Evolving authoritative state |

The existing scenario format plus frozen definition artifacts is the incremental starting point. A standalone general ruleset registry, full procedural generator and second complete setting remain deferred. New food-source references are validated against existing sites. A deterministic label-change check confirms that changing settlement/inhabitant/cultural labels leaves production and harvest costs equivalent. This is a mechanics test, not a claim that different cultures make identical LLM decisions.

No new attribute framework is needed for this test: health, hunger, energy, carried food and the existing traits already have relevant effects. Preserve caution, introspection and empathy. Body-specific charging waits for a representative non-eating inhabitant and a concrete resource requirement.

## Next experiment: a renewable necessity budget

The smallest missing mechanism is **accounted food production**. Add a seed-configured source driven by elapsed simulation time and a versioned Rhai rule. Production is an explicit world event; it does not put food into anyone's inventory or choose who harvests. Full sites discard unused growth opportunities, and deposits remain conserved transfers. Growth and source configuration become character knowledge only through local perception.

Current upkeep: hunger rises 2 per 2.5 seconds and a meal removes 35, so one continuously fed actor needs approximately 1.371 food/minute; four need **5.486/minute**. The proposed source produces one unit per 7.5 seconds: **8/minute**, before losses from sitting at its growth ceiling. A twelve-minute trial would require roughly 66 meals in steady state, adjusted by starting/final hunger.

Use a renewable patch at the settlement's buildable clearing, with a growth ceiling of 12. This isolates production and allocation from perpetual exposure on a required travel route; transport already has separate evidence. Keep the initial shelter unfinished and cold unchanged. The supplied and disabled-production controls start with the same small stocks and people. Record initial food, actual production, consumption, final available food and food stranded on dead actors. Survival must continue after consumption exceeds all starting food, not just carried inventory.

Measure useful activity and safe intervals alongside survival. Distinguish purposeful waiting from starvation or a failed policy. Do not call script availability, cooperation in speech, or replacement spawning a completed settlement. Require a fresh repeat or a resource challenge before accepting Stage 1.

Knowledge transfer/archives are Stage 2; reproduction/fabrication and dependent newcomers are Stage 3. Plan those against a measured surplus and explicit knowledge continuity after Stage 1 passes. Nothing here implements or completes them.
