# Stage 1 evidence: a viable four-person settlement

This maps retained experiments to [Stage 1 of the world roadmap](WORLD_ROADMAP.md#1-a-viable-settlement), following the expanded [iteration plan](SOCIETY_ITERATION_PLAN.md). It retains the M1 experiments and does not mark later world stages complete.

## What the retained trials establish

| Roadmap concern | Evidence | Current limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shared authoritative entities and capabilities | Mixed built-in/external controllers in the six-arena matrix and four-character trials; scope/transaction tests | Representative biological needs only; no distinct charging body yet |
| Executable intent and recovery | [Batch 006](SOCIETY_BATCH_006.md) resumed a delivery after urgent rest; deterministic reload/preemption tests | Generated plans can still oscillate, hoard, or fail parsing |
| Material interaction | [Batches 006–007](SOCIETY_BATCH_007.md): net food transport, shared construction, later collection and meals | Gross gather/deposit counts include reuse; no specialization is guaranteed |
| Communication and private learning | Speech-grounded accepted beliefs/trust changes in batches 006–007; bounded private evidence and isolation tests | Delayed learning can be rejected after intervening harm; no physical archive or durable social knowledge store |
| Consequences and permanent death | Batch 007 shortage consumed all ten starting units, then all four died; cold still damages exposed actors | Finite reserve trials do not prove a renewable economy |
| Repeatability and inspection | Pinned implementations, isolated databases, common start gates, immutable final captures, live/archive dashboard | Model decisions remain stochastic; same seed is not an exact model replay |
| Group survival | Renewable m1-17: 4/4 at health 100 in batch 009; seeded m1-18: 4/4 at health 100 in batch 010 | Bounded twelve-minute trials, not indefinite viability across arbitrary worlds |

Batch 008's shorter five-second post-completion cadence produced 2/4 survivors versus 3/4 at fifteen seconds. It made more calls and had more output errors. This pair does not justify adopting faster scheduling as an improvement; retain the existing fifteen-second cadence for the next mechanism test.

## Mechanics, balance and seed ownership

| Existing field or artifact | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Rhai law/skill definitions, effect validation and controller authorization | Reusable rules and execution |
| Needs rates, action nutrition/costs, growth rule and permitted parameter bounds | Reusable balance definitions |
| Scenario geography, sites, initial stocks/shelter, weather parameters, initial people, motives, beliefs and relationships | Seed content/configuration |
| Scenario food-source location, interval, yield and growth ceiling | Configured physical assets; no faction-name dependency |
| Implementation bundle plus resolved scenario/controller files and hashes | Current versioned seed composition boundary |
| Later inventories, observations, beliefs, decisions, transfers, damage and production | Evolving authoritative state |

The existing scenario format plus frozen definition artifacts is the incremental starting point. A standalone general ruleset registry, full procedural generator and second complete setting remain deferred. New food-source references are validated against existing sites. A deterministic label-change check confirms that changing settlement/inhabitant/cultural labels leaves production and harvest costs equivalent. This is a mechanics test, not a claim that different cultures make identical LLM decisions.

No new attribute framework is needed for this test: health, hunger, energy, carried food and the existing traits already have relevant effects. Preserve caution, introspection and empathy. Body-specific charging waits for a representative non-eating inhabitant and a concrete resource requirement.

## Renewable necessity budget and retained result

The added mechanism is **accounted food production**. A seed-configured source is driven by elapsed simulation time and a versioned Rhai rule. Production is an explicit world event; it does not put food into anyone's inventory or choose who harvests. Full sites discard unused growth opportunities, and deposits remain conserved transfers. Growth and source configuration become character knowledge only through local perception.

Current upkeep: hunger rises 2 per 2.5 seconds and a meal removes 35, so one continuously fed actor needs approximately 1.371 food/minute; four need **5.486/minute**. The tested source produces one unit per 7.5 seconds: **8/minute**, before losses from sitting at its growth ceiling. A twelve-minute trial would require roughly 66 meals in steady state, adjusted by starting/final hunger.

The trial uses a renewable patch at the settlement's buildable clearing, with a growth ceiling of 12. This isolates production and allocation from perpetual exposure on a required travel route; transport already has separate evidence. Keep the initial shelter unfinished and cold unchanged. The supplied and disabled-production controls start with the same small stocks and people. Record initial food, actual production, consumption, final available food and food stranded on dead actors. Survival must continue after consumption exceeds all starting food, not just carried inventory.

Measure useful activity and safe intervals alongside survival. Distinguish purposeful waiting from starvation or a failed policy. Do not call script availability, cooperation in speech, or replacement spawning a completed settlement. Require a fresh repeat or a resource challenge before accepting Stage 1.

Knowledge transfer/archives are Stage 2; reproduction/fabrication and dependent newcomers are Stage 3. Plan those against a measured surplus and explicit knowledge continuity after Stage 1 passes. Nothing here implements or completes them.

Batch 009 completed with all four renewable inhabitants at health 100 after 720.046 seconds, 87 meals consumed, and 85 units actually produced: `22 initial + 85 produced = 20 final + 87 eaten`. Consumption exceeded every initial food unit at 233.482 seconds. The matched disabled-production control consumed its 22 units and all four died by 410.138 seconds. This establishes the resource-budget prerequisite; repeated deposits remain recycling and do not establish specialization. The [full report](SOCIETY_BATCH_009.md) records behavior and limitations.

Batch 010 repeated the renewable world with and without visible starting habits under m1-18. Both share the same controller cadence and mechanics. The normal seed now supplies builder, reserve keeper, shared provider and cautious observer policies, installed through ordinary policy validation and freely revisable. Authored startup behavior is not emergent invention. The completed repeat and startup challenge are assessed below.

## Acceptance decision: bounded Stage 1 slice

**Accepted on 2026-09-05 for the four-person renewable settlement.** Batch 009 established group survival beyond all starting supplies and a matched finite-food collapse. [Batch 010](SOCIETY_BATCH_010.md) repeated twelve-minute renewable viability and tested the newly authorized starting habits against an empty-start control. All four seeded inhabitants finished at health 100; all four empty-start inhabitants survived with health 98/2/20/100. Seeded food reconciles as `22 initial + 68 produced = 25 final + 65 eaten`; empty-start food reconciles as `22 + 90 = 44 + 68`. The seeded group remained healthy for 450.132 seconds after consumption exceeded all initial food. Both completed captures report zero engine errors and zero scope violations. The difference supports the usefulness of this seed in this pair, not a universal statistical estimate of starter benefit.

The acceptance combines retained evidence rather than requiring every social mechanism to recur in every run:

| Gate | Executed evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Renewable survival and consequences | 009 consumed 87 meals from 22 initial food plus 85 produced, with four healthy survivors; disabled production exhausted its 22 food and all four died. 010 provides a fresh renewable repeat and changed startup condition. |
| Shared work and conserved material effects | 006 resumed food delivery after urgent recovery; 007 showed net camp provision and later collection. 009/010 completed communal shelter that protected occupants from cold. Recycled deposits are excluded from useful delivery claims. |
| Intent, learning and available time | 010 includes explicit choices to retain safe observing/waiting, an accepted reserve change followed by actual gathering to the new reserve, a chosen longer wait that yielded to eating and reserve refill before resuming, and a chosen spoken acknowledgment of another person's coordination. The report records sources and cautions; safe-time samples are opportunity measures, not proof of leisure. |
| Immediate, revisable starting behavior | All four seeded people acted at the first 52 ms update before model results. Live decisions retained, replaced and later patched starters. Seven deterministic tests verify actual seeds, equal human/AI costs, private reload provenance, validation/scope and durable revision. |
| Inspection and execution boundaries | Frozen implementation/source/configs, independent databases and clocks with common start gates, retained model journals, final captures, conservation summaries and the comparison dashboard. The m1-18 verification run passed 120 tests. |

This accepts a first functioning settlement, not the full world vision. Efficiency, fair allocation and specialized roles are not guaranteed. Some generated patches retain an unwanted outer guard, some action loops waste work, and empty-start inference failures can cause lasting injury. Those failures remain visible evidence. The source is deliberately near shared shelter and has production headroom; remote infrastructure, tighter budgets, injuries and larger populations need their own bounded challenges. No statistical reliability rate or indefinite-survival claim follows from these few runs.

Normal settlement seeds now include visible revisable habits; empty-start and finite-production controls remain available. Preserve all frozen trials, including unsuccessful policies and control deaths. Future batches may compare several justified candidates concurrently and reconcile their findings before selecting a change.

Stage 2 is the next development gate: explicit teaching and one physical archive, with personal understanding, provenance, consultation and real loss when all accessible copies disappear. Archives, reproduction/fabrication, dependent newcomers, factions, research and universal editing are not implemented by this acceptance.
