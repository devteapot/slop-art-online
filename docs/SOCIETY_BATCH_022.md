# Batch 022: no physical-law edits in four 24-minute worlds

All four fresh worlds completed on frozen `reality-m7-1`, but no person attempted a physical-law draft, experiment, inspection or installation. The final law registries are empty. An independent review of every retained raw proposal, including malformed replies, found no hidden law attempt. This batch does not demonstrate autonomous physical-law invention, territorial or universal editing, changed-law border effects, or persistence after an author's death.

Ten built-in forecasts completed. In author-absence, Cato also completed one numeric identity prototype and one personal practice. Thirteen people survived. Dara starved in local-borders and cooling under ordinary laws; the detailed traces distinguish contested food, accepted gathering policies, delivered cooperation messages and failed attempts to change behavior. All final law, research, material, physical-copy, food, engine, scope and base audits pass. An audit pass is not Stage 7 behavioral acceptance.

## Fixed inputs and actual opportunity

The [campaign](../configs/experiments/campaign/022-physical-laws.json) fixed four concurrent 24-minute runs, Luna medium for all responsibilities, serial behavior/communication/learning, fifteen seconds after each completed call, and no call cap. The duration and author-absence disturbance at eighteen minutes were selected before launch. The frozen implementation manifest SHA-256 is `a89660bf0907234f3bd64687ffb2367f6cba5d471b1806bfacba9c6b0d421350`, covering 502 artifacts.

Four people inhabit adjacent west/east territories. Ari has the initial west editing grant and Cato the east grant; Bryn and Dara have none. Ari and Cato need electricity, while Bryn and Dara eat food. Both camps have shelter, a food source, a terminal and a cabinet. Local-borders and universal-repeat differ only in display name. Cooling changes west terminal water from 36 to 1; author-absence adds 100 damage against Ari at 1,080 seconds. [The preflight](../output/batches/022-physical-laws/MONITOR_PREFLIGHT.json) retains the exact scenario differences. No law source, cases, proofs, required research sequence or imported discovery was supplied, and no live participant action or controller change was injected.

[Delivered-control checks](../output/batches/022-physical-laws/law-delivered-controls.json) verify all sixteen initial actual requests and their grants, source syntax, scope/revision/binding and proof requirements. Every retained request also matches its logged participant context. The [causal review](../output/batches/022-physical-laws/law-causal-findings.md) retains explicit alternatives: ordinary survival, archive work, supply checks, bounded rest comparisons, cooling transport and intentional no-operation decisions. In author-absence, Ari explicitly chose to retain current rules and continue observation. Thus absent law work is an observed choice pattern, not evidence that a submitted law was rejected or that the interface was missing.

## Food access, attempted corrections and deaths

The two starvation outcomes occurred before any installed law and outside the authored absence treatment. Aggregate surplus did not make food physically available to Dara.

In [local-borders](../output/batches/022-physical-laws/local-borders/DARA_RESOURCE_REVIEW.json), Cato's electric starter contains no gathering. Dara later restricts gathering to two perceived food, then restores one-unit attempts; her last successful acquisition is 240.073 seconds and last meal 241.274.

Dara's urgent warning reaches Cato at 234.505 seconds. His delivered reply at 243.737 promises limited gathering and alternation, but changes no policy or inventory. Cato acquires every east replenishment from 255 through 345 seconds. His relevant later Behavior replies are malformed; the final pre-death-issued one completes after the death. Dara's next Behavior request also has no accepted response before her death. Starvation begins at 320.200 seconds and death 17172 occurs at 350.083. Exact death-time stocks are Cato 26, Dara 0, east site 0 and west site 12. East accounting is `6 initial + 23 produced = 26 Cato gathers + 3 Dara gathers`; Dara's `2 initial + 3 gathered = 5 meals`. No food aid or Dara movement is attempted before death.

In [cooling](../output/batches/022-physical-laws/cooling/COOLING_DARA_RESOURCE_REVIEW.json), Cato's accepted replacement at 127.273 seconds adds an ungated gather/wait cycle. Dara last gathers at 226.482 seconds, holds a reserve for longer and eats her final meal at 400.247. Cato takes all twenty subsequent east replenishments through death. Importantly, he repeatedly proposes removing this loop, including two Behavior responses after Dara's warning; malformed outputs prevent the changes from taking effect. Dara also proposes repairs, including a response returned during active starvation and before death, but that response is malformed too. Continued gathering is not evidence that Cato refused to change or intended harm.

Cooling starvation starts at 495.061 seconds and death 28323 occurs at 525.103. Cato acquires a newly renewed unit before the final starvation hit in that update. Exact death-time stocks are Cato 34, Dara 0, east site 0 and west site 12. East accounting is `6 + 35 = 34 Cato gathers + 7 Dara gathers`; Dara's `2 + 7 = 9 meals`. No aid or Dara relocation executes. These are observed resource and proposal-application failures, not evidence of malice or a proved counterfactual outcome.

Finally, author-absence Ari dies from the scheduled 100-damage disturbance at 1080.338 seconds, damage 37777 and death 37782. No law existed before or after his death. The author-persistence condition was therefore never activated, despite the actual death and the subsequent six-minute observation window.
