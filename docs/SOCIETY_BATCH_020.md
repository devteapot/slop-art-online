# Batch 020: interrupted research trial and lost interpretation evidence

Batch 020 did not complete its four planned twelve-minute worlds. The first launch failed before readiness and made no model calls. The corrected retry reached actual participant work, including four paid retrieved built-in forecasts, but a growing publication and subscription backlog impaired three worlds. The operator interrupted all four pilots after approximately 482 active wall seconds. Invention retained its interrupted final snapshot; the other three paused authority snapshots were recovered after a server restart. No retained trace establishes a personally interpreted research bootstrap, authored numeric technique, practice, ordinary technique run, code transfer or total discovery loss.

A separate concrete finding explains part of the research nonactivation: invention's two forecast recipients continued holding their reports, but neither report receipt ever appeared in a later model observation page. Their first learning turns arrived after the receipt had also left the bounded experience trace. Current authority could not interpret an old personally held report through its retained receipt metadata. The visible repeated retrieval failures instead dominated their reflections. This is a recoverability defect in evidence access; it is not proof that invention would otherwise have occurred.

Loss-risk schedules 100 damage to Ari at 540,000 ms and destruction of the camp cabinet at 570,000 ms. These authored disturbances do not select an inventor or erase terminal copies.

The original frozen `research-m6-1` launch under [020-research](../output/batches/020-research) failed because the generated site shelter was 16 while actual `World::new` validation allows at most 12. The first invention host logged `invalid site`; its pilot has zero calls, no run and no readiness. The remaining worlds did not start. Those files remain unchanged. The generator was corrected to shelter 12, and all four seeds passed an actual-core preflight that loads `World::new`, enables participants and executes the first 50 ms without database or model calls. Fresh `research-m6-2` froze 461 artifacts before the retry.

All four exact final or recovered inputs now pass research, infrastructure, physical-copy, engine and scope audits.
