# Stage 4 evidence: connected settlements

The evidence supports the bounded connected-settlement slice described below. It applies the existing `m3-2-perceived-care.1` authority to three connected settlements rather than adding a separate trade or migration simulator. [Campaign 015](../configs/experiments/campaign/015-multisociety.json) ran four eight-minute sessions frozen as `multisociety-m4-1`; [batch 015](SOCIETY_BATCH_015.md) records the final outcomes and snapshot hashes.

## Experiment and boundaries

Six people initially live in pairs at western cell 82, eastern cell 93 and southern cell 152. All three camps share one arena; arena isolation boundaries must not divide societies intended to interact. Trips span nine to eleven ordinary movement steps. Camps have shared shelter and no local hazard; travel retains normal movement energy and weather exposure.

Western, eastern and southern sources nominally produce six, two and three food per minute. Total eleven exceeds the six-person reference requirement of approximately 8.23, but the eastern camp has a local deficit. The pressure variant removes only the eastern source, leaving nine food/minute globally. Initial carried food and pantries are finite buffers. Actual production is measured because stock ceilings can suppress renewal while another camp lacks food.

Only southern actor 5 starts with a private report describing the western resource. Each person has a distinct authored copy of a geometry-only survey identifying camp coordinates; it discloses no remote resources or inhabitants. The southern camp has an initially empty archive. A disputed variant matches the pressure physics and adds eastern actor 4's separate mistaken report that the western site does not regrow food. Records preserve distinct provenance; neither receipt nor agreement occurs automatically.

The scale variant has twelve people, with source amounts, capacities and pantry stocks doubled. Per-person needs, initial inventory and travel costs stay fixed. Its positive report author and archive count stay unchanged, making communication load part of the challenge. This is not a controlled estimate of population size alone.

All participants use fresh Luna medium calls on the existing fifteen-second post-completion schedule without a call cap. Ordinary revisable home habits contain no authored inter-camp travel, speech, teaching or exchange. Lifecycle creation is disabled: movement must preserve existing identities. Motives invite different choices but no diplomatic outcome is required.

## Evidence to inspect

Look for actual food carried between camps and completed transfers or deposits; distinguish gifts or informal reciprocity from enforceable trade contracts. Trace first report acquisition, personal interpretation, chosen travel and material outcomes. A visit, an independently discovered resource or mere temporal ordering is not automatically an information-caused migration.

Residence reporting integrates time at camps from movement events, with subsequent gathering, eating and resting. It retains policy and stated-intention references for interpretation rather than declaring migration from one final coordinate. It also separates exact cited/guarded report use from later actions without explicit links. Deposited food is fungible, so preceding harvests show possible origins, not a proved unit-by-unit supply chain.

The generator validates the four seed differences and their material budgets. All four scenarios load and advance through the authoritative core without script errors. Eight reporting fixtures verify fixed identities, distinct report lineage and repeated copies, material transfers, residence intervals including integer-valued damage effects, conservative causal attribution and the absence of automatic knowledge or migration claims from meetings. Final live audits pass for all four variants. The damage fixture arose from a reporter defect found during the trial; correcting the postprocessor did not change frozen authority behavior or evidence.

## Bounded Stage 4 decision

The connected-settlement mechanism is supported by the following evidence. A cooperative diplomatic outcome is not required by the roadmap, and another sample is not needed merely to replace these failures with a favorable story.

| Evidence | Observed result |
| --- | --- |
| Several settlements share physical rules and a traversable world | Three camps in one arena; fixed populations of six or twelve; no creation events or identity replacement |
| Geography and unequal supplies have material consequences | Four starvation deaths across the four samples; travelers collect and consume food at another camp; weather damages travel; real production reflects local stock ceilings |
| Existing people can leave their initial settlements | Four actor/variant journeys with 181–447 seconds of measured nonhome-camp residence, including three endpoints away from initial homes |
| Information requires experience and interpretation | Disputed speech receipts 21784/22282 lead to the recipients' own attributed assertions 24229/24003; promises remain distinct from deliveries |
| Knowledge and material effects remain accounted | Clean food and exact-copy audits; one local teaching copy plus nine repeats, one archive copy plus sixteen repeats, no automatic knowledge from proximity |
| Outcomes vary without scripted cooperation | Baseline/scale isolation, pressure/disputed travel, deprivation despite remote stocks, malformed proposals and unsuccessful transport policies remain recorded |

This supports a bounded Stage 4 capability slice: contact, movement, changed resource access and personally interpreted inter-origin communication. It does not establish a successful trade route, delivered aid, a cross-origin exact useful-record transfer, explicit report-driven material use, permanent migration, shared institutions or a stable multi-settlement society. The observed speech interpretations did not by themselves feed anyone. Travel loops sometimes kept contradicting a character's stated delivery intention, and scarce camps lost people despite food elsewhere.

The next faction-world integration can reuse this mechanism while retaining those gaps as measurements: actual carried deliveries and their beneficiaries, useful information after receipt, durable residence and cooperation, and survival under changing demand. Adding faction names, offices or infrastructure must not turn these unobserved outcomes into assumed facts.
